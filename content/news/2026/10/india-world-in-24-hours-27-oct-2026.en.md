## Cyclone Arnab

At 08:30 IST on 27 October, Cyclone Arnab, a very severe cyclonic storm, was about 220 km from Visakhapatnam. IMD forecasts landfall near Yanam by the forenoon of 28 October. The wind line in that forecast is 125-135 km/h, with gusts to 150. Landfall has not happened. The caption says 61 or more trains have been cancelled. Reports put about 89,000 people being moved to relief camps in Kakinada. That camp figure is the reports' figure. The fuller note, from the earlier bulletins, is [Arnab now very severe; Yanam by 28 Oct](/news/2026/10/imd-depression-bay-of-bengal-very-severe-cyclone-andhra-oct-28). This recap's position is the 08:30 IST line. The slide is an IMD INSAT-3DS image from 02:30 IST on 27 October, from Bulletin No. 15, courtesy IMD. It is not the 08:30 IST position.

## The local toll pass

The road transport ministry's Concessional Local Pass starts on 1 November, under G.S.R. 917(E) of 26 October. It is for a resident within 20 km of a national-highway toll plaza, with a non-commercial vehicle and a valid FASTag. The fee is Rs 1,500 a year when the plaza is outside town limits, and Rs 3,000 a year when the plaza is inside town limits. The pass works at that plaza only. It does not apply on closed-system expressways. The caption says the details of how to apply are still to come. The Economic Times and NDTV are the reports named with the gazette listing. The slide is a 2022 file of a National Highway 6 toll plaza, Arindambasu2 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this pass.

## Jammu and Kashmir

Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha swore in four National Conference MLAs: Ali Mohammad Sagar, Shameema Firdous, Mir Saifullah and Altaf Ahmad Wani, also called Kalloo. They join Chief Minister Omar Abdullah's council. The caption says the council is now at its maximum of 9. Hindustan Times and The Indian Express say Satish Sharma was dropped. That removal is those two papers' line. Congress declines berths until statehood. The Hindu and PTI are also named with this expansion. The slide is a 2025 file of Omar Abdullah, Prime Minister's Office / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not this swearing-in.

## Karnataka's voter roll

Karnataka's final electoral roll, after the Special Intensive Revision, lists 4,55,00,011 voters. That is about 99.32 lakh fewer than the 5,54,32,314 names before. After the draft, 8.64 lakh names were added. The caption's shorter line calls the new total 4.55 crore. The caption says a person should check their name at voters.eci.gov.in. The Hindu, Hindustan Times and Deccan Herald are the reports named for this item. The slide is a file of the Vidhana Soudha in Bengaluru, Moheen Reeyad / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this roll.

## Wellington

India beat New Zealand by 6 wickets in the third T20I at Wellington. New Zealand made 198/3. India made 199/4 in 13.2 overs. Abhishek Sharma scored 113 off 38 balls. The hundred came off 35 balls. India lead the five-match series 3-0. The Times of India, The Indian Express and NDTV are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2024 file of Wellington Regional Stadium, Tom Ackroyd / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this match.

## Anthropic

Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, has publicly filed an S-1 with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. The filing is for a listing on Nasdaq Global Select, with the ticker ANT. The share price and the share count are not set. This note states no valuation. Anthropic's own note, the SEC filing, the Associated Press and CNBC are the sources named for this item. The slide is a 2021 file of the Nasdaq MarketSite in New York, ajay_suresh / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0, not this filing.

## Gabon

On 25 October the Prospector 5 jack-up rig sank off Mayumba, in Gabon. An Indian national died. The package does not name the person, and this note does not add a name. The Gabon government says four people are still missing: three Gabonese and one Croatian. The caption says no oil spill was seen. The government's statement, The Hindu and Offshore are the sources named for this item. The slide is a May 2008 file of a jack-up rig at Abu Dhabi port, Nial Farrell / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0. It is a similar rig, not Prospector 5.

## Tehran

On 26 October, Saudi Defence Minister Khalid bin Salman, Pakistan Army chief Asim Munir and Turkey's intelligence chief Ibrahim Kalin met Iran's President Pezeshkian in Tehran. The package describes them as Mecca pact officials. Geo News says they agreed to continue dialogue. This note reports no deal and no ceasefire. Al Jazeera and Business Standard are the other reports named for this item. The slide is a 2025 file of Masoud Pezeshkian, khamenei.ir / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0, not this meeting. The Commons file is labelled cropped and is shown whole.
