## Odisha

A landslide struck Gidipabali village, in the Bhanjanagar area of Ganjam district, after heavy rain from Cyclone Arnab. Odisha's Special Relief Commissioner said on Saturday 31 October that 13 people were missing under the debris. Prameya later reported that 12 were still missing. The count on this page is at least 12 to 13 missing, per the Special Relief Commissioner. The missing people are not described as dead. Rescuers pulled out one elderly woman, Tarabati Pradhan, alive. ODRAF, the NDRF and Fire Services are searching.

The four confirmed deaths are statewide rain deaths, and they are a separate line from those missing people. The Special Relief Commissioner confirmed four: three members of one family, including a nine-month-old baby, in a landslide at Dangesi village in Kandhamal's Raikia block, and one person in Gajapati. Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi announced Rs 4 lakh ex gratia to the next of kin of the Kandhamal deaths. The Indian Express says about 300 villages in Ganjam were still marooned, and that the Chief Minister did an aerial survey on 30 October. The earlier cyclone note is [Arnab crosses near Narsapur, weakens](/news/2026/10/imd-depression-bay-of-bengal-very-severe-cyclone-andhra-oct-28). This item is the landslide after that rain. It does not replace that note.

## Vande Bharat Sleeper

The Varanasi-New Delhi Vande Bharat Sleeper was flagged off from Varanasi on 31 October by Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath and Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw. Prime Minister Narendra Modi welcomed the train on X. A DD News headline says the Prime Minister flagged it off. The Press Information Bureau and The Economic Times say the flag-off in Varanasi was by Yogi Adityanath and Ashwini Vaishnaw, and that the Prime Minister posted on X. This page uses that second line. He did not flag it off.

It is the country's second Vande Bharat Sleeper, after Howrah-Guwahati in January 2026. On the Press Information Bureau timings, the regular train leaves Varanasi at 8:50 pm and reaches New Delhi at 6:10 am. The return leaves New Delhi at 9:40 pm and reaches Varanasi at 7:10 am. It stops at Prayagraj, Kanpur Central, Aligarh and Ghaziabad. It runs six days a week: no departure from Varanasi on Friday, and no departure from New Delhi on Saturday. Varanasi to Delhi is about 55 minutes faster than the Shiv Ganga Express. Fares are not in this package, so this page states none.

## The voter list

On 30 October the Supreme Court declined to formally extend the deadline for re-verification under the Special Intensive Revision in Kerala. The bench is Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana. The hearing covered pleas on the revision in Uttar Pradesh, Kerala and Chhattisgarh. The court said inclusion and correction of voters is a continuing process. Petitioners may submit details of people who could not file enumeration forms in time. The Election Commission will examine those representations and report back to the court. The Times of India reports the Election Commission counsel as saying people can apply for inclusion after the revision, until nominations for an election. CPI(M) counsel said over 24 lakh names were excluded in the Kerala revision. That figure is the counsel's claim. It is not stated here as a court finding. The court did not order names restored, and it did not extend a deadline.

## A symbolic impeachment

The Congress-led INDIA bloc held a symbolic public impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar at Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge's residence, 10 Rajaji Marg, New Delhi, on Saturday 31 October. It had been postponed from 30 October because of rain. Former Lok Sabha Speaker Meira Kumar presided. Kharge moved a mock motion. Rahul Gandhi and Sonia Gandhi attended. The motion lists six charges. Those charges are the bloc's allegations. They include the alleged deletion of 13.2 crore voters and the alleged rewriting of Form 6. The event has no legal effect. The Chief Election Commissioner was not impeached. Real removal notices were rejected by the Lok Sabha Speaker and the Rajya Sabha Chairman in April. The BJP mocked the event. The line in the package is that Rahul Gandhi was sitting in a Parliament of his own choice. On Friday 30 October, Home Minister Amit Shah offered a debate in a special session of Parliament. The bloc plans a rally in Delhi on 1 November. The route, and any permission, are not in this package.

## The air chief

Air Chief Marshal Ashutosh Dixit took charge on 31 October as the 29th Chief of the Air Staff. He had been Vice Chief. He succeeds Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh, A.P. Singh, who superannuated the same day after over four decades. Reports differ between 42 and 43 years, so this page says over four decades, and it does not give flying hours. Before the handover, Singh laid a wreath at the National War Memorial and received a ceremonial guard of honour at the Race Course Air Force Station. Dixit was commissioned into the fighter stream on 6 December 1986. He is an experimental test pilot, and he has flown the Mirage-2000, MiG variants and the Jaguar. The government notified the appointment on 6 October. That notification is the Press Information Bureau's. The Hindu says Air Marshal Inderpal Singh Walia has been named the next Vice Chief.

## Hormuz

Tanker MT Louise 1 was hit by limpet mines about 25 nautical miles north-east of Khasab, in Oman, near the Strait of Hormuz. Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal says all 27 Indian crew are safe. Who carried out the attack is not known. The attackers are not identified. This page does not name a group, a state, or a person. News on Air, Hindustan Times, The New Indian Express and gCaptain are the reports named for this item.

## Atlanta

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar met US Secretary of State Marco Rubio on the sidelines of the G20 Foreign Ministers' Meeting in Atlanta. Jaishankar said they took stock of India-US bilateral cooperation, and he thanked Rubio for his hospitality. The meeting follows Rubio's visit to India on 20-22 October. An earlier note recorded what he said in Mumbai on 20 October. [Rubio: trade deal very close, unsigned](/news/2026/10/rubio-jaishankar-mumbai-india-us-trade-deal-very-very-close-not-signed). This roundup does not report a new trade-deal step. None was announced from the Atlanta meeting.

## Suncatcher

Google says its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite, launched on 1 October, has produced the first AI response from a data-centre-class TPU chip in orbit. The milestone was announced on 30 October. Engineers uplinked a prompt asking the onboard Gemma 4 open model for a haiku about space. The reply came down during a ground-station pass over Svalbard, Norway. This page does not quote the reply. Google will next test how the chips cope with heat and radiation, ahead of a planned pair of linked satellites in 2027, as it studies solar-powered AI computing in space. This is one prototype test. It is not a working space data centre.
