As of 9:00 am IST on Friday 2 October 2026, entry and exit were closed at 11 Delhi Metro stations until further notice, and Delhi Police had refused permission for an 11 am protest at Jantar Mantar. Neutral Politics checked The Economic Times, The Times of India, The Indian Express and ABP Live, which quote a Delhi Metro Rail Corporation post on X, and an Election Commission statement as Mint reported it on 26 September. The accounts were checked at about 8:55 am IST on 2 October. This note was posted at 10:14 am IST the same morning. The reports checked still had the closures in place.

The DMRC post itself was not opened directly. The station list below is as those outlets quote it. The Times of India embeds a different status id from the one attached to the relayed post.

The 11 stations are Janpath, Patel Chowk, Central Secretariat, Rajiv Chowk, Seva Teerth, Mandi House, Lok Kalyan Marg, INA, Chawri Bazar, Ramakrishna Ashram Marg and New Delhi. The Times of India story is timed at 8:22 am IST. ABP Live, carrying PTI, is timed at 8:35 am IST. The Indian Express metro story is timed at 8:48 am IST.

Interchange is a separate point, and the reports do not all name the same stations. The Economic Times, The Times of India and ABP Live say interchange continues at Rajiv Chowk, Mandi House, New Delhi and Central Secretariat. The Indian Express also lists INA. Whether the INA interchange is open is not settled in the reports checked. The corporation's own post, as @OfficialDMRC, is the place to confirm that before travel.

Delhi Police refused permission for the 11 am protest at Jantar Mantar. The call, as these reports describe it, came from AISA, Rajya Sabha MP Manoj Jha and advocate Prashant Bhushan. The Indian Express police story is timed at 1:31 am IST on 2 October. The reports also describe extra forces and barricades. Police sources expect 3,000 to 4,000 people. That figure is an estimate of what police sources expect. It is not a count of who turned up.

ABP Live reports Delhi Traffic Police diversions near Connaught Place, Patel Chowk, the GPO, Tolstoy Marg, Jantar Mantar Road and Parliament Street. The caption of the Instagram post also names Organiser for those diversions. No Organiser link is in the source list used here.

What is a claim, and not a verified finding, is the demand itself. The organisers seek the resignation or impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged electoral-roll and election irregularities. The Times of India and The Indian Express report that demand. The irregularities are alleged. They are not findings in these reports. Separately, the Election Commission, in a 26 September statement as Mint reported it at 8:17 pm IST, said SIR orders had the full Commission's unanimous approval and carry legal sanction. That is the commission's statement. It does not turn the organisers' allegations into facts, and it does not decide the demand.

## What's next

What is not known yet is when the 11 stations reopen, what the turnout at Jantar Mantar was, whether the INA interchange is open, and whether the permission decision changes after 9:00 am IST. The closures and the refusal of permission are the verified part of the morning's reports. The resignation and impeachment demand stays alleged.
