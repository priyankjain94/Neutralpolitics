This note is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is verified for the police order. The revised SOP and Tuesday's hearing are developing. It was re-checked at 3:05 pm IST on 12 October 2026 and posted at 3:03 pm IST.

Delhi Police has de-notified Jantar Mantar as the capital's designated protest venue. The notice is from Police Commissioner Anurag Kumar. The Indian Express, updated at 2:43 pm IST on 12 October, quotes that notice via PTI. The Times of India, timed 1:53 pm IST, reports the same de-notification and dates the order 11 October. This note takes the date from The Times of India. The official document was not opened in this check. What is used here is the notice as those two reports carry it.

The order says it acts on the recommendations of a committee set up by the Union Home Ministry. The check describes that committee as one on alternative venues. That is the basis the notice gives. This note stays with that stated basis.

Three sites are now the designated places for protests and demonstrations. They are Ramlila Ground, Burari Ground, and CBD Ground in East Delhi. The Indian Express and The Times of India name all three. ABP Live also reports the three new venues. The screen's list is those three grounds, with CBD Ground marked as East Delhi.

The order partially modifies Standing Order L&O/48/22 of 28 April 2022. That number and that date are in the notice as The Indian Express carries it via PTI, and in the stamp of this check. A detailed revised SOP for the three new venues is to be issued separately. At 3:05 pm IST on 12 October that SOP was not out. That pending SOP is the developing half of this note, together with the court date below.

The Delhi High Court agreed to hear a plea against the closure of Jantar Mantar as a protest site. Hindustan Times is the report for that hearing. The date is Tuesday, 13 October. The check found no ruling. This note records an agreement to hear.

The order comes amid recent anti-CEC protests in central Delhi. That line is context only. It is in the brief as the setting of the week.

What is verified, then, is the de-notification, the three named venues, the Home Ministry committee's recommendations as the stated basis, and the partial modification of Standing Order L&O/48/22 of 28 April 2022. What is developing is the revised SOP, still to be issued, and the High Court hearing on Tuesday, 13 October, with no ruling in hand.
