This note is a claim check, and the claim is unverified. The reel was posted at 9:09 am IST on 23 October 2026. The series is CLAIM CHECK. The stamp is CLAIM UNVERIFIED. This file is a draft. It stays unpublished because the post is a company claim, and because the material here does not include an independent check of that claim. Of the sources named on the reel, only TokenPost is a link to a specific article. The other three addresses are homepages.

The claim, as the caption sets it out, is this. Marathon Fusion, a startup in San Francisco, said on X on 21 October 2026 that in September it converted mercury into stable gold using fusion neutrons. The caption records that as what the company said. It is not recorded here as a result that has been shown.

The company says this is 10 times CERN's 2025 record. That comparison is the company's. The caption does not give CERN's figure, CERN's method, or a paper, and this note does not add them.

The caption lists what has not been disclosed. Yield is not disclosed. Purity is not disclosed. Cost is not disclosed. There is no independent verification. The caption's own status line follows from that list: CLAIM UNVERIFIED. The caption says this is the company's claim, not proof.

The claimed route, in the caption's words, is Hg-198 to Hg-197, then Au-197. Those are the symbols the caption gives for the claimed path. The caption does not give a measured yield or a purity for that path, and this note does not supply either.

The caption says such gold may stay radioactive, and that it may need roughly 14 to 18 years of storage. Both lines use "may". The caption does not give a measured level of radioactivity, a half-life, or a storage plan. They remain possibilities stated in the caption.

The caption also says that no commercial fusion plant exists in the world yet. That is the caption's statement. It does not name an experimental machine, and it does not give a date for a commercial plant.

The caption names four sources: Marathon Fusion's thread on X, The Deep Dive, TokenPost, and The Oregon Group. TokenPost is the one specific article URL in the source list, https://tokenpost.com/news/technology/38276. Marathon Fusion's thread is listed as the X homepage, https://x.com/, and the source name dates it 21 October 2026. The list does not include a link to one post. The Deep Dive is listed as https://thedeepdive.ca. The Oregon Group is listed as https://theoregongroup.com. Those three are homepages. They are listed because the caption names them.
