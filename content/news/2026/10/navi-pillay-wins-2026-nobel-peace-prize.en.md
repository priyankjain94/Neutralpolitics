Navi Pillay has won the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize. She is an Indian-origin South African jurist. The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced the prize in its press release on NobelPrize.org, dated 9 October 2026. The prize motivation, in that release, is "for her efforts to promote peace and international law." The same release says she was instrumental in ensuring that war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide are prosecuted. The committee's line is "Peace requires justice."

The facts page on NobelPrize.org names her Navanethem Pillay. It says she was born on 23 September 1941 in Durban, South Africa. It lists the prize share as 1/1. The press release describes her origin as Indian Tamil, and Durban as the place. CNBC, a secondary report, describes a family of Indian Tamil origin under apartheid in Durban. Al Jazeera describes her as a South African jurist. ABC News describes her as South Africa-born. Those are the identity lines in the sources used here. She is an Indian-origin South African jurist, from an Indian Tamil family, born in Durban.

The courts named by the committee are the ICTR, the Rwanda tribunal, the ICC and the High Court. ABC News reports those same three: the ICC, the High Court and the ICTR. The caption's shorter line is that she was a judge on the Rwanda tribunal and at the ICC. UN News describes her as a former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights. That is the UN News description. This note does not add years to that post.

Al Jazeera's report, timed at 2:36 pm IST on 9 October, is corroboration of the award, and it gives the number of candidates as 287. That figure is Al Jazeera's. ABC's corroboration adds that she is 85. That age is ABC's. The facts page's birth date remains 23 September 1941.
