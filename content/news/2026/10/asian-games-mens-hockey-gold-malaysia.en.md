On Saturday, 3 October 2026, India beat Malaysia 5-1 in the Asian Games men's hockey final. The match was at the Gifu Prefectural Green Stadium in Kakamigahara. That score is the official results page. Olympics.com, The Hindu with PTI, Indian Express and The Star report the same result. Malaysia took the silver. This note is NP NOW. The result, as those sources give it, is verified.

The official page lists the scorers. Harmanpreet Singh scored in the 11th minute and the 57th minute, both from penalty corners. Jugraj Singh scored in the 23rd minute from a penalty corner. Hardik Singh scored in the 42nd minute from a penalty stroke. Shilanand Lakra scored in the 54th minute, a field goal. For Malaysia, Shello Silverius scored in the 14th minute. Olympics.com, The Hindu with PTI, Indian Express and The Star list those same names. One mid-match report named Abhishek as the late scorer. This note uses Lakra. The official page and those four reports list Lakra.

Olympics.com, The Hindu with PTI and Indian Express report three things with the gold. India has a direct berth for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics. This is the first successful defence of the Asian Games men's hockey title. It is a fifth Asian Games men's hockey gold for India. Those three points are those outlets' reports. This note does not add a medal table of its own.

Pakistan won the bronze, beating South Korea 5-3. The Hindu with PTI and Dawn report that score. The same result is also attributed to The Tribune, Geo and APP. This note does not list who scored in that match. Those names are not in the material used here. Dawn's report is of a comeback bronze. The 5-3 is the score used.

The women's final is a separate result. Neutral Politics has already reported it on its own. On Friday, 2 October, India beat China 1-0. Olympics.com, Hockey India and The Hindu with PTI report that score. India Today's account is among the reports of the same 1-0. Olympics.com and Hockey India call it India's first Asian Games women's hockey gold since 1982. Hockey India describes a 44-year wait. Indian Express reports that this is the first Asian Games at which both Indian teams won the hockey gold. That double is Indian Express's report. This note does not retell the women's match.

On the same Saturday, Aman Sehrawat won the men's freestyle 57kg wrestling gold. He beat Han Chong Song of North Korea 11-7. The official wrestling winners list gold for India and silver for PRK. The Times of India, Hindustan Times, The Economic Times with PTI, and DD News report that 11-7 final. The Times of India says he fought back from 6-7 down, and that Han was the world champion. DD News calls Han the reigning world champion. Those descriptions are those outlets'. The line taken from the official list is the 11-7, with gold to India and silver to PRK. The Economic Times with PTI is timed at 3:24 pm IST on 3 October.

The Hindu with PTI is timed at 5:38 pm IST on 3 October. Indian Express is timed at 5:18 pm IST the same day. Neutral Politics checked the outlets listed here. This note was posted at 1:04 pm IST on 4 October. No later change to the official hockey scorers, or to the 11-7 wrestling score, is in that material. No further Sunday result is added.
