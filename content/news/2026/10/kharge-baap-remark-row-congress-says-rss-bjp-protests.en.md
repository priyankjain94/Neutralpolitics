This note is verified as a report of a political row, and the file is a draft. The series is NP NOW. The carousel was posted at 10:33 am IST on 26 October 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover uses file photographs: Mallikarjun Kharge taking charge as Union Minister for Railways in New Delhi on 19 June 2013, Ministry of Railways, and Amit Shah in New Delhi on 19 August 2019, Ministry of Home Affairs. Both are Wikimedia Commons files under GODL-India, and both Commons files are themselves labelled cropped. They are not photographs of this row.

On 25 October, in Patna, at an event for Shri Krishna Singh's birth anniversary, Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge answered an audience member. The member had said that Modi will not go. Kharge's words, quoted here as the caption gives them, were: "Modi ka baap bhi jayega". The caption's literal meaning is "Modi ke pita bhi jayenge". This note uses that gloss. It does not add a harsher reading.

Congress's account comes from ThePrint, with PTI, and from Hindustan Times. Congress media head Pawan Khera said Kharge was speaking about the RSS. Khera called the RSS the Prime Minister's "ideological father". Khera also alleges that a BJP worker had first insulted Kharge's late father. That insult is Khera's allegation. It is not a finding on this page, and it is not a finding that the person Kharge answered was from the BJP.

The man Kharge was answering has given a different account. Santosh Kumar, of Chhapra, told The Indian Express and NDTV on 25 October that he is a Congress worker, that his words were cut off, and that they were misunderstood. The caption says Khera's claim and Kumar's account are not the same. This draft records both. It does not decide between them.

The BJP's account, as the caption and the named reports give it, is this. Amit Shah wrote on X that Kharge had broken "all norms of decorum". The Indian Express is the report named for Shah's response. The caption says Bihar Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary demanded an apology from Kharge and from Rahul Gandhi. BJP president Nitin Nabin wrote on X that the remark shows the Congress's "ideological bankruptcy". That sentence is the caption's record of Nabin's post. The Hindu and The Times of India report that the BJP is protesting on 26 October at every state headquarters, and that the Bihar unit is marching from the Patna party headquarters. Those are the reports' lines about the protests. This note does not predict how many people will come.

No apology from Kharge appears in the verified reports, as of this posting. The package says a rebuild would be needed only if he apologises. This draft does not say that he has.

Read together, the lines that stay inside the package are these. The day of the remark is 25 October. The place is Patna, at Shri Krishna Singh's birth anniversary. The words are "Modi ka baap bhi jayega", with the literal gloss "Modi ke pita bhi jayenge". Khera's claim, in ThePrint with PTI and in Hindustan Times, is that Kharge meant the RSS, called an "ideological father", and that a BJP worker had first insulted Kharge's late father. Kumar's account, in The Indian Express and NDTV, is that he is a Congress worker from Chhapra and was misunderstood. Shah's X line is "all norms of decorum". Choudhary's demand for an apology, and Nabin's "ideological bankruptcy", are in the caption. The 26 October protests at state headquarters, and the Patna march, are in The Hindu and The Times of India. No Kharge apology is in the verified reports.
