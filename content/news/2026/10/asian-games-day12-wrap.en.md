India won all three compound archery team events at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya on 30 September 2026, and added trap mixed team gold the same day. Neutral Politics checked the official results, the official medal table, Sportstar and the Indian Express on 1 October 2026 (IST). This note was posted at 9:57 am IST that morning. It is a Day 12 wrap. It does not replace the separate reports of each final.

The official result pages time the three compound finals on 30 September. The women's team beat China 238-234, and that 238 equals the world record. The page lists the final at 10:50. The mixed team beat Korea 158-157. The match had finished level at 157-157 and went to a shoot-off. That final is listed at 12:45. The men's team beat China 238-237. That final is listed at 15:50. Those clock times are the ones printed on the official pages. This account does not convert them to IST, and it does not call the men's 238 a world record. The world-record line here is the women's score only.

Neeru Dhanda and Kynan Chenai won trap mixed team gold, beating Qatar in the final. The official page lists that final at 14:30 on 30 September. The account used here does not print Qatar's score. The gold is the result. The margin is not in these notes.

Sportstar and the Indian Express record three bronzes from the day. In boxing, Priya Ghanghas won bronze at 60kg and Kapil Pokhariya won bronze at 90kg. In wrestling, Sunil Kumar won bronze in Greco-Roman at 87kg. Those names, weights and that style are as those two reports give them. This wrap does not add other Indian medals from 30 September.

The official medal table, checked between 8:05 and 8:10 am IST on 1 October, listed India eighth with 10 gold, 21 silver and 29 bronze, 60 medals in all. That reading is the figure used here for the table at the end of Day 12. Bahrain was seventh with 11 gold, 5 silver and 5 bronze, 21 medals. India has more medals in total. Bahrain stands above India because the table ranks by gold first, and 11 gold ranks above 10. The table changes with every medal. A later reading can move these places, which is why the numbers here are tied to that morning check and not offered as a standing total.

The Indian Express page on the Day 13 medal tally was updated at 7:24 am IST on 1 October, before the official check used here. Sportstar's Day 13 live blog is dated 1 October. The 10, 21, 29 and 60 in this note are the official table's figures from 8:05–8:10 am IST. They are not taken from a later pass through those live pages.

The four golds in the wrap are the three compound team events and the trap mixed team. The three bronzes are the two in boxing and the one in wrestling. Nothing else from earlier days of the Games is folded into this count of the day.

## What's next

The official table is the place to read the next change. At the check on the morning of 1 October 2026 (IST), India was eighth with 10 gold, 21 silver and 29 bronze. Bahrain was seventh with 11 gold and 21 medals, because the table ranks by gold first. Those places are the morning's, and the table moves with every medal after that.
