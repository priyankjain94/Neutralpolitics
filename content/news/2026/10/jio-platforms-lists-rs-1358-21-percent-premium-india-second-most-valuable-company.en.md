This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 5:13 pm IST on 28 October 2026. The shares listed on 28 October. The issue price was Rs 1,119 a share. The listing price was Rs 1,358, on both the NSE and the BSE. The premium is Rs 239 a share, which is 21.36 percent. At that listing price, one lot of 12 shares shows a gain of Rs 2,868. The Hindu BusinessLine is the report named for the lot figure. The arithmetic in the package is 239 times 12.

At the listing, the market value was about Rs 12.5 lakh crore. Business Standard, citing BSE data, says Jio Platforms is now India's No. 2 listed company, after its parent, Reliance Industries. Bharti Airtel is third. NDTV Profit is also named for the rank. This page does not add a rupee value for Reliance Industries or for Bharti Airtel.

The issue size was Rs 30,213 crore. Overall, the offer was subscribed 22.19 times. Qualified institutional buyers were at 70.55 times. Retail was at 2.72 times. Business Standard and NDTV Profit are the reports named for those multiples.

The Rs 1,358 figure is the listing price. It is not a live share price. After listing, the price moves up and down through the day. This page does not give a price from later in the session. It does not say whether to buy or to sell.
