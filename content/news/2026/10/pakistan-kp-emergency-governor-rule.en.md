This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified. It was checked at 6:47 pm IST on 11 October 2026 and posted at 7:00 pm IST. It records an emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

President Zardari imposed both, on the advice of the cabinet, under Articles 232 and 234. Dawn, Al Jazeera, AP, The Hindu with PTI, Radio Pakistan, Dunya and Daily Times report that step. The Hindu, with PTI, describes a proclamation under which the provincial government ceases to exist. AP and Dunya report the same suspension. The screen says the chief minister and the cabinet are out. This note says the PTI-led government is suspended, and that the chief minister and the ministers cease to hold office. PTI is Imran Khan's party.

The province is to be run by the governor and an 11-member advisory council, with army support. The accounts name Governor Kundi. Radio Pakistan, citing Law Minister Tarar, reports the 11-member council. AP, citing Information Minister Tarar, reports the same council. Bloom Pakistan reports the council in Federal Order No. 1. Radio Pakistan, Dunya citing an Interior Ministry notification, and Nukta report that the army is assisting the governor. The screen's line is the same: the governor runs Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the army assists, and the council has 11 members.

Parliament must approve the move within two months, at a joint sitting. Dawn quotes the Constitution on that clock, Article 234(3). Radio Pakistan reports that a joint sitting is being summoned. This note uses that two-month approval clock.

PTI rejects the move. Dawn reports that Gohar said it was "completely rejected". AP reports that Bukhari condemned it. Those sentences are the party's response as those outlets carry it. The rejection is PTI's position. The government's side, in the reports used here, is the proclamation: an emergency, governor's rule, and a suspended provincial government. This note sets the two side by side.

The caption matches the screen. It names Articles 232 and 234, the suspension of the PTI government, the governor with an 11-member council and army support, the two-month approval, and PTI's rejection. The check closed at 6:47 pm IST. The post went up at 7:00 pm IST.

The links under the article are Dawn, Al Jazeera, AP as carried by KIRO 7, The Hindu, Radio Pakistan, two Dunya reports and Bloom Pakistan. Daily Times and Nukta are named in the check and are not given as separate links here.
