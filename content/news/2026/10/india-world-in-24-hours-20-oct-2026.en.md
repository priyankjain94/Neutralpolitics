## Rubio and Jaishankar in Mumbai

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio and External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar met in Mumbai. Rubio, in the caption, called an India-US trade deal "very, very close," with some items left. The deal is not signed. The longer note, posted at 7:27 pm IST, is [Rubio in Mumbai](/news/2026/10/rubio-jaishankar-mumbai-india-us-trade-deal-very-very-close-not-signed). Sources for that note include the US State Department remarks page and Hindustan Times.

## States seeking extra power

Several states, including Rajasthan, Gujarat and Maharashtra, sought extra central power to avoid night outages, officials told Reuters. The September shortfall was 560 million units, the highest since August 2023. Business Standard's 20 October report cites Reuters for that shortfall and for the requests, including those three states.

## Asian Para Games

At the Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya, the caption records an Indian 1-2-3 in the men's discus: gold Devender Kumar, silver Rakesh, bronze Pradeep Kumar. The Times of India spells the bronze medallist Pardeep Kumar. Nishad Kumar won a second gold, in the high jump T47, at 2.04 metres, a Games record. Sportstar and India Today report that clearance and that it was his second gold.

Earlier notes on this site: [day one](/news/2026/10/asian-para-games-2026-mona-agarwal-nishad-kumar-gold-day1), [day two](/news/2026/10/asian-para-games-2026-day2-deepthi-jeevanji-400m-t20-gold-games-record), and [day three](/news/2026/10/asian-para-games-2026-day3-pranav-soorma-simran-sharma-gold). The day-three note is a different discus, Pranav Soorma's F51/52/53. This section is the caption's 1-2-3.

## Jio Platforms and the anchor book

Jio Platforms raised Rs 8,110 crore from 212 anchor investors, the caption says, per a BSE notice, ahead of an issue of about Rs 30,200 crore. The issue opens on 21 October and closes on 23 October. BSE notice 20261019-46 is the exchange notice on the anchor allocation. CNBC-TV18 reports the Rs 8,110 crore figure and the count of 212 investors. This is not investment advice.

## A missile strike in Shabwa

A Houthi missile strike on a training camp in Shabwa, Yemen, killed 73 recruits, and 14 were missing, per Yemeni officials. Reuters reports that toll from the provincial account. The Houthis say the camp was run by Saudi forces. Reuters names the spokesperson as Yahya Saree. Both accounts are in that report. This note does not choose between them.

## Curfews in France

After weekend riots, local curfews were in place in several cities, including Paris, Lyon, Nice and Bordeaux. Interior Minister Nunez put the number in custody at 2,513. Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu said the night was calm and that more curfews would be imposed where needed. ABC News reports those lines.

## Alberta's remain projection

CBC projects that Alberta voters chose "remain." The caption's unofficial count is about 76.5 percent remain. The vote was on starting a separation process, not on a completed separation. Final results are on 29 October. CBC's published line, cited here, is the projection that voters chose to remain.
