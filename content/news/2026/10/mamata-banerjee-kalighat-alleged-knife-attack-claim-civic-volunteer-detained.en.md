This note is NP VERIFY. The stamp is verified for the altercation, the detention and the complaint, and developing for everything past that. It was checked at 2:13 pm IST on 14 October 2026 and posted at 2:18 pm IST.

Developing: details may change.

Around 6 pm on 13 October, outside Mamata Banerjee's home in Kalighat, Kolkata, a civic volunteer confronted her. Mamata Banerjee is the former chief minister of West Bengal. The volunteer is Anusua Saha, 38. Kolkata Police describe what followed as a heated altercation. She was detained. The word in this note for that step is detained. A civic volunteer, in the definition NDTV gives, is a contract helper who works with the police.

Trinamool Congress, Mamata Banerjee's party, filed a police complaint. The Hindu, updated at 10:32 am IST on 14 October, reports the complaint and quotes the police statement. The same report says no injuries were reported. The Times of India reports that she has Z+ security. The caption glosses Z+ as top-level security. The caption also asks how someone got that close. This note records the question. It does not answer it.

The party's allegation and the police statement are not the same account.

Trinamool Congress alleges that a woman tried to attack her with a knife. That sentence is the party's allegation. The Hindu and NDTV report that the police statement does not mention a knife or any sharp object. NDTV adds that police made no mention of an attack or a sharp object. The Indian Express reports that police did not confirm an attack, and that she was detained. The Indian Express also places the episode at about 6 pm.

The Times of India reports an officer saying an FIR is being drawn up and that CCTV has been collected. No findings from that footage have been published. No FIR sections have been published. The same outlet reports the Z+ security, and it notes that this was the first puja inauguration of the season. That timing line is The Times of India's. It is not a finding about what was said in the altercation.

A recheck before the post, at 2:13 pm IST, read The Hindu, The Indian Express, NDTV and The Times of India again, and searched. The position was unchanged. FIR sections were still unpublished. CCTV findings were still unpublished. The knife was still absent from the police statement. The step on record was still detention.

Trinamool Congress leaders allege a BJP conspiracy. The caption marks that as a claim, and says there is no proof. This note keeps it there. The allegation is the party's. These sources do not supply proof of a conspiracy, and this note does not treat the allegation as a finding.
