On Tuesday, 6 October 2026, a projectile struck the Panama-flagged commercial vessel MT On Peace, IMO 9893204, while it was transiting the Strait of Hormuz off the coast of Oman. India's Ministry of External Affairs said so in a statement. The Times of India, IANS, AFP, via Al Arabiya, and The Hindu with PTI quote that statement. This note is NP NOW. The crew figures below are verified as that statement gives them. Neutral Politics did not read the ministry's own site for this note.

The crew was 19. Seventeen were Indian nationals. The ministry said it was learnt that 12 crew members were injured, including 11 Indian nationals. Those are the ministry's figures, as the outlets quote them. The Times of India, IANS and AFP agree on 19, 17, 12 and 11. This note does not add a count.

With the assistance of the Omani authorities, the injured were evacuated and are receiving medical treatment in Khasab, Oman. The ministry thanked the Omani authorities for their support.

India condemned the attack. The ministry said continuing attacks on shipping in the region remain a cause of deep concern for India, and it reiterated a call for an immediate end to such attacks, in the interests of peace and stability. The statement said: "The targeting of commercial shipping, seafarers and civilian infrastructure in the region must end, and navigation and commerce through international waterways must be restored at the earliest."

The ministry did not say who fired the projectile. This note does not name anyone, and it does not attribute the strike.

The Strait of Hormuz is a key route for oil and gas from the Gulf, including much of India's oil supply, and India is one of the largest contributors of sailors to merchant shipping worldwide. AFP reports that context. It is not an account of who struck this ship.

This vessel is MT On Peace. A separate published note covers a different ship, MT Kazimah III: [Five Indians safe ashore after a projectile hit MT Kazimah III in the Strait of Hormuz](/news/2026/10/hormuz-tanker-kazimah-iii-indians-safe). The two are not the same incident. This note does not retell that one.

As of about 7:14 pm IST on 6 October, the ministry's figures of 19, 17, 12 and 11 were unchanged in the reports checked, and no deaths were reported. This note was posted at 7:15 pm IST.
