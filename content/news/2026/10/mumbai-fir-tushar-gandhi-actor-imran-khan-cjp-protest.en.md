This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified. It was last checked at 2:49 pm IST on 11 October 2026 and posted at 2:51 pm IST.

Mumbai Police have registered an FIR over a protest on 10 October at August Kranti Maidan. The FIR is at Gamdevi police station. It names 12 people. The names this note uses, because the reports put them in that list of 12, are Tushar Gandhi and actor Imran Khan. "Actor" stays in front of Imran Khan so the name is not read as anyone else. The caption adds about 350 others besides the 12. The Hindu and The Times of India are the reports for the 12 named. IANS reports the FIR after the detentions.

The sections, as listed, are not identical in every paper. The Hindu, IANS and ANI give BNS 223, 126(2), 189(2), 189(3) and 189(5). The Times of India gives 223, 126(2) and 189(2), and says among others. This note records both lists. It does not collapse them into one.

What those sections allege about the protest is alleged. An FIR is the police paper that starts a case. It is not a court's finding. This note says the 12 are named in the FIR. It does not say a court has held anything against them.

The detention figure is separate from the FIR list. The Hindu, at 11:12 am IST, and The Times of India in Mumbai, at 7:52 am IST, quote DCP Zone 2 Ragasudha R. About 600 people were detained across Mumbai, and they were released. That is the figure this note uses. Police, on the screen, say the legal process continues. IANS, at 12:38 pm IST, is a further report of the FIR after those detentions.

The ground is August Kranti Maidan. The protest is described as pro-CJP, on 10 October, over the CEC/SIR row. That is the caption's one line for what the protest was about. ANI, carried by Daily Prabhat, notes that this maidan is where Quit India was launched in 1942. That is the one explainer line. It is history of the ground, not a claim about this FIR.

Read as three reported facts and one boundary. Named in the FIR: 12 people, including Tushar Gandhi and actor Imran Khan, and about 350 others. Detained: about 600 across Mumbai. Released: those detained, in the DCP's account to The Hindu and The Times of India. The boundary: the FIR's accusations stay alleged until a court says otherwise. The legal process, police say, continues.
