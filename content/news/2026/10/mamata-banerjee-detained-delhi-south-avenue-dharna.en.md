This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified for the detention. It was last checked at 2:58 pm IST on 10 October 2026 and posted at 2:58 pm IST. The word in the reports used here is detained.

Delhi Police detained Mamata Banerjee in New Delhi during a dharna on the road at South Avenue. PTI's posts, as logged in this check, are a video at 2:13 pm IST, a photo at 2:15 pm IST, and a News Alert at 2:20 pm IST, with the words detained by Delhi Police. ANI, at 2:25 pm IST, said Mamata Banerjee and other TMC MPs were detained by Delhi Police. Oneindia, read at 2:37 pm IST, reported her detained near South Avenue. The Business Standard, Dhaka, citing HT, reported the detention in Delhi. The caption describes her as a former chief minister of Bengal.

The dharna and the demand are hers. She was on the road at South Avenue. Her demand, and only as her demand, was the removal of CEC Gyanesh Kumar over SIR. This note attributes that demand to her. It does not treat the demand as a finding about the Election Commission. No reply from the Election Commission, and no reply from the government, was sourced, so none is implied.

A separate permission decision sits beside that dharna, and it is not the same event. Delhi Police refused permission for CJP's protest at Jantar Mantar on 10 October. The check names a letter from the DCP, New Delhi, dated 9 October, reported by PTI, The Hindu, Mint and HT. ThePrint and The Hindu are the reports linked here. That refusal was for CJP's Jantar Mantar protest. It is not reported here as a refusal of her dharna at South Avenue.

The citywide detentions are a floor, not a single settled total. India Today's figure, 400 or more people detained across the city, is the floor this note uses. Other reports give higher totals. Oneindia's figure is about 500. TOI, via PressInsider, gives 1,500 or more by about 1:00 pm IST. Those higher figures are recorded as what those reports say. They are not substituted for the 400-plus floor.

Where she was taken is not confirmed. Release is not confirmed. As of 2:58 pm IST, the last check, no release had been reported by PTI, ANI, Delhi Police or TMC, on X or in the web search done for this note. Derek O'Brien, a TMC MP, posted at 2:34 pm IST that a prison van took "Mamata Di along with 5 of us" to an unknown destination. That is his claim, on the party side. It is not police-confirmed, and this note does not adopt it as where she was taken.
