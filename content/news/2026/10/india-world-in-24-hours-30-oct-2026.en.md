This roundup is verified. The carousel was posted at 9:10 pm IST on 30 October 2026. The package was current as of 8:00 pm IST. Seven stories follow, each from the caption and that item's lines. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. One item already has its own note on this site. This page links to it and does not replace it. The slides are archive photographs, shown whole. Each file line below is an old photograph, not a picture of that day's event. No crash-scene image is used.

## Joshimath

In Chamoli, Uttarakhand, a helicopter flying from Badrinath to Dehradun crashed near Joshimath. It was a Trans Bharat Aviation aircraft, registration VT-HCX. Chamoli district magistrate Gaurav Kumar says all six people on board died: the pilot and five passengers from Bengaluru. The cause is under investigation. This roundup does not guess at a cause, and it does not blame the operator or the pilot. The fuller note is [Joshimath crash: all six aboard dead](/news/2026/10/joshimath-helicopter-crash-chamoli-all-six-dead-badrinath-dehradun). The slide is a 2022 file of Joshimath town, Rohanshah657 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this crash.

## PM-KISAN

Prime Minister Narendra Modi released the 24th PM-KISAN instalment from Bhildi, in Banaskantha, Gujarat. The sum is about Rs 18,680 crore. It went to more than 9.34 crore farmer families, Rs 2,000 to each family. The caption says eKYC is required to receive the money. This page does not set out those steps. The Economic Times, the MyGov event page and Moneycontrol are the reports named for this item. The slide is a November 2020 official portrait of the Prime Minister, Government of India / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not the Bhildi event.

## Cyber-fraud rules

The Supreme Court, on a bench with Chief Justice Surya Kant, said that in the Reserve Bank's new cyber-fraud rules, banks will have to compensate people hit by digital arrest and by fraud, where the bank did not do the required checks. The court will look at the rules before they are notified. Said, here, is the caption's verb. This roundup does not turn it into a rule already notified, and it does not say that compensation has been paid. Business Standard, LiveLaw and The Hindu are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2019 file of the Supreme Court, Subhashish Panigrahi / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this hearing.

## Nandini milk

In Karnataka, Nandini milk rises by Rs 8 a litre from 1 November. Toned milk goes from Rs 46 to Rs 54. The state says Rs 7 of that rise goes to farmers and Rs 1 to the cooperatives. The Economic Times, The Indian Express and The Hindu are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2024 file of a Nandini milk booth in Mysuru, Timothy A. Gonsalves / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this price decision.

## Rafale Marine

The Indian Navy: a Rafale Marine fighter jet landed on INS Vikrant for the first time on 29 October. These are trials. The jet has not been inducted. India has ordered 26 jets. The Times of India, CNBC-TV18, India Today, The Week and the Indian Navy account are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2022 file of INS Vikrant, Indian Navy / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not this landing.

## Binance

Binance, described in the caption as the world's largest crypto exchange, reported a security incident. About $6.3 billion in assets were impacted, the Associated Press and CNBC say. That is the caption's verb: impacted. This page does not call the sum stolen, and it does not call it hacked. Tether and Circle froze more than $2.38 billion in tokens. Binance says it will cover users' losses. That pledge is Binance's statement. This is not investment advice. The Associated Press, Binance's announcement, CNBC and a Reuters report carried by StreetInsider are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2022 file of Binance merchandise, Bretwa / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this incident.

## Bhutan

After the Shintaphu glacial lake burst in Gasa, Bhutan, the Central Water Commission issued a flood alert for Alipurduar in West Bengal and Dhubri in Assam. Thousands of people were moved as a precaution. Officials say the Sankosh river stayed below the danger mark. The Indian Express, News on Air, Hindustan Times and NDTV are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2018 file of a river in Bhutan, Bernard Gagnon / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0. It is not the Sankosh on this day.
