This note is NP NOW. It was last checked at 5:12 pm IST on 9 October 2026 and posted at 5:20 pm IST. On 8 October the United States sanctioned two Mumbai companies and five Indian nationals. The trade claims in this note are US allegations. No court has ruled on them.

The State Department designated SSPL Solutions Private Limited and Samudra Marine Services Private Limited, both based in Mumbai, under Executive Order 13846, an Iran sanctions order. The Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control added the two companies and the five individuals to the Specially Designated Nationals list the same day. The individuals, spelled as on that list, are VORA, Dhwani Nisarg and VORA, Nisarg Samir, linked to SSPL, and CHUNDAKATTIL, Harishyam Hariharan; KOCHIKAR, Ketan Manohar; and SAHU, Bhupendrasingh Dhalsingh, linked to Samudra.

A State Department fact sheet alleges that SSPL, described as a petrochemical trading company, imported $2 million of Iranian-origin petrochemical products between February and July 2025. It alleges that Samudra, described as a customs broker, facilitated multiple shipments of Iranian-origin petrochemical products into India. It describes all five as directors. Those sentences are US government allegations. The Hindu and PTI, and the wider US action, also speak of petroleum, petroleum products and petrochemicals. The firm-specific allegation used here is the fact sheet's petrochemical wording.

Under the Treasury's description of the listing, property and interests in property of listed persons that are in the United States, or held by US persons, are blocked and must be reported to OFAC. US persons are generally barred from dealing with them unless a licence allows it. A 50 percent rule applies. The Treasury also describes a secondary-sanctions risk for foreign financial institutions. That is a description of the US programme. It is not a finding against any named Indian bank.

OFAC's General License EE authorises transactions needed to wind down dealings with Samudra, and with entities it owns 50 percent or more, until 12:01 a.m. US Eastern time on 23 October 2026. The US record gives that clock time as 9:31 a.m. IST. Payments to blocked persons go into a blocked interest-bearing account in the United States. No such licence was issued for SSPL.

The US says the action is part of Operation Economic Outcast, which Treasury Secretary Bessent announced on 24 August 2026, and which it describes as aimed at Iran's oil revenue. The Treasury separately designated 17 shadow-fleet vessels the same day, under Executive Order 13902. Those vessels are not SSPL or Samudra. This note does not name them.

No statement from India's Ministry of External Affairs, and no response from either firm, was found as of 5:12 pm IST on 9 October. The Hindu, carrying PTI, and Firstpost reported that check.
