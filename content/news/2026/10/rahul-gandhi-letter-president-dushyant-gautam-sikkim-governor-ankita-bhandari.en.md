It was checked at 9:00 am IST on 16 October 2026. The post went up at 11:48 am IST. A recheck at posting found the President's reply still pending.

On 15 October 2026, Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi wrote to President Droupadi Murmu. The letter asks that the Centre be asked to reconsider Dushyant Gautam's appointment as Governor of Sikkim, until the CBI probe of the alleged VIP angle in the Ankita Bhandari case is complete. The Hindu, India Today and the Free Press Journal report the letter.

The letter, as those reports describe it, cites Article 361. A criminal case cannot proceed against a Governor during the term. That protection does not remain after the term ends. It cites Article 74(1). The President can return the Council of Ministers' advice once, for reconsideration.

The appointment the letter asks be reconsidered is a separate published note: [President Murmu appoints Governors for six states](/news/2026/10/president-appoints-new-governors-6-states-oct-2026). That note is the 12 October communique. This draft is the later letter. They are not the same event.

The background, as the reports give it, is a murder in 2022. Ankita Bhandari, a 19-year-old resort receptionist in Uttarakhand, was murdered. Pulkit Arya and two associates were convicted and sentenced to life imprisonment in May 2025. The reports do not agree on how long it took to recover the body, or on where the resort was. This note leaves both out.

The VIP claim is an allegation. Ankita Bhandari's family alleges that the case's VIP was Dushyant Gautam. That has not been proved in court. Gautam's side is on the record in these reports: he has denied the allegations, and he has filed defamation cases. The denial and the cases are his response. They are not a court finding either way.

The same day, Delhi Police cited Section 163 of the BNSS, the prohibitory orders, and removed the parents' sit-in at India Gate. People taken into custody were later let go. Congress alleged manhandling. That is the party's allegation about how the removal was done.

Uttarakhand Police, through the ADGP, say the three accused were caught within 24 hours, and the chargesheet was filed in 88 days. That is the police account of the earlier investigation.

What is ahead is developing. The CBI probe is continuing. The President's reply was pending when this was checked at 9:00 am IST, and it was still pending when the post went up at 11:48 am IST.
