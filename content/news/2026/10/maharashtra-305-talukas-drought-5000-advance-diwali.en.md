This note is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is verified. It is a new decision, not a rewrite of the earlier drought declaration. NDTV, carrying PTI, The Hindu and PTI via BusinessLine were read in full at about 3:00 pm IST on 17 October 2026. The reel was posted at 5:16 pm IST the same day. The caption's hashtag is NPExplains.

The state added 40 talukas to the drought list. The count moves from 265 to 305 out of 358, which the reports put at more than 85 percent. Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said this after a cabinet meeting, and said a supplementary government resolution was issued. The package's date note places the announcement on Wednesday 14 October 2026. The Hindu's report is timed 9:05 pm IST on 15 October. BusinessLine's PTI copy is timed 5:20 pm IST on 16 October. Those are the report times. They are not three different decisions.

The ₹5,000 is an advance on drought relief, paid into farmers' bank accounts before Diwali. The caption says it is not a bonus, and this note does not call it one. NDTV reports that the money comes before Diwali because the central assessment cannot conclude before the festival. The Chief Minister's line, in the package, is that a full administrative and central assessment cannot finish before Diwali, so this advance comes first. BusinessLine reports that he described ₹5,000 as immediate relief and as an advance on central assistance. That sentence does not say central aid has been sanctioned. This note does not say it has.

Twelve state relief measures apply in these talukas. The Hindu names these among them: restructuring of crop loans, a stay on recovery of farm loans, relief on farm-pump electricity bills, a waiver of students' exam fees, and exemption from land revenue. The caption names crop-loan restructuring, a stay on farm-loan recovery, farm-pump bill relief, and an exam-fee waiver. This note does not invent the measures that are not named. The total in the reports is 12.

A separate line, not part of the 14 October list itself, is a salary pledge. IAS and IPS officers pledged one day's salary for October to the Chief Minister's Relief Fund. NDTV's report is dated 17 October. ABP Majha reported it at 7:37 pm IST on 16 October. Both were read at about 3:01 pm IST on 17 October. The on-screen line in the reel cites NDTV (17 October) and ABP Majha, and does not date the pledge to a single day. This note does the same.

The caption says drought is usually declared by the end of November, and that this notification came in October, with Diwali ahead. That timing line is the caption's.

An earlier published note covers the declaration of 265 of 358 talukas: [Maharashtra declares drought in 265 of 358 talukas; crop-loss aid is still a separate order](/news/2026/10/maharashtra-drought-265-talukas). This article does not replace that one. The new facts are the 40 added talukas, the 305 total, the supplementary resolution, the ₹5,000 advance, and the salary pledge.
