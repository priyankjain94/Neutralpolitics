This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 4:58 pm IST on 29 October 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic, a typographic card. The data line on the cover is the Spanish Transport Ministry, via media reports. No photograph is used. No crash-site image is used.

On Wednesday 28 October, near Urda, in Toledo province, two high-speed trains collided. They were a Renfe AVE and a stationary Iryo train. The toll is at least 69. It may rise. The caption says the toll is at that figure because one injured passenger died in hospital. About 50 people are still in hospital. That hospital figure is RTVE's. The search continues. Spain has begun three days of national mourning. The Guardian, RTVE and the Associated Press are the reports named for the toll of at least 69, for the hospital figure with RTVE, and for the mourning. The Guardian is named in the package. A separate Guardian link is not in the source list used here.

Transport Minister Oscar Puente's timeline is this. At about 08:30 the LZB train-control system failed, on a stretch of about 63 km. At about 08:36 the Madrid control centre, Adif at Atocha, authorised the AVE to resume normal running. At about 08:53 the train passed a red signal and hit the stopped train about 25 km later. He called the control centre's order inexplicable. Euronews is the report named for that timeline and for that word. The word is his. This note does not add a cause of its own.

Guardia Civil and the rail accident commission, CIAF, are investigating. RTVE says a control-centre operator has been questioned. This note does not name the operator. It does not blame the operator. Nobody has been charged. The drivers have not been blamed.

This is Spain's worst rail disaster since 2013, at Santiago de Compostela, where 80 people died. The 28 October roundup on this site used an earlier toll of at least 68, from the regional government, and said the cause was under investigation. The Associated Press and the BBC are named with the earlier figures. Those earlier figures are not the toll on this page.
