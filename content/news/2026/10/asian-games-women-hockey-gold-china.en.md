On 2 October 2026, at Gifu Prefectural Green Stadium, India's women beat defending champions China 1-0 in the Asian Games hockey final. Neutral Politics checked the official results, including the play-by-play, and the reports in Sportstar, The Times of India, Business Standard and Olympics.com. The final page was read at about 7:05 pm IST. This note was posted at 10:00 pm IST the same day. It does not state India's medal tally, and it does not state who passed for the goal.

The final page is marked Official. Its match time is 20:00 local. The source note gives that as 4:30 pm IST. Lalremsiami scored the only goal. Sportstar, The Times of India, Business Standard and Olympics.com call it the 10th minute. The official log records 9:18 elapsed in the first quarter. Sportstar's story is timed at 6:18 pm IST. The Times of India is timed at 6:24 pm IST. Business Standard, by Anish Kumar, is timed at 6:26 pm IST. Olympics.com's report is by Utathya Nag.

In the second quarter, goalkeeper Savita Punia saved a penalty stroke. The official log, Sportstar, The Times of India and Business Standard name the taker as Zhang Ying. Olympics.com says Zhang Yi. This note uses Zhang Ying. The minute is not settled in one figure. Business Standard says the 23rd minute. The official log is about the 22nd. The fact used here is the save in the second quarter.

China pulled their goalkeeper in the last three minutes. The score stayed 1-0. Official match stats are five penalty corners to China and one to India, possession 52 percent to China and 48 percent to India, and one green card each.

The last India gold in this event was at New Delhi in 1982, 44 years earlier, as Sportstar, The Times of India, Business Standard and Olympics.com report. India won silver in 1998 and 2018, as The Times of India and Business Standard report, and bronze at Hangzhou, held in 2023, as Olympics.com and Business Standard report. India missed Paris 2024. Sportstar and The Times of India say this gold earns a direct qualification spot for Los Angeles 2028. Business Standard says qualification. Olympics.com says a quota. No International Hockey Federation statement was read for this note.

The route on the official results is five Pool B wins: Uzbekistan 19-0, Indonesia 17-1, Thailand 20-1, Japan 3-1 and Singapore 7-0. The semi-final, on 30 September, was 2-0 over South Korea.
