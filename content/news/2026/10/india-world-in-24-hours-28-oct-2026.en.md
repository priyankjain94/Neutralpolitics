This roundup is verified. The carousel was posted at 9:04 pm IST on 28 October 2026. The package was checked at 7:56 pm IST. Around 9 pm IST the Spain toll and the cyclone toll were rechecked, and those later figures are the ones used here. Eight stories follow, each from the caption and that item's lines. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. Three items already have their own notes on this site. This page links to them and does not replace them.

## Cyclone Arnab

Cyclone Arnab crossed the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coast near Narsapur early on 28 October. That crossing is IMD's. The storm is weakening, and the caption says the rain will continue. Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu's figure is at least 5 dead. Some reports put the figure at 6. The package names India Today and Deccan Herald for that 6. An earlier figure from the NDRF and PTI was 3. The later line on this page is the Chief Minister's. Ex gratia is Rs 5 lakh for each family. The fuller note is [Arnab crosses near Narsapur, weakens](/news/2026/10/imd-depression-bay-of-bengal-very-severe-cyclone-andhra-oct-28). The slide is an IMD INSAT-3DS image from 08:00 IST on 28 October. Source: IMD.

## The dearness rate

The Union Cabinet moved dearness allowance, for employees, and dearness relief, for pensioners, from 60 percent to 63 percent, from 1 July 2026. About 1.16 crore people are covered, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw says. The annual cost is Rs 10,016 crore. The caption says this is the dearness rate. It is not a 3 percent raise of the whole salary. The fuller note is [DA, DR raised to 63% from 1 July](/news/2026/10/cabinet-da-dr-hike-3-points-63-percent-from-july-1-2026). The slide is a 2019 file of North Block in New Delhi, Pinakpani / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this meeting.

## The Delhi voter list

The Supreme Court heard the Delhi voter-list revision, called SIR in the caption. The bench is Chief Justice Surya Kant, Justice Bagchi and Justice Mohana. The court said the Election Commission cannot alter Forms 6, 7 and 8. It asked the Commission to extend the 30 October deadline, and not to keep a cut-off for re-inclusion. The Commission said it would consider that. Two weeks were given. Asked, here, is the caption's verb. This roundup does not turn it into an order. The Hindu, NDTV and LiveLaw are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2019 file of the Supreme Court, Subhashish Panigrahi / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this hearing.

## Maharashtra's council seats

The final results for the Maharashtra Legislative Council, on the graduates' and teachers' seats, are these. Mahayuti took 3: the BJP on the Nagpur graduates' seat and the Pune teachers' seat, and Shiv Sena on the Amravati teachers' seat. Sharad Pawar's NCP (SP) took 2: the Pune graduates' seat and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. The caption's shorter line is Mahayuti 3, which is the BJP 2 and Shiv Sena 1, and NCP (SP) 2. Navbharat Times, TV9 Marathi and Pudhari are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2012 file of Vidhan Bhavan in Mumbai, Prashant Nadar / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0, not these results.

## Jio Platforms

Jio Platforms listed at Rs 1,358 on the NSE and the BSE. The issue price was Rs 1,119. The premium is 21.36 percent. Per BSE data, it is India's No. 2 listed company, after Reliance Industries. The caption says this is not investment advice. The Rs 1,358 figure is the listing price. The fuller note is [Jio lists at Rs 1,358, up 21.36%](/news/2026/10/jio-platforms-lists-rs-1358-21-percent-premium-india-second-most-valuable-company). The slide is a 2021 file of the National Stock Exchange in Mumbai, 312user / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this listing.

## Spain

Near Urda, in Toledo, on the Madrid-Seville high-speed line, a Renfe AVE train rear-ended a stationary Iryo train. The government of Castilla-La Mancha puts the toll at at least 68. The toll may rise. The cause is under investigation. A recheck around 9 pm IST named that figure to Garcia-Page, via Europa Press, the Associated Press and RTVE. The Associated Press, RTVE, The Guardian and the BBC are the reports linked here. The slide is a 2011 file of an AVE train at Madrid Puerta de Atocha, Savh / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0. It is a representative train, not the trains in this crash.

## Israel

The Israel count on this page is partial: 85.6 percent of votes. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc is at 53. Gadi Eisenkot's bloc is at 48. Arab-led parties are at 19. A government needs 61. About 600,000 double envelopes were still pending, on the package's count line. No winner is named. The package notes that the percentage was not rechecked after the 18:38 pull. The Times of Israel, Ynet and Globes are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2022 file of the Knesset, Clema12 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this count.

## Cebu

In Cebu, during the ADMM-Plus meeting, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh met US Secretary of War Pete Hegseth. They reviewed the 10-year India-US Defence Framework, which was signed in 2025. No new deal was signed. DD News and Devdiscourse are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2023 official portrait of Rajnath Singh, Ministry of Defence / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not this meeting.
