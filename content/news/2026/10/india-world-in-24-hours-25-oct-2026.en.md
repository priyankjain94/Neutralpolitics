This roundup is verified. The carousel was posted at 9:04 pm IST on 25 October 2026. The package was checked at 7:56 pm IST. Eight stories follow, each from the caption and that item's lines. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. The first three items already have their own notes on this site. This page links to them and does not replace them.

## The Bay of Bengal forecast

IMD National Bulletin No. 5, issued at 11:30 IST on 25 October, describes a deep depression over the Bay of Bengal. The bulletin's forecast, as this package uses it, is that the system is likely to become a very severe cyclonic storm by 28 October, and that it may cross the Andhra coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam by the forenoon of 28 October. The wind line is 115-125 km/h, with gusts to 140. The caption says this is a forecast and that the track can change. A cyclone had not formed. The system is not named, and this note does not give it a name. The fuller note is [IMD: very severe cyclone risk, Andhra](/news/2026/10/imd-depression-bay-of-bengal-very-severe-cyclone-andhra-oct-28). The slide is a 2019 satellite file of Cyclone Fani, NOAA / Wikimedia Commons, public domain, not this system.

## Pasighat

In Pasighat, Arunachal Pradesh, a protest against the Siang dam project turned violent. Per reports, the NHPC office and the police station were set on fire, and more than 40 police were injured. Chief Minister Pema Khandu appealed for peace. No group is officially blamed, and this roundup does not name one. The fuller note is [Pasighat: Siang dam protest turns violent](/news/2026/10/siang-dam-protest-turns-violent-pasighat-arunachal). The slide is a file of the Siang banks at Pasighat, Sumantbarooah / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0, not this night.

## Operation Trident

According to Home Minister Amit Shah, anti-drug raids under the name Operation Trident took place in Assam, Manipur and Mizoram. According to Shah, 18 people were arrested, and hawala transactions of ₹100 crore were found. The caption says the figures are the Home Minister's. They are not a court finding on this page. The fuller note is [Operation Trident: 18 arrests, Shah says](/news/2026/10/operation-trident-18-arrested-assam-mizoram-manipur-drug-raids-amit-shah). The slide is a 2011 file of an Assam Police vehicle, Deepjyoti05 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0, not these raids.

## The launch pads

ISRO chairman V. Narayanan says India is to have four operational launch pads by 2029. That count includes a third pad at Sriharikota and an SSLV complex at Kulasekarapattinam, in Tamil Nadu. The first launch from Kulasekarapattinam is expected in 2027. The package says those pads are not built yet. Expected, here, is his timetable, not a launch that has happened. The Hindu, The Times of India and Hindustan Times are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2018 file of a GSLV Mk III launch from Sriharikota, ISRO / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not the pads that are not built yet.

## The French Open final

PV Sindhu was runner-up at the French Open. She lost the final to South Korea's An Se Young. The scores, with Sindhu's first, are 21-16, 11-21, 12-21. The caption puts the same games the other way: Sindhu won the first, 21-16, and An Se Young won the next two, 21-11 and 21-12. Sportstar and WION are the reports named for this item. The slide is an illustrative badminton shuttle, annca / Wikimedia Commons, CC0, not this final.

## Iran, the deal, and a strike claim

Iran's President Pezeshkian blames the United States for the collapse of an interim deal, and he urges a new try. That blame is his. US President Trump says a strike is possible, in his words "maybe tomorrow", and that a deal is also possible. No attack has been announced. Iran denies a Hormuz offer. The strike lines are Trump's claims. Al Jazeera, Hindustan Times and Times Now are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2020 satellite file of the Strait of Hormuz, NASA / Wikimedia Commons, public domain, not this dispute.

## The marches in France

People marched in France over the cost of living and schools. The government's estimate is about 410,000. The unions' estimate is about 800,000. Both estimates are on this page. Neither is treated here as the other's correction. Nearly 1,000 people were arrested, according to Interior Minister Nunez. The package notes that this arrest count may be revised. Trump's line that "France is burning down" is his claim. It is not a description this note adopts. The BBC, the Associated Press and Hindustan Times are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2023 file of a Paris protest, Jules* / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this march.

## Riyadh

King Khalid airport in Riyadh resumed flights on 25 October after a closure of two weeks. The Houthis call the airport a military target. That is what they said. According to the Indian Embassy, Air India and Air India Express flights to Riyadh and Dammam stay suspended until 7 November. The caption's shorter line names Air India's Riyadh flights in that suspension. Al-Monitor, Middle East Eye and IANS are the reports named for this item. The slide is a 2008 file of the airport, Ned Russell / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0, not this reopening. The file is shown whole.

Pasighat's fires and the figure of more than 40 injured police are per reports, and no group is blamed. Shah's Operation Trident figures are 18 arrests and ₹100 crore in hawala transactions. Riyadh's airport is open again, and the Indian Embassy says the Air India and Air India Express flights to Riyadh and Dammam stay suspended until 7 November.
