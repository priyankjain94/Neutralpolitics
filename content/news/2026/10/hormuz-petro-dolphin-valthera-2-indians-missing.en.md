This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified. It was re-checked at about 10:55 am IST on 12 October 2026 and posted at 11:00 am IST. It records two strikes on 11 October 2026, on two tankers with Indian crews. The strikes are kept separate.

The first ship is MT Petro Dolphin, flagged in Nicaragua. It was hit by a drone at Sohar Anchorage, off Oman. Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal, quoted by The Hindu with PTI and by The Economic Times with IANS, said: "While nine of our Indian crew members have been successfully rescued and are safe, two Indian nationals currently remain missing. Search-and-rescue operations are actively underway by the local Coast Guard." The screen uses the same split: a drone hit, two Indians missing, and a Coast Guard search under way. The Tribune and The Economic Times also report the drone strike, the nine rescued, and the two missing.

The Embassy of India in Muscat is coordinating. News On AIR reports that the embassy is monitoring. The screen says the embassy is coordinating. Sonowal's post and the embassy's account are the sources named for the crew status.

The second ship is MT Valthera, flagged in the Marshall Islands. It was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz, and there was a fire on board. Sonowal said all 11 Indian crew on that ship are safe. The caption records the fire. The screen's line is that the Indian crew are all safe. This note keeps the projectile as unknown. It does not give it an owner.

The Ministry of External Affairs condemned the attacks. News On AIR, The Indian Express, The Economic Times and Firstpost carry the sentence: "We condemn the attacks on commercial vessels in the Strait of Hormuz region on 11 October 2026." That is the MEA's sentence. It names no attacker. This note does not add one.

The two missing people stay as Sonowal stated them: two Indian nationals. The caption records the same pair of ships. On Petro Dolphin, off Oman, a drone hit, nine Indians rescued, two still missing, and the Coast Guard search still going. On Valthera, in the Hormuz, a fire, and all 11 Indians safe. The MEA condemned the attacks. The caption says an attacker has not been officially named. This note stays inside that limit. The check that closed the note was about 10:55 am IST on 12 October, and the caption's status line is checked at 11:00 am IST, the time the reel was posted.
