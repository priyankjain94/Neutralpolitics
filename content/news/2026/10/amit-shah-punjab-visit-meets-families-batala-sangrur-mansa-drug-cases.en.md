This note is verified as an account of a visit, and it is held as a draft. The carousel was posted at 4:52 pm IST on 20 October 2026. The draft is the right shelf because the day covers killings and a family's allegation against a serving minister. The caption's sources are The Hindu, carrying PTI, Dainik Bhaskar, Punjabi Jagran, ETV Bharat, Hindustan Times and PIB. The links used here are The Hindu, PTI via ThePrint, Punjabi Jagran and Hindustan Times.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah met three families in Punjab on 20 October, in Gurdaspur, Sangrur and Mansa. The caption and The Hindu, via PTI, describe the day as meetings with families tied to the state's drug cases.

The first stop was Batala, in Gurdaspur, in Mari Panwan village. Shah met the family of Bikramjit Singh, who was 40, for about 15 minutes. The family gave him written demands. Bikramjit Singh was attacked with a sharp weapon on 15 October and died. Two accounts of why sit side by side in the caption, and in the PTI copy carried by ThePrint. Villagers say he opposed drug peddling. Police say the killing followed a scuffle over a mobile phone. This note does not choose between those accounts.

The second stop was Sangrur. The caption names the village Toor Banjara. PTI, via ThePrint, spells it Turbanjara. Shah met the family of Gulzar Singh, described as a Dalit man, who died by suicide on 7 September. The family alleges that he was harassed after he questioned Punjab Finance Minister Harpal Singh Cheema about the drug supply. That sentence is the family's allegation. It is not a finding. Punjabi Jagran reports that Shah assured the family of justice within two to three months. The caption says Dainik Bhaskar reports the same assurance.

The third stop was Mansa, with the family of Parvinder Singh Jhota. He was shot on 13 October while he was on a Facebook Live. A medical store owner is in custody. Jhota's father, Bhim Singh, had said earlier that he would ask Shah for a CBI probe. Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann said the case will go to the CBI. The Hindu's PTI copy records that announcement, made hours before the Mansa meeting. A report that the CBI had registered the case had not appeared in these accounts.

The sentences quoted from Shah in the carousel are not from 20 October. The caption says they come from his post on X on 18 October, carried by PIB. In that post, as the caption quotes it, he said the state government had "left no stone unturned in pushing the state into an abyss of drug abuse", and that the Centre "will track down drug traffickers and put them behind bars".

Punjab votes early next year. The caption notes that timing. The picture on this page is a map NP drew. It is a schematic. The caption says no official photograph of the visit was available.
