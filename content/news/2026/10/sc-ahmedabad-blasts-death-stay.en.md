On 1 October 2026 a Supreme Court Bench of Justices Vikram Nath, Sandeep Mehta and Augustine George Masih stayed the execution of one death sentence in the 2008 Ahmedabad serial blasts case. The convict is Ahmed Bawa alias Abbu Abubakar Barelvi, one of 38 people sentenced to death in that case. The Court issued notice to the State of Gujarat on his challenge to the Gujarat High Court judgment. The Hindu, carrying PTI, LiveLaw and Bar and Bench reported the order. This note uses only those three reports. It was posted at 6:33 pm IST on 1 October.

A stay pauses carrying out the sentence while the appeal is heard. Barelvi has not been acquitted. The death sentence has not been set aside. The 1 October order, as these reports describe it, does not decide the appeal.

The earlier record, as the same reports give it, is this. In February 2022 a special court convicted 49 people. Thirty-eight were sentenced to death, eleven to life imprisonment, and twenty-eight were acquitted. In July 2026 the Gujarat High Court upheld the 38 death sentences. Barelvi is one of those 38. The stay reported on 1 October is of his execution. The reports checked do not say what the Supreme Court has done about the other death sentences.

LiveLaw and Bar and Bench report that his counsel told the Court he was convicted on the basis of conspiracy. That alleged basis is what counsel submitted, in those two accounts. It is not written up here as a finding the Bench made on 1 October. The Hindu (PTI), LiveLaw and Bar and Bench also report that the 2008 blasts killed 56 people and injured more than 200. That toll is what those reports state.

What is not in the reports checked is the next date, and the result of the appeal. How the Court decides the challenge, and when it is heard again, is not known from this material. The notice to Gujarat is the step these reports record. It asks the state to answer. It does not itself change the conviction or wipe out the sentence.

## What's next

The reported order is a stay of one execution and a notice to the State of Gujarat. It is not an acquittal, and it does not set the death sentence aside. The special court's February 2022 sentences, and the High Court's July 2026 decision upholding the 38 death sentences, remain the earlier record these reports describe. Nothing in the 1 October reports says the Court has stayed the other death sentences.
