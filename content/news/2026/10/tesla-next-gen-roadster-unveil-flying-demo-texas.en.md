This note is verified. The reel was posted at 10:47 am IST on 16 October 2026. The facts below are the ones tied to the named reports. Acceleration times are left out because the reports do not agree.

Tesla unveiled the next-generation Roadster on 15 October 2026, United States time. The place was the SpaceX McGregor site near Waco, Texas. Engadget, The Verge and Electrek report the unveil and the site.

The demonstration was controlled. The car climbed a ramp. It flipped in mid-air. It hovered. It landed. The movement used SpaceX cold-gas thrusters. The Verge, Engadget and TechCrunch report that sequence. The caption's caution is the frame for it: this was a controlled demo. It is not an everyday flying car, the sort of vehicle a person would use on an ordinary day. The version that flies is invite-only.

Elon Musk posted the footage on X. The post says, "Turns out the future does have flying cars." The post time in the source record is 7:27 am IST on 16 October 2026. That sentence is his caption on the clip. It does not change the limit above: the clip is a controlled demo, and the flying version is invite-only. The footage credit is Tesla / Elon Musk (X).

Reservations have reopened. The deposit is $50,000. Of that, $5,000 is due now and $45,000 within 10 days. Tesla's own reservation page, Electrek and Engadget report the deposit split. A deposit is the amount paid to hold a reservation. It is not the price of the car. Production, as Musk states it, is in 2028. No price has been announced. Electrek, The Verge and Engadget carry both the production year and the absence of a price.

The reports do not agree on acceleration. Electrek's tier figures and other reports conflict. This note does not state a 0–60 time, and it does not pick a tier.

Tesla has been selling cars in India since 2025. No launch of this Roadster in India has been announced. A sale of other Tesla cars in India is not a date, a price, or a plan for this model.

What is verified is the unveil on 15 October 2026 at the SpaceX site near Waco, the controlled demo and the thrusters, the reopened reservation and the $50,000 deposit, production in 2028 as Musk states it, and the absence of an announced price. What is not claimed is an everyday flying car, a 0–60 figure, or a Roadster launch in India.
