Seventeen-year-old Kumkum Anil Mohod won the women's individual recurve gold at the Asian Games on Saturday, 3 October 2026, at Okazaki Chuo Sogo Park. She beat South Korea's Oh Yejin 6-5 on set points after a one-arrow shoot-off. Mohod's arrow was a 10. Oh's was an 8. The official results scorecard has that finish. Sportstar and Business Standard report it the same way.

The set scores, Mohod first and then Oh, were 27-29, 29-29, 30-29, 27-28 and 30-28. She trailed 3-5 after the fourth set. In the fifth she shot a perfect 30, and the match was level at 5-5 before the shoot-off. The official card, Sportstar and Business Standard all give that sequence. Business Standard's set scores match the official card.

On the way to the final she beat Kang Chaeyoung of South Korea 6-4 in the quarterfinal and Zhu Jingyi of China 6-2 in the semifinal. Those scores are on the official quarterfinal and semifinal cards, and in Sportstar and Business Standard.

Sportstar, Outlook and Business Standard describe the gold as India's first in individual recurve at an Asian Games. This note follows those three reports. It does not add a medal history of its own. The gold is her third medal of these Games. She had a team gold and a mixed-team silver on 2 October, and this individual gold on 3 October. Neutral Politics reported the women's team final in a separate story. This one is only the individual match.

The same three outlets say the result secures a women's recurve quota place for India at the Los Angeles 2028 Games. That claim is not on the official results page. This check found no World Archery statement, and no International Olympic Committee statement, saying the place is booked. The quota is what the outlets report. It is developing. It is not confirmed here.

Also on Saturday, away from the archery field, Pranavi Urs won the women's individual golf gold. She is 23 and from Mysuru. The round was at Kasugai Country Club in Nagoya. The official golf standings list her at 15-under 265, one stroke clear. The Hindu, carrying PTI and timed at 9:53 am IST, NDTV, and The Economic Times, carrying IANS and timed at 10:13 am IST, report that total. Those reports say her final round was an even-par 70. PTI, as The Hindu and NDTV carry it, calls her the first Indian woman to win Asian Games golf gold, and India's first golf gold since Shiv Kapur in 2002. India also took the team silver, at 14-under 546. China won the team gold at 22-under 538. Those team figures are in PTI and NDTV. They are a same-day golf result. They are not part of the archery scorecard.

Neutral Politics checked the official archery and golf pages, Sportstar, Business Standard, Outlook, The Hindu with PTI, NDTV, and The Economic Times with IANS, on 3 October 2026 (IST). This note was posted at 1:08 pm IST.

The cover is a stock photograph of an archery target from Pixabay. It is illustrative. It is not a photograph of Mohod, of Oh, or of the final at Okazaki.
