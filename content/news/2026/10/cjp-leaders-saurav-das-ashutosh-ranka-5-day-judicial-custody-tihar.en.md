This note is NP NEWS. The stamp is verified for the custody order. It was current as of 10:45 am IST on 14 October 2026, re-verified at 12:24 pm IST, and posted at 12:33 pm IST.

Cockroach Janta Party leaders Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka were sent to five days' judicial custody in Tihar Jail on 14 October. A Special Executive Magistrate court passed the order. The Hindu, BBC, Bar and Bench, India Today and The Wire report that custody. Judicial custody, as the caption defines it, means the accused stay in jail on a court order. Here that jail is Tihar. It is not police remand. A Special Executive Magistrate court, again in the caption's gloss, is a magistrate court that hears cases tied to peace and public order.

Bar and Bench reports that the two were detained on 13 October, and that the custody followed. This note uses "detained" for 13 October. The custody order is the 14 October order.

Police allege that on 13 October Das and Ranka climbed barricades and a locked gate and entered Jantar Mantar. Jantar Mantar had been removed from the list of protest sites on 12 October. Police allege that the entry breached a one-year peace bond they had signed after they were detained at the airport on 10 October. The bond carried a surety of Rs 50,000 each. The caption's gloss of that bond is a written promise to keep the peace, failing which each would have to pay that sum. The FIR alleges criminal trespass, breach of the bond, and disobedience of orders under Section 163. The Hindu and India Today report those allegations. The caption glosses Section 163 once, as an order restricting protest or a crowd in the area. These are allegations. The stamp does not treat them as proved.

Both deny the charges. Cockroach Janta Party legal head Ratna Singh alleges that lawyers were denied access for hours. BBC reports that allegation. The party says the two only went to unfurl the tricolour. India Today reports that account. Bail is to be sought in a higher court. A lawyer says bail will be pursued. As of 12:24 pm IST on 14 October, no bail order had been made and no higher-court plea had been filed. The re-check names The Hindu's 11:25 update, BBC, Bar and Bench, Hindustan Times, The New Indian Express and ThePrint. This note does not add separate links for Hindustan Times, The New Indian Express or ThePrint, because they are not in the source list.

A third member, Ajinkya Shinde, was released on a personal bond. The Hindu and Bar and Bench report that release.

The caption describes the Cockroach Janta Party as a new protest group that protests the voter-list check (SIR) and the Chief Election Commissioner. That description is the caption's. This note does not expand SIR past "voter-list check".
