This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified for one fact: Delhi Police registered an FIR. Everything the complaint alleges stays an allegation. It was re-checked at 11:05 am IST on 12 October 2026 and posted at 1:00 pm IST.

The FIR follows a complaint from the DSGMC. The complaint names Arvind Kejriwal and other AAP leaders and MLAs. Times Now, using PTI inputs, reported the registration at about 9:24 am IST on 12 October and reported that footage was being examined. Mint's headline, timed about 10:24 am IST, reports an FIR naming the former Delhi chief minister over the row at Gurudwara Bangla Sahib. The Mint article body was not fetched for this check. The headline and the time are what this note uses from Mint. ABP Live reports the FIR on the complaint, at Sansad Marg police station. The Statesman, timed about 10:02 am IST, is named in the same check. The brief does not give a full Statesman URL, so this note names the paper and does not invent a link. An ANI post was among the items read for the check. The official FIR document was not found. No FIR number is reported. No sections are reported. This note does not supply either.

What the complaint alleges is taken from The Hindu. On the evening of 11 October, The Hindu reported that the gurdwara body had filed a police complaint against AAP leaders. In that account, the complaint alleges political activity at the gurdwara and misleading videos. The 12 October reports are the next step on that timeline: the complaint is now an FIR. The Statesman, Mint, Times Now with PTI, and ABP Live are the outlets the check uses for the registration. The screen's wording for the alleged acts is disruption at Gurudwara Bangla Sahib during the 10 October anti-CEC protest, and misleading posts and videos. Those acts are allegations. An FIR is not a verdict.

Police are examining footage. That line is Times Now with PTI, in the same report that carried the registration at about 9:24 am IST. The check describes the investigation as ongoing.

Two further claims sit on either side, and both stay allegations. AAP alleges that police violated gurdwara maryada. Times Now carries that party allegation. Police, through DCP Sachin Sharma, say officers used a common passage. Hindustan Times, ThePrint with PTI, and The Hindu carry that account. The same police account says legal action will be taken over the shoes video. This note records the video only as the subject of that proposed action. It does not describe what the video shows.

The Hindu on the evening of 11 October is the filing of the complaint. The morning reports on 12 October are the registration of the FIR. The check treats those as one sequence, not as a dispute about whether a complaint existed. What happened inside the gurdwara on 10 October is not settled by these reports. The full sequence of that day remains open.
