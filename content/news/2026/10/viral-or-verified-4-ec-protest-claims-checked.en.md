Two viral claims from the protests around the Election Commission were checked. This is Viral or Verified number 4, under the NP VERIFY label. The stamp on both claims is misleading. The claims are alleged in the posts and clips. They are not findings. This note describes them. It does not show the posts or the clips, and it does not name the accounts PIB Fact Check stamped.

The first claim: posts said officials from the Union Home Ministry reached out to Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi to end their sit-in at Akashvani Bhawan on 6 October, after the march toward the Election Commission was stopped. That contact is what the posts alleged.

PIB Fact Check, the Government of India's fact-check unit, posted on X at about 5:56 pm IST on 6 October. The card stamped the posts "FAKE". The text said: "Do not fall for misleading claims or unverified information. Rely only on verified sources." The post gave no further detail. IANS reports that PIB declared the posts misleading. ANI, carried by Devdiscourse, reports the same. Dainik Jagran embeds the PIB post. Neutral Politics's label for this claim is misleading, which is also how IANS, ANI and Dainik Jagran report PIB's finding. The card's own stamp is the word fake.

The second claim, in India Today's summary, is that videos of young women dancing are from the ongoing protests demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. India Today ties that claim to protests in New Delhi by Opposition parties and the Cockroach Janta Party. It does not say the clips are only from the 6 October march. Vishvas News says one post, on 4 October, linked clips to Jantar Mantar.

India Today Fact Check, timed at 10:07 pm IST on 6 October, said: "These videos are old and unrelated to the ongoing protests." It traced both clips to GGDSD College, Sector 32, Chandigarh: a prom night on 7 March, and a club event on 18 February called "Riwayat". Vishvas News, in a separate check the same day, traced one clip to a Chandigarh college event in March 2026, and rates that check fake. Neutral Politics's label for this claim is misleading: old clips placed in a new context. India Today's verdict badge was not read for this note. The page text is the "old and unrelated" line above.

A separate published note is the 6 October march itself: [Police stop the INDIA bloc march to the Election Commission](/news/2026/10/india-bloc-march-ec-stopped-rahul-priyanka-detained). This note does not retell that march. The dance-clip claim, as India Today frames it, is about the wider protests, not that march alone.

A check at 9:31 am IST on 7 October still had the PIB flag, as IANS and ANI report it, and India Today's Chandigarh finding. This note was posted at 9:33 am IST. The earlier pass of the same material was about 8:05 am IST.

To check a viral clip: screenshot a frame and reverse-search it, find the first upload and its date, look for clues such as banners and the venue, and check fact-checkers. If you are not sure, do not forward it yet.
