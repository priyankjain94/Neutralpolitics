On Monday, 5 October 2026, the Bharatiya Janata Party named eight candidates for the Uttar Pradesh Rajya Sabha biennial polls on 16 October. Hindustan Times, India Today and The Times of India report the list. Bhaskar English also published a candidates list the same day. This note is NP NOW. The names, as those reports give them, are verified. No official reason was stated for who was left off.

The eight are Dharampal Singh, Radhakant Ojha, Prakash Pal, Vidyasagar Sonkar, Gopi Krishna Pandey, Priyanka Singh Rawat, Ram Pratap Singh Chauhan and Shruti Gangwar.

None of the retiring sitting BJP MPs from Uttar Pradesh was renominated. That includes Union ministers Hardeep Singh Puri and BL Verma. Hindustan Times, India Today and The Times of India report that omission. The reports do not give an official reason. This note does not supply one, and it does not speculate about ministries.

India Today also names others left out: Arun Singh, Seema Dwivedi, Neeraj Shekhar, Brijlal and Geeta Shakya. That further list is India Today's. India Today's headline describes Hardeep Puri and seven other sitting BJP Rajya Sabha MPs from Uttar Pradesh as dropped. This note does not add a name that those reports do not print.

Hindustan Times and The Times of India report a social mix for the eight: three from the Other Backward Classes, two from the Scheduled Castes, two Brahmin and one Thakur. They also report two women. Those are the papers' counts. This note does not assign a category to each name.

The Samajwadi Party named Ram Gopal Yadav and Dhanraj Nathwani. The Times of India reports those two names. The same paper says the polls may be unopposed if neither side fields an extra candidate. That is The Times of India's condition. It is not a declaration that the election is already unopposed.

Rajya Sabha members from a state are elected by the state's MLAs. Voters do not elect them directly. Strength in the Uttar Pradesh Assembly decides how many of the 10 seats a party can win. These reports state that rule. They do not set out a fresh count of MLAs in this note.

Neutral Politics checked these outlets on 5 October 2026 (IST). This note was posted at 9:01 pm IST. A later filing, a stated reason, or an extra candidate that would force a contest is not in the material used here.

## What's next

Whether anyone files an extra nomination, and whether the BJP states a reason, is not in these reports.
