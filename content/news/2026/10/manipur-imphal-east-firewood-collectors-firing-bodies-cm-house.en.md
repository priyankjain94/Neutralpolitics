This note is verified as of 5:50 pm IST on 19 October 2026. The carousel was posted at 5:56 pm IST the same day. The toll is not final. No official final figure has been given. The wording used here is at least. Each outlet's number stays with that outlet.

On Monday morning, 19 October, 16 people went to collect firewood in the hills near Nongshum village in Imphal East. Officials, speaking through PTI, said suspected militants fired on them. No group has been named. No arrest has been reported. The attackers are described only as suspected militants.

The clocks do not match, so both are kept and neither is smoothed into the other. PTI said the group left at about 7 am from Khongbal Sunurok Uyok in Kangpokpi, a point metres from Nongshum in Imphal East. The Assam Tribune, in a report timed 2:52 pm IST, said the attack was at about 9:30 am. This note says Monday morning, and it attributes each clock to the outlet that printed it.

The death toll is in conflict.

PTI, carried by ThePrint at 2:46 pm IST, quoted officials: two people killed, four injured, and three missing. The Times of India at 3:31 pm IST, citing police officials, matches that count: two dead, four injured, three missing. News18 at 4:20 pm IST matches it again: two killed, four injured, three missing. Those three reports are the officials' figures as published through the afternoon.

A second count says three. The Indian Express, updated at 5:36 pm IST, the Assam Tribune, and an India Today north-east report say three people died and three were injured. The Assam Tribune says the third person died on the way to hospital. Those are reports. They are not an official final toll. The earlier official figures given to PTI stay beside them. The numbers can still change, and this note will not pick one count and drop the other.

PTI described one of the injured people as serious. This note does not name that person. Names and ages of the people who died are not used, because the reports that give them do not agree with one another.

Locals brought bodies to Imphal and placed them outside the main gate of Chief Minister Y. Khemchand Singh's residence at Babupara. Police fired tear gas to move the crowd. That is reported by PTI, by The Times of India, and by News18. The bodies were later taken to JNIMS. PTI said it was not clear whether the Chief Minister was at the residence then.

Thokchom Lokeshor Singh, the local MLA, has demanded that a nearby SoO camp be removed, The Assam Tribune reported. He said Assam Rifles had assured the villagers of protection. The caption's phrase for that assurance is security forces. The Assam Tribune's phrase, which this note uses, is Assam Rifles. No official reply to the demand has been reported.

SoO means Suspension of Operations. As the caption explains it, it is an understanding among the Centre, the state and some armed groups. Cadres of the signatory groups stop operations and stay in designated camps. The camp in the MLA's demand is described only as a nearby SoO camp. The name of a camp is not used.

The picture is an NP graphic. It is a schematic map of Kangpokpi, Imphal East and Imphal West, not to scale, with the locations marked as approximate. There is no photograph of a body, of the gathering at the gate, or of anyone who was hurt. The families of the people who died have this desk's condolences.

What is on the record is a Monday-morning firing on people who had gone to collect firewood, attackers described only as suspected militants, bodies placed outside the Chief Minister's gate, and tear gas by the police. What is not on the record is an official final toll, the name of any group, or an official reply to the MLA.
