This note is verified. The carousel was posted at 7:39 pm IST on 30 October 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic, a typographic card. The cards are NP-made. No photograph is used, and no image of the crash scene is used.

On Friday 30 October, at about 2:20 in the afternoon, a helicopter of Trans Bharat Aviation crashed near Joshimath, in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand. The registration in the package is VT-HCX. The helicopter was flying from Badrinath to Dehradun. The place named is near the JP Power House, Marwari Bridge and Chai village.

Chamoli district magistrate Gaurav Kumar confirmed the deaths. The Hindu is the report named for that confirmation. The same report says an Army team at the site confirmed the fatalities. The caption also says an Army team reached the site. Chamoli superintendent of police Surjit Singh Panwar confirmed that all six people on board died. That confirmation is from PTI, carried by ThePrint. The Times of India is the third report named with this crash.

The six people, in the name forms the package gives, are these. The pilot was Capt Himanshu. The passengers were Prathapa, Arun, Shankar Gowda, Sudheer K and H S Natraja. All five passengers were from Bengaluru. The package says outlets differ on full names. This page uses the forms above and does not lengthen them.

The cause is not yet known. An investigation is under way. The operator's fleet has been grounded. That grounding is the step the package records. It is not, on this page, a finding that the operator or the pilot was at fault. This note does not guess at a cause. It does not blame the operator. It does not blame the pilot.

The same day's roundup records this item, and does not replace this note. [India & World, 30 Oct: 7 stories](/news/2026/10/india-world-in-24-hours-30-oct-2026).

Read together, the lines that stay inside the package are these. The day is Friday 30 October 2026. The time is about 2:20 in the afternoon. The aircraft is Trans Bharat Aviation helicopter VT-HCX, on the way from Badrinath to Dehradun. The place is near Joshimath, by the JP Power House, Marwari Bridge and Chai village, in Chamoli. All six people on board died, as district magistrate Gaurav Kumar confirmed in The Hindu and as superintendent of police Surjit Singh Panwar confirmed in PTI via ThePrint. The Hindu reports that an Army team at the site confirmed the fatalities. The names are Capt Himanshu and, from Bengaluru, Prathapa, Arun, Shankar Gowda, Sudheer K and H S Natraja. The cause is not known. The fleet has been grounded. Neither of those last two lines is a finding of blame.

The sources named on the carousel are The Hindu, ThePrint (PTI) and The Times of India.
