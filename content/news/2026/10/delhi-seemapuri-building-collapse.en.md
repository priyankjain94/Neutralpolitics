On Wednesday afternoon, 7 October 2026, a multi-storey residential building collapsed near Idgah Machhli Mandi, the fish market, in New Seemapuri, northeast Delhi. This note is NP NOW. The stamp is developing. It was last checked at 6:36 pm IST. A figure is used here only when the Delhi Fire Service, Delhi Police, the NDRF or the district administration is the source, and each figure is attributed. There is no official figure on deaths.

Delhi Police DCP R.P. Meena said at least eight people have been hospitalised, according to India Today at 6:29 pm IST. Those eight are six men, a woman and a three-year-old child, at Guru Tegh Bahadur Hospital, also called GTB Hospital. Earlier the same afternoon, PTI at 5:45 pm and The Times of India at 6:27 pm reported at least five injured. Both accounts stay in this note. The later police figure, via India Today, is at least eight people in hospital.

The DCP said some people had been rescued and sent to hospitals, and that two to three were suspected to be still trapped under the debris. "They are talking... rescue operation is underway," he said, according to PTI at about 5:40 pm IST. There is no official count of how many have been rescued. There is no official death figure. Other rescued or trapped figures have appeared in reports and in local accounts. Officials have not confirmed them, so they are not used here.

The time of the Delhi Fire Service call is not settled. India Today, citing the fire service, puts it at about 3:15 pm. PTI, NDTV and The Times of India put it at about 3:30 pm. Officials quoted by PTI said the front portion of the building gave way and debris hit two to three adjoining houses. Excavators are clearing the rubble. The Delhi Fire Service, Delhi Police, civic officials and civil defence personnel are carrying out the search.

The number of floors is not settled, so this note says only multi-storey. The DCP is quoted as saying four storeys by PTI and by Firstpost, and five storeys by The Times of India at 6:27 pm. Hindustan Times and NDTV say five. An India Today headline says three and the body of that report says four. This note does not pick a number.

The hospital named with the DCP's later account, via India Today, is GTB Hospital. An earlier IANS report, carried by Amar Ujala, named Rajiv Gandhi Hospital. Both are attributed. This note follows the later police account for GTB. India TV and Live Hindustan also reported the collapse on Wednesday afternoon. Casualty figures in this note are not taken from those pages.

Delhi Traffic Police said traffic was affected around Seemapuri Gol Chakkar, DLF Bhopura and adjoining roads. They asked people to avoid the area, and not to stop, park or gather near the site.

Officials said, via PTI, that the cause will be established after an investigation. This note does not state a cause, and it does not assign blame.

For context only, and separate from this rescue: a building collapse in Satya Niketan on 6 September 2026 killed seven people, according to the Delhi Fire Service and PTI, as The New Indian Express reported. That was about a month before Wednesday. The two collapses are not treated as one incident.
