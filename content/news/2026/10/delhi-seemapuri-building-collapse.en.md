Update, 5:24 pm IST on Thursday, 8 October 2026. Police have confirmed five deaths in the collapse of a multi-storey building near Idgah Machhli Mandi in New Seemapuri, northeast Delhi. Deputy Commissioner of Police (Shahdara) Rajendra Prasad Meena said so on record to Hindustan Times. Two more bodies, of women aged 60 and 35 who lived in the building, were recovered on Thursday. Their identities have not been released. The Times of India, with PTI inputs, and Dainik Jagran also report five deaths, citing officials. This note is NP NOW. The stamp is developing. It was last checked at 5:24 pm IST. This update was posted at 5:20 pm IST.

The five are two children, a girl of about 12 and a toddler, a 21-year-old woman who was four months pregnant, and the two women recovered on Thursday. The DCP gave that account to Hindustan Times, and officials gave it to PTI. This note does not name anyone.

Eight people were injured and taken to Rajiv Gandhi Super Speciality Hospital at Tahirpur. Police and the Municipal Corporation of Delhi said 11 people were pulled from the debris on 7 October, and that the eight injured were among them. PTI, carried by The New Indian Express, and The Hindu report that. As of 5:24 pm IST there was no newer official rescue count. One English report's figure of 11 injured is not used. This note uses eight injured, and 11 people pulled out on 7 October.

The search continues. The DCP told Hindustan Times that teams are clearing debris to see whether more victims are trapped. There is no official count of anyone still trapped.

The cause has not been officially established. The Times of India, with PTI inputs, says the exact cause is yet to be ascertained. This note states no cause and assigns no blame. It gives no floor count. The building is described only as multi-storey.

This follows the note posted at 6:39 pm IST on 7 October, when there was no official figure on deaths. That evening DCP R.P. Meena said at least eight people were in hospital, six men, a woman and a three-year-old, at Guru Tegh Bahadur Hospital, India Today reported at 6:29 pm. Earlier that afternoon PTI and The Times of India reported at least five injured. The injured figure in this update is eight, from police and the civic body via PTI, at Rajiv Gandhi Super Speciality Hospital, Tahirpur. The 7 October account via India Today named GTB Hospital. An earlier IANS report named Rajiv Gandhi Hospital. Hindustan Times described a GTB emergency on the Rajiv Gandhi Hospital campus. Those accounts stay attributed.

On 7 October the Delhi Fire Service was called at about 3:15 pm, India Today citing the service, or about 3:30 pm, PTI, NDTV and The Times of India. Officials told PTI the front of the building fell and debris hit two or three adjoining houses. The fire service, police, civic officials and civil defence searched, and excavators cleared rubble. Delhi Traffic Police said traffic was affected around Seemapuri Gol Chakkar and DLF Bhopura, and asked people not to gather there.

For context only, a collapse in Satya Niketan on 6 September 2026 killed seven people, the Delhi Fire Service and PTI via The New Indian Express, and The Hindu. It is a separate incident, about a month earlier.

A morning toll of four, in reports from ANI, Business Standard and Zee, is not the figure used here. This note uses five, from the DCP on the record. Local accounts of people still trapped are not used.
