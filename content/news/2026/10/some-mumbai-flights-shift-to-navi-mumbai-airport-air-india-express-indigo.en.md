This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 11:36 am IST on 26 October 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic. The photograph in it is of Navi Mumbai airport, by Suraj Digrase, Wikimedia Commons, CC0. On 25 October, IndiGo, the Air India Group and Adani Airport Holdings announced that some flights will shift in phases from Mumbai's CSMIA, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, to Navi Mumbai International Airport, NMIA. The shift is not every flight out of Mumbai. It is some flights.

Air India Express, according to Air India, is the carrier with the dated domestic plan. From December 2026 it is to operate four daily domestic flights from Navi Mumbai. From January 2027, Oman services. Kuwait flights are planned, and they wait on approval. Flights to Jeddah, Riyadh, Dammam, Doha, Muscat and Sharjah are to shift gradually, by the IATA Northern Summer of 2027. Those lines are Air India's. This note does not turn the Kuwait plan into an approved service.

IndiGo's line is a window, not a day. Between February and July 2027 it is to operate 12 daily international departures from Navi Mumbai. The places named are Bahrain, Riyadh, Dammam, Jeddah, Madinah, Kuwait, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Muscat, Dhaka, Jakarta, and a new flight to Kathmandu. IndiGo's final dates come after operating approvals. They are not set. This note does not give an exact IndiGo date inside that window, and it does not treat the window itself as a confirmed first day.

The reason given for the shift is capacity. Mumbai airport is to free capacity for the redevelopment of Terminal 1. The announcement is the source of that reason.

The move is not described as settled for every airline. The Indian Express reports that other airlines are still opposing this reallocation. This note does not say those airlines have agreed. Moneycontrol and CNBC-TV18 are the other reports named here. The package counts those two, both Network18, as one outlet. The Air India press release is the source for the Air India Express timetable.

An earlier note on this site is about a reported plan for international slots at Mumbai airport: [Mumbai airport has reportedly asked airlines to give up 265 weekly international slots; the plan is not final](/news/2026/10/mumbai-airport-265-weekly-international-slots-cut-plan). That page is the slots plan. This page is the later announcement of a phased shift of some flights.

For a passenger, the practical line in the caption is this. If you are flying from Mumbai in the coming months, check the ticket and the message from the airline, and see which airport the flight uses. CSMIA and Navi Mumbai are not the same airport in this announcement. A booking that does not name the airport is not, on this page, a reason to assume the flight has moved.

Read together, the lines that stay inside the announcement are these. The date of the announcement is 25 October. The carriers named for the shift are IndiGo and Air India Express, in an announcement by IndiGo, the Air India Group and Adani Airport Holdings. The airport gaining the flights is Navi Mumbai International Airport. The airport giving them up, for these flights, is Mumbai's CSMIA. Air India Express: four daily domestic flights from December 2026, Oman services from January 2027, Kuwait planned after approval, and Jeddah, Riyadh, Dammam, Doha, Muscat and Sharjah shifting gradually by the IATA Northern Summer of 2027. IndiGo: 12 daily international departures between February and July 2027, to the places listed above, including a new Kathmandu flight, with final dates not yet out. The reason is Terminal 1 redevelopment. Other airlines still oppose the reallocation, The Indian Express reports. Not every Mumbai flight moves.
