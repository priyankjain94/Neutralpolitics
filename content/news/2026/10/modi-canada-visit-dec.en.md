The dates are verified. The agenda is developing. This note was rechecked at about 9:00 am IST on 16 October 2026, and the post went up at 4:13 pm IST.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Canada on 11 and 12 December 2026. The High Commission of India in Ottawa announced those dates on X on 15 October. The Hindu and Hindustan Times quote that post. The Times of India is among the reports listed with them. A recheck at about 9:00 am IST used The Hindu, Hindustan Times and the Canadian readout. It did not pull a separate fact from The Times of India.

The near context is a phone call on 13 October between Prime Minister Modi and Canada's Prime Minister Mark Carney. The readout is published by the Prime Minister of Canada's office. The two leaders spoke of carrying forward the renewed momentum of the past year. The caption records that relations were under considerable strain in 2023 and 2024, and that the conversation is back on track. This note does not add a cause for that strain. It uses only the caption's description.

The agenda is marked developing. The lines named for it are trade, energy, technology, security, and people-to-people ties. Those are headings. They are not a published programme of meetings. The full itinerary is not public.

On trade, the two leaders repeated a commitment to conclude negotiations on a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement at the earliest opportunity, with the aim that two-way trade doubles by 2030. That commitment is in the Canadian readout, as the caption and the recheck use it. The agreement has not been signed. A commitment to finish talks is not a signed text.

The caption's reason for paying attention is practical. Indian students in Canada, families who live there, and businesses on both sides have a stake in whether the relationship improves. That is the caption's "why it matters". It is not a forecast of what the December visit will produce.

The photographs are from the Prime Minister's Office, via Wikimedia Commons, under GODL-India. One shows the two prime ministers at Hyderabad House in New Delhi on 2 March 2026. The other shows them on the sidelines of the G7 summit in Evian on 16 June 2026. The credit is "Photo: Prime Minister's Office (India), Wikimedia Commons (GODL-India)". The other cards are graphics made by Neutral Politics. The credit for those is "NP graphic".

What is verified is the announcement, the dates, and the 13 October call. What is developing is the December agenda. What is not signed is the economic partnership agreement.
