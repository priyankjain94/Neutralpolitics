This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified. It was checked at 6:47 pm IST on 11 October 2026 and posted at 6:57 pm IST. It records one thing: fresh clashes in Manipur's hill districts killed nine people.

The violence ran from Friday night through Saturday. Reports place it in three hill districts: Kamjong, Kangpokpi and Ukhrul. Hindustan Times, The Indian Express, India Today in its own report and in a PTI account, The Hindu with PTI, and The Times of India put the number killed at nine. The New Indian Express and Deccan Chronicle also report that the toll in this fresh round rose to nine. This note uses nine. It gives no names, no ages, and no split of the dead.

About 14 houses were torched. The Indian Express and India Today report that figure. The figure in this note stays at about 14 houses. The screen uses the same number.

Extra forces were sent to the hill districts. The Hindu, with PTI, and India Today, with PTI, report that extra forces were deployed to Kangpokpi and Kamjong. The screen's last line says the same thing in shorter form: extra forces were sent to the hill districts.
