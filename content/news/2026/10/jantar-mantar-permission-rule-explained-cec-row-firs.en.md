This developing note is NP Explains and NP NOW. Last checked at 2:51 pm IST on 8 October 2026; posted at 2:55 pm IST. An FIR starts an investigation. It is not a verdict. Alleged assault and alleged obstruction are not treated as fact.

More FIRs over the 7 October protest name Rahul Gandhi, Priyanka Gandhi Vadra and other Congress leaders and workers. Police sources told The Hindu at least two fresh FIRs were registered, for alleged assault on police and alleged obstruction of government work. Delhi Police told ANI, as ThePrint reports, that three were in so far, with more being registered. IANS reported three, citing police sources. This note does not adopt one count. By 2:51 pm IST no named officer was on the record, and no Congress response specific to these FIRs was found. Earlier, one FIR was reported by The Indian Express, The New Indian Express and The Times of India, and three by IANS and News18.

The first FIR, on 7 October, was over alleged unauthorised entry into Akashvani Bhawan on 6 October, ThePrint and The New Indian Express report.

Delhi Police said on 7 October, via The Times of India, that Jantar Mantar protests need DCP New Delhi permission about 10 days ahead, for up to 1,000 people. Standing Order 10/2018 says at least seven days. No amendment was found. Mazdoor Kisan Shakti Sangathan v Union of India, 23 July 2018, barred a complete ban at Jantar Mantar and the Boat Club and told police to frame guidelines.

On 6 October police said no permission was sought. About 260 people were detained after an Akashvani Bhawan sit-in. Rahul Gandhi said, as IANS reports: "We wanted to meet the CEC and the Election Commissioners." That march is a [separate published note](/news/2026/10/india-bloc-march-ec-stopped-rahul-priyanka-detained). On 7 October police stopped MPs short of Jantar Mantar for lack of prior permission, NDTV Profit reports. A spokesperson said 91 were detained, Hindustan Times reports; The Times of India and Moneycontrol said around 90. Reports differ on the distance and on where Priyanka Gandhi Vadra was taken. Some personnel alleged manhandling, Hindustan Times reports. She alleged, via PTI and ANI: "Their policemen have scratched me all over." Those detained were released a little after 8 pm, PTI reports.

On 8 October MPs lunched at Moets, Connaught Place, with Rajghat planned, NDTV and India Today report. No Delhi detentions were reported by 2:51 pm IST. They sought time with President Droupadi Murmu on the Special Intensive Revision and removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Sources said 10 of 15 names were permitted, Hindustan Times and Times Now report. No date was officially announced. The Times of India reported Mamata Banerjee's arrival and unconfirmed talk of a meeting.

The Election Commission said no authorised appointment was sought by 4 pm on 6 October, a later request was unusual, meetings are inside its premises, and differing views are normal, Hindustan Times and NDTV Profit report. Chief Justice Surya Kant, with Justices Bagchi and Mohana, said the court would "examine and take a call" on recalling the 23 September split verdict, The Times of India quoting PTI. No order was passed. A non-disclosure claim reported by LiveLaw is alleged. Refusal to suspend the Chief Election Commissioner is a [separate draft](/news/2026/10/sc-refuses-to-suspend-cec-notice).

The Delhi High Court hears an Indian Youth Congress permission plea on 9 October, The Hindu, LiveLaw and The Economic Times report. The Hindu reports a Cockroach Janta Party plan for 10 October; The Times of India reports permission was denied. India Today reports a Congress rally on 1 November. The Hindu reports that a Bombay High Court bench in Goa was told enumeration forms apply, otherwise Form 6, with the next hearing on 13 October. PIB Fact Check called Home Ministry outreach posts "misleading", ANI and IANS report. Dance clips were old footage from GGDSD College, Chandigarh, India Today Fact Check found, in a [separate draft](/news/2026/10/viral-or-verified-4-ec-protest-claims-checked). PTI via The Times of India and ABP report an Information Technology Act notice on 11 posts about "a recent protest".
