This note is verified. The carousel was posted at about 8:51 am IST on 24 October 2026. That clock time is approximate, as recorded from the profile. The series is NP NOW. The stamp is VERIFIED. The picture is an NP-made typographic graphic. No photograph of the site is used. The names of the people who died are not in this note.

The explosion was on Friday 23 October, at a house in Alampur village, in Siwan district, Bihar. The caption says firecrackers were stored at that house. The district administration confirmed that five people died. District magistrate Vijay Prakash Meena says all five have been identified. Hindustan Times, NDTV and The Times of India are the reports named for that official toll.

Hindustan Times reports that two people are still untraced. That is the newspaper's line. This note does not add those two to the five deaths the district magistrate confirmed. Untraced, in that report, is a separate line from those five deaths.

The action reported with the toll is this. Jamo Bazar station house officer Dhiraj Kumar and chowkidar Chandrika Manjhi have been suspended. ETV Bharat and Hindustan Times report the suspensions. An FIR has been registered. A special team under sub-divisional police officer Aman Kumar, of Maharajganj, is probing. The licence is also under check. Hindustan Times and ETV Bharat are the reports named for the FIR, the team and the licence check.

The licence check is not a finding about the status of that licence. This note does not go past "under check", and it does not assign blame before the probe. The suspensions and the FIR are the steps the reports record. They are not, in this note, a conclusion about who was at fault.

Read together, the brief's lines are these. The place is Alampur village, Siwan district, Bihar. The day of the explosion is Friday 23 October. The house is described as one where firecrackers were stored. The official toll is five dead, all identified, from district magistrate Vijay Prakash Meena, in Hindustan Times, NDTV and The Times of India. The two people still untraced are a Hindustan Times line. The suspensions are of Jamo Bazar SHO Dhiraj Kumar and chowkidar Chandrika Manjhi, in ETV Bharat and Hindustan Times. The FIR, the special team under SDPO Maharajganj Aman Kumar, and the licence check are in Hindustan Times and ETV Bharat.

The sources named on the carousel are Hindustan Times, The Times of India, NDTV and ETV Bharat.
