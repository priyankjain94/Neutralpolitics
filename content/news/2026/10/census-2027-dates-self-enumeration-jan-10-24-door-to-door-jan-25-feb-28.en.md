This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 8:48 am IST on 31 October 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic, a typographic card. No photograph is used.

The population-enumeration phase of Census 2027 is set by a Ministry of Home Affairs gazette notification, S.O. 5913(E), dated 30 October 2026. Self-enumeration is optional and online, from 10 to 24 January 2027. Door-to-door counting runs from 25 January to 28 February 2027. The revisional round is from 1 to 5 March 2027. The reference date is 00:00 on 1 March 2027. The Hindu and The New Indian Express are the reports linked for the notification. Mint is named for one further line. A gazette URL is not in the source list used here. The notification is cited by its number and date.

Mint says that a person who fills the online form receives a Self-Enumeration ID, and that the ID has to be shown to the enumerator. That is Mint's line. This note does not add the steps of the form.

This schedule does not apply in these places, listed as the caption lists them. Manipur, until further orders. Goa. Uttar Pradesh. Punjab. Uttarakhand. Ladakh. And the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir.

The Hindu says that in some states the count will start from 1 December 2026. The package does not name those states. This note does not name them either. The dates and the exclusions above were checked against the text of S.O. 5913(E). A separate PIB release was not located, so this note does not cite one.

An earlier note on this site recorded the Prime Minister's Mann Ki Baat remarks on Census 2027 self-enumeration. [Mann Ki Baat: Modi recalls the 2016 surgical strikes as they near 10 years](/news/2026/09/mann-ki-baat-surgical-strike). That note is not this gazette. The dates on this page are the dates in S.O. 5913(E).

Read together, the schedule is this. Self-enumeration, optional and online, 10 to 24 January 2027. Door-to-door counting, 25 January to 28 February 2027. Revisional round, 1 to 5 March 2027. Reference date, 00:00 on 1 March 2027. Mint says the online form produces a Self-Enumeration ID to be shown to the enumerator. The schedule does not apply to Manipur until further orders, to Goa, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Uttarakhand or Ladakh, or to the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir. The Hindu says counting in some states starts from 1 December 2026.

The sources named on the carousel are the MHA gazette notification, The Hindu, Indian Express and Mint. The newspaper link in the package is a New Indian Express report.
