India beat Pakistan by 19 runs in the men's T20 final at the Asian Games on Saturday, 3 October 2026, at Korogi in Nisshin, Japan. India batted first and made 211 for 6 in 20 overs. Pakistan made 192 for 6 in 20 overs. The official results page, the BCCI scorecard, The Indian Express and Dawn all have that result.

Abhishek Sharma made 61 off 28 balls, with three fours and seven sixes. Tilak Varma made 51 not out off 22, with three fours and four sixes. Shivam Dube made 27 off 15. Those figures are the official statistics. Some reports give Dube's 27 as off 14 balls. The official page says 15, and this note uses 15. Axar Patel and Washington Sundar took 2 for 29 each, again on the official statistics.

Hasan Nawaz made 96 off 51 for Pakistan, with five fours and seven sixes. He was caught behind off the final ball. The official statistics, Dawn and The Indian Express show that dismissal. Hindustan Times' text calls him unbeaten. The official card and Dawn do not. He and Saim Ayub added 87 for the fourth wicket, as Dawn and The Indian Express report. Arafat Minhas took 2 for 25. Ahmed Daniyal took 2 for 43.

Pakistan needed 33 runs off the last over after Jasprit Bumrah's 19th over. The Indian Express, Hindustan Times and Dawn report that requirement. Dawn says Nawaz hit Shivam Dube for a four and a six before he was out off the last ball.

Sportstar and Hindustan Times call the win India's second straight Asian Games men's cricket gold, after Hangzhou in 2023. Pakistan took silver. That silver is on the official final ranking, and Dawn reports it. Hindustan Times says this was the two teams' first meeting at these Games. This note does not give an overall gold tally for India. None of these sources is used here for one.

The Indian Express story is timed at 2:27 pm IST. Hindustan Times is timed at 1:55 pm IST. The Hindu, with PTI, filed an innings report at 12:08 pm IST. Neutral Politics checked the official results and the final rank, the BCCI scorecard, The Indian Express, Hindustan Times, Dawn, Sportstar and The Hindu on 3 October 2026 (IST). This note was posted at 3:38 pm IST.
