This note is verified. The series is NP NOW. The carousel was posted at 10:38 am IST on 27 October 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic, and the data are credited to PIB. The graphics are NP-made. No photograph is used, and no AI image is used.

On 26 October 2026 the Defence Acquisition Council met under Defence Minister Rajnath Singh. It gave Acceptance of Necessity, AoN, to capital acquisition proposals for the Army, the Navy and the Indian Air Force. The PIB release is PRID 2328486. The package dates that release to 16:21 IST on 26 October. PIB puts the estimated cost at about Rs 2,12,000 crore. Written the other way, that is Rs 2.12 lakh crore. This note uses that figure as PIB gives it. It does not replace it with a rounder number.

The caption attributes a dollar figure to Reuters: about $22.27 billion. The package notes that this dollar figure was not fetched on its own. This page does not add a Reuters link, and it does not treat the dollar line as a second official estimate. The rupee estimate on this page is PIB's.

The lists below are the caption's lists.

For the Army: a Self-Propelled Tracked Gun System; VSHORADS, which the caption expands as very short-range air defence; a counter-drone system, C-UAS; surveillance UAVs; long range kamikaze drones; High Mobility Vehicles; and Light Multi Role Helicopters.

For the Navy: an Active Offboard Decoy System, AODS, and other major naval platforms. The caption does not name those other platforms.

For the Indian Air Force: Special Utility Helicopters, air defence systems, weapons, and a Mobile X-Band Doppler Weather Radar.

PIB says about 82 percent of this procurement is to come from Indian industry. That share is PIB's figure. India Today and The Economic Times are the other reports named with links. The Economic Times report is one of the reports of this decision. This note does not move the 82 percent line off PIB.

AoN is a step, not the end of a purchase. It is the first step of defence procurement. It means in-principle acceptance that the requirement exists. After it come proposals, bids, trials and negotiation. No contract has been signed. This page does not describe the equipment as ordered, and it does not describe a price as paid.

Read together, the lines that stay inside the release and the caption are these. The date of the decision is 26 October 2026. The chair was Defence Minister Rajnath Singh. The grant was Acceptance of Necessity for capital acquisition proposals. The estimated cost, per PIB, is about Rs 2,12,000 crore, which is Rs 2.12 lakh crore. The services are the Army, the Navy and the Indian Air Force, with the items listed above. About 82 percent of the procurement is to come from Indian industry, PIB says. The caption's dollar line, about $22.27 billion, is attributed to Reuters and was not fetched on its own. AoN is the first step. No contract has been signed.
