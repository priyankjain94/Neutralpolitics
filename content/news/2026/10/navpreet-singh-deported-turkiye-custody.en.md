On Saturday, 3 October 2026, Union Home Minister Amit Shah said on X that India had secured the deportation from Turkiye of Navpreet Singh. Shah called him a "hardened narco-trafficking kingpin". He said Singh was "behind bars" in cases he is accused of. Those are Shah's words, as Indian Express, The Economic Times with PTI, News18, ETV Bharat, Hindustan Times, The Tribune, The New Indian Express and The Times of India reported them. News18 is timed at 10:22 am IST. The Economic Times and ETV Bharat are timed at 10:28 am IST. The Times of India is timed at 10:40 am IST. The Tribune is timed at 10:44 am IST. Indian Express updated its story at 4:25 am IST on 4 October. The trafficking label in Shah's post is an allegation. It is not a court finding.

Singh was produced before the Rouse Avenue Court in Delhi, sitting as a special court under the NDPS Act, by the Delhi Police Special Cell. The court gave Delhi Police 10 days' custody. India Today, timed at 8:56 pm IST on 3 October, and ANI reported that remand. Ten days' custody is not a conviction. He is in custody. He has not been found guilty in these reports.

What Shah alleges, and what the prosecutor alleges, stays in that category. Shah alleges that Singh obtained Turkish citizenship under the fake identity "Navab Virk", and that he kept smuggling heroin and methamphetamine from Afghanistan. In court the prosecutor called him the kingpin of the network, and said iCloud and app links tie him to co-accused. ANI reported that. His lawyer said he is innocent, and that aside from co-accused statements there is no evidence. ANI, including the copy Devdiscourse carried, reported the defence. None of those claims is proven in the material checked. This note does not treat them as facts.

Police told the court that Singh must be taken to Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Punjab. ANI, timed at 10:16 pm IST on 3 October via LatestLY, and India Today reported those four states. His lawyer said he will be produced again on 13 October. That date is the defence lawyer's statement, as ANI reported it. It is not a report of what happens that day.

The Tribune, in a separate Amritsar story, reports that SSOC Amritsar wants him in a 105 kg heroin case from Baba Bakala in October 2024. The Economic Times reported a 105 kg heroin seizure by Punjab Police in October 2024. The Tribune treats the Amritsar file as separate from the Delhi remand. This note does not fold that seizure into a finding against Singh.

No statement from Turkish authorities was found in the reports checked.

One earlier case sits beside this, and it is not this remand. In May 2026 Prabhdeep Singh, described by investigators as Singh's cousin, was extradited from Azerbaijan by the CBI in a 2021 Delhi heroin case. The Indian Express puts that seizure at 358 kg. India Today puts it at about 355.5 kg. Both figures are in those reports. The cousin description is the investigators' description. It is not a finding recorded here.

Neutral Politics checked those outlets on 4 October 2026 (IST). As of about 8:20 am IST that morning, the remand and the 13 October date were as the night reports of 3 October had them. This note was posted at 8:29 am IST.

The cover is a Neutral Politics schematic. It is not to scale. It is not a photograph of Singh.

## What's next

Whether a Turkish authority comments, whether he is taken to those four states, and what the court does on 13 October are not known. Custody remains custody.
