This note is NP NOW. The stamp is verified. It was checked at 8:55 am IST on 12 October 2026 and posted at the same time. It records one decision: Chief Justice of India Surya Kant declined a meeting, and the route he pointed to is the court.

Kharge and Rahul Gandhi had asked for the meeting. They wanted to raise SIR and the CEC. The screen names those two subjects. The caption names the same two leaders and the same two subjects.

The Hindu's article was updated at 7:55 am IST. In that updated text, the CJI's office confirmed that the request was "declined with reasons". The same check records that an earlier version of The Hindu quoted the Chief Justice himself: "Yes, I have declined with reasons." That quotation is from the earlier version. It is not printed on the screen. This note keeps the two apart. The current Hindu account, as re-read for this post, is the office confirming a decline with reasons. The first-person sentence belongs to the earlier version.

The reasons in the reports are attributed to the CJI's office and to sources, not set out here as this desk's own finding. The Times of India, citing CJI-office sources about a CJI email, says similar issues are already pending, that a meeting would "contravene" the separation of power, and that the Supreme Court has no administrative power in the matter, only an adjudicatory power. The same account says a petition can be filed and that a priority hearing is available. Hindustan Times reports the decline and connects it to that pendency and to the adjudicatory role. Bar & Bench reports that the matter is sub judice, that there is no administrative power for this request, and that the leaders are free to approach the court on its judicial side.

What happens next is a petition, on the screen's last card. Bar & Bench says similar matters are listed for Tuesday, 13 October, and that a petition could be heard with them. LiveLaw, Deccan Herald with PTI, and WION also place that hearing on Tuesday. Deccan Herald and WION are named in the check and are not separate links below. The Hindu's updated article says connected petitions are listed for 12 October. The screen follows the Tuesday date, which is the account in Bar & Bench, LiveLaw and Deccan Herald with PTI.

The links under this article are The Hindu, The Times of India, Hindustan Times, Bar & Bench and LiveLaw.
