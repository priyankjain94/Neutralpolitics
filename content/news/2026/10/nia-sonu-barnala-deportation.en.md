On 1 October 2026 the National Investigation Agency arrested Harwinder Kumar alias Sonu Barnala at Delhi's Indira Gandhi International airport after he was deported from the United Arab Emirates. He is an accused in the 13 April 2024 killing of Vishwa Hindu Parishad leader Vikas Prabhakar in Nangal, Roopnagar, Punjab. An arrest is not a conviction. There has been no trial and no verdict. Neutral Politics checked The Hindu, Hindustan Times, News On AIR, The News Mill and Indian Economic Observer, all dated 1 October 2026 (IST). This note was posted at 8:05 pm IST that evening.

The Hindu and Hindustan Times, citing the Home Ministry and the NIA, report the arrest and the accusation. News On AIR carried a Home Ministry statement the same afternoon, timed at 3:21 pm IST. Hindustan Times is timed at 3:08 pm IST. The Hindu is timed at 4:48 pm IST. What those reports establish is the arrest, the deportation, and the fact that he is an accused. They do not establish guilt.

The NIA's account of what he allegedly did is a separate layer, and it stays alleged. The Hindu and Hindustan Times, again citing the Home Ministry and the NIA, report that the agency alleges he operated from the UAE as a logistics provider and financer for Babbar Khalsa International operatives abroad. The agency alleges he acted as a recruiter and a handler, that he arranged weapons for the shooters, and that he received and transferred funds on the directions of two Germany-based operatives. Those are the NIA's allegations. They are not findings of a court. The names of those two operatives are not in these reports. This note does not add them.

The investigation file, as the same reports describe it, has these dates. The NIA registered the case on 9 May 2024 and has filed three chargesheets. A chargesheet is a prosecution document. It is not a verdict. Another UAE-based accused, Manveer Ram, was arrested on 13 August 2026. A Red Corner Notice was issued on 17 April 2026. Hindustan Times attributes that notice to the Home Ministry. Nothing in that list is a conviction of Barnala.

On the evening of 1 October, two outlets reported a court step that the others in this check had not. The News Mill, with ANI inputs, and Indian Economic Observer report that a Special NIA Court at Rouse Avenue in Delhi granted a two-day transit remand to the Chandigarh NIA unit. The person they name is Harvinder Kumar alias Sonu, a spelling those two reports use. The arrest reports above use Harwinder Kumar alias Sonu Barnala. Both remand reports name Special Judge (NIA) Prashant Sharma. They say he is to be produced before an NIA court at Chandigarh on Friday, 2 October. The News Mill story is timed at 5:47 pm IST. Indian Economic Observer's is timed at 5:44 pm IST. No other outlet was found carrying this remand. A transit remand moves an accused so he can be produced before another court. It is not a trial, and it is not a finding that the allegations are true.

What these reports do not say is whether the NIA will seek longer custody, what Barnala says in answer to the allegations, or when a trial could begin.

## What's next

The next dated step in the two remand reports is production before an NIA court at Chandigarh on Friday, 2 October 2026. That is a production on a transit remand, as The News Mill and Indian Economic Observer report it. It is not a verdict. Until a court decides the case, the logistics, financer, recruiter and handler account remains the NIA's allegation.
