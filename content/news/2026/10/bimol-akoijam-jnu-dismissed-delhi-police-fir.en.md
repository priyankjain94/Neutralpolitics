This note is NP NEWS. The stamp is verified for the dismissal and for the registration of the FIR. The complaints are allegations. It was checked at 9:05 am IST on 14 October 2026 and posted at 9:07 am IST. An earlier read of the same reports was about 2:40 am IST. This note uses the 9:05 am check.

JNU dismissed Congress MP Angomcha Bimol Akoijam from his professor post on 9 October 2026. He represents Inner Manipur. The dismissal followed an Internal Committee inquiry. The inquiry was into complaints by 11 women students. Those complaints allege sexual harassment. The Times of India, Hindustan Times, The Indian Express, ThePrint and Times Now report the dismissal and the complaints. This note keeps the complaints as allegations. It does not treat them as a finding.

On 11 October Delhi Police registered an FIR at Vasant Kunj North police station. The complaint came from JNU's CSO. The sections reported are BNS 75, 75(1)(i), 75(1)(iv) and 79. The same five outlets are the check for that FIR. He is booked. As of 9:05 am IST on 14 October, no arrest was reported and no detention was reported.

The caption's shorter line names BNS 75 and 79. The stamp, and the reports used for this check, list four citations: 75, 75(1)(i), 75(1)(iv) and 79. This note uses that longer list. The police station is Vasant Kunj North. The complainant, as the stamp records it, is JNU's CSO. The screen's sequence is the one in the headline: dismissed by the university, then booked by the police. The Times of India, Hindustan Times, The Indian Express, ThePrint and Times Now are the five links. They are what this note uses for the 9 October dismissal, the 11 student complaints, the 11 October FIR, the denial, and JNUTA's statement about the Executive Council.

Akoijam denies the allegations. He called it a "witch hunt". That phrase is his. The denial is his account, set beside the complaints, which stay alleged.

JNUTA says the Vice-Chancellor acted without the approval of the Executive Council. That sentence is JNUTA's. This note records it as the association's statement. It is not a finding that the dismissal was taken without that approval, beyond what JNUTA says.

The allegations are not proven in court. An FIR at this stage is the police booking described above. The five reports agree on the dismissal date, the FIR date, the police station, the sections, the denial, and the absence of an arrest in what they had published by the check.
