This note is a fact-check. The stamp is verified for what the police and the news reports say, not for a viral video. PTI, carried by News18 at 2:45 pm IST on 17 October 2026 and by The Siasat Daily, The Times of India at 12:42 pm IST, Dainik Bhaskar and NDTV India were read between about 7:40 pm and 7:50 pm IST. The reel was posted at about 8:16 pm IST. The package marks that clock as approximate, inside 8:15 to 8:20 pm IST. Both students are 16. This note does not name them.

The claim in circulation, as the caption describes it, is that there was a "firing" or a "gunshot" at a coaching campus in Kota. Local police denied the firing reports. NDTV India says police said no bullet was fired, and that the object was a metal toy pistol, or something like an air gun. The verdict on the firing claim is misleading. An assault is what the reports describe. A shooting is what police say did not happen.

What those reports do describe is this. On Friday evening, 16 October 2026, inside the limits of Jawahar Nagar police station in Kota, two 16-year-old JEE aspirants in the same class argued, and the argument became a fight. The injured student is from Bihar. The other student is from Uttar Pradesh. Per police, the Uttar Pradesh student allegedly hit his Bihar classmate on the head with the butt of a toy gun. The Times of India and the package's confirmed list tie that account to Circle Inspector Mukesh Meena. Dainik Bhaskar quotes DSP Manish Sharma and places the incident at about 6 pm on Friday. The blow is alleged. It is the police account. It is not a court finding.

The place is an ALLEN Career Institute campus. PTI via Siasat names ALLEN Career Institute. NDTV India names the Sabal campus. Siasat also carries an institute statement: the incident was during a class break on 16 October, and medical attention was prompt. That statement is the institute's.

The injured student was admitted to a nearby private hospital. Police say doctors have described him as out of danger. A case is registered against the other minor at Jawahar Nagar police station, on a complaint by the injured student's mother. Both are minors, and the caption says police did not give further details for that reason. The investigation is continuing. The package's recheck list says the reel states only that a case is registered. It does not say the other minor was apprehended. That point is not confirmed.

The motive is not confirmed. The caption says the real cause of the fight is not confirmed, and that police have not confirmed claims in viral posts. Police are looking into the cause and into the object that was used.

The picture of the city is a file photograph of Kota, by Kushagra140, Wikimedia Commons, file Kota City of Rajasthan View, licence CC BY-SA 4.0. It is the city, not the campus and not the incident. The other footage is representational: an empty classroom by RDNE Stock project, and a student at a desk by Nishant Das, both on Pexels. None of it is the incident. The package says circulating classroom or CCTV video was not used. There is no graphic image in the reel. The music is from Pixabay.

What police confirm, in these reports, is an alleged blow with a toy gun's butt, a student out of danger, and a case. What they deny is that a bullet was fired. What is still open is the motive, the exact object, and whether the other minor has been apprehended.
