Tejaswin Shankar won bronze in the men's decathlon at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday, 27 September 2026, with 7,934 points. Sportstar, The Times of India and The Indian Express reported that he won the final event, the 1,500 metres, in 4:33.09.

Japan's Yuma Maruyama took gold with 8,123 points. China's Chen Xinghua took silver with 8,012. The decathlon is ten events, scored over two days.

Sportstar said Shankar is the first Indian man to win two Asian Games decathlon medals, after his silver at Hangzhou. The same report said the other Indian in the event, Thowfeeq Noushad, missed the second day through injury. Inshorts carried the result card, citing The Indian Express.

The points gap to gold was 189. The report does not list Shankar's marks in the other nine events.
