Neeru Dhanda and Kynan Chenai won the trap mixed team gold at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya on 30 September 2026. They shot 34 in the final. Qatar took silver with 32. China took bronze with 23. The official results page, Sportstar, the Times of India, Deccan Chronicle carrying PTI, and Outlook India report the Indian pair and the 34. This is the mixed team. It is not Neeru Dhanda's individual trap final, which is a separate report from 29 September.

The record line belongs to the official page. That page flags India's 34 as an Asian record. Its records box lists the previous Asian record as 30, set by Qatar on 20 January 2026 in Doha. Sportstar describes the earlier mark as 30 by Qatar earlier this year. Deccan Chronicle, citing PTI, says the mark was set only this year, in January. Outlook carries the record as well. This report attributes the Asian record, and the previous 30, to the official page. It does not call 34 a world record.

India came into the final from fourth in qualification. The official qualification page, Sportstar and Outlook put India fourth with 136. On that same table China is first with 142, Qatar second with 139, and Iran third with 138. The top four reached the final. The 136 is the qualification total. The 34 is the final. They are not the same score.

Sportstar and PTI call this India's first gold in trap mixed team at the Asian Games. Sportstar's wording is a first-ever gold in the trap mixed team. Deccan Chronicle's PTI line is India's first gold in the trap mixed team event at the Asian Games. Outlook and the Times of India do not state that "first". The official page does not say first. The line is therefore what those two reports say about the event's history for India. It is not a label printed on the results page.

The individual gold is the day before, and the outlets here do report it. Sportstar, Deccan Chronicle via PTI, Outlook and the Times of India say Neeru Dhanda won the women's individual trap gold on Tuesday, 29 September. The partner in this final is Kynan Chenai. The score in this final is 34. The two events stay separate.

The papers do not agree on how many medals this is for her at these Games, so this report does not choose a number. Sportstar calls the gold her second medal in one line and her second gold in another. The Times of India and Outlook call it her third medal, counting an individual gold, a team silver and this mixed gold. Those counts conflict. What the four outlets do share is the individual gold on 29 September.

Other lines stay out because they sit with one outlet or because they blur the result. One Sportstar sentence says India finished third with a total of 34. That contradicts Sportstar's own headline and the official page, which have India first with 34, so it is not used. The Times of India's series scores, 6, 16, 26 and 34, and Sportstar's shot-by-shot misses, are single-source detail and are not used. Kynan Chenai's earlier medals, and a World Cup line for Neeru, are not in the agreed account. The names of Qatar's silver pair appear only in Deccan Chronicle. Iran's 12 in the final is on the official page and not in these newspapers, so it is not stated as a shared result. A medal-table total, including a line that this was India's ninth gold, sits with one paper while other tables disagree, and it is not stated. No Olympic quota for this event is in the material used here.

## What's next

The shared result is a trap mixed team gold for Neeru Dhanda and Kynan Chenai: 34, to Qatar's 32 and China's 23, after qualifying fourth with 136. The Asian record is the official page's listing. The first gold in the event at the Asian Games is what Sportstar and PTI say.
