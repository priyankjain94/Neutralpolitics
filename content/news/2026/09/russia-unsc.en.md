Russia has backed India's bid for a permanent seat on the UN Security Council. Speaking at the UN General Assembly on Saturday, Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov said Moscow supports the candidatures of India and Brazil and wants greater representation for Asia, Africa and Latin America.

He opposed additional permanent seats for Western countries, singling out Germany and Japan.

India has long sought a permanent seat as part of a wider reform of the 15-member Council. Five permanent members hold veto power. Lavrov's remarks, as reported in the account used here, were an expression of support. They do not change the Council's membership.
