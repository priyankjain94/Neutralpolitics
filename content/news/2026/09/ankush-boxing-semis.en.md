Indian boxer Ankush Panghal assured India at least a bronze medal at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games by reaching the semi-finals. On Sunday he beat Tajikistan's Nekruz Salimov by a unanimous 5-0 decision in the men's 80kg quarterfinal at the Games, controlling all three rounds.

In Asian Games boxing, both boxers who lose in the semi-finals receive bronze. A quarterfinal win therefore guarantees a medal. Panghal's semi-final was due later in the competition. The result does not fix the colour of the medal beyond the guaranteed bronze.
