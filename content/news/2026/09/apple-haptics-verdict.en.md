A US federal jury in San Diego has found that Apple owes more than $5.7 billion to San Diego-based Taction Technology for infringing two patents on haptic, or vibration, technology.

The jury said Apple's Taptic Engine, used in iPhones and Apple Watches, infringed the patents. It found the infringement was not wilful. Taction sued Apple in 2021. Reuters described the award as the largest such verdict in the United States to date.

Apple said its Taptic Engine is "fundamentally different" from Taction's technology and that it will appeal. The verdict is a jury finding. An appeal, if filed, would be a later step. This report does not treat the sum as a final payment.
