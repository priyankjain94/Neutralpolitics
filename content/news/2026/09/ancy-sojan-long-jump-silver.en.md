Ancy Sojan won silver in the women's long jump at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday. The national record holder led from the first round. Her best jump was 6.53 metres, on her fifth attempt.

China's Huang Yingying jumped 6.55 metres in the final round and took the gold, a margin of two centimetres. Sojan's last attempt measured 6.49 metres. China's Tan Mengyi took bronze with 6.46 metres. India's Shaili Singh finished fourth with 6.42 metres.
