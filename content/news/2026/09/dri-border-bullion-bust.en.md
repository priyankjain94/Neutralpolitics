The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence in Mumbai says it has broken an organised gold-smuggling syndicate in an action it calls Operation Border Bullion Bust.

According to the DRI, the network allegedly moved about 318 kilograms of foreign-origin gold, worth about ₹475 crore, through a courier network across India in the first nine months of 2026. The DRI said the gold entered via the India-Bangladesh border.

The DRI said 11 people, including an alleged mastermind, were arrested in Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Ahmedabad and Guwahati. It said 6.61 kilograms of gold bars, worth about ₹10.36 crore, were seized under the Customs Act. The quantities, the route and the roles described here are the agency's account. They are allegations until a court decides.
