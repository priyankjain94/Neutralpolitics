In the 138th edition of Mann Ki Baat on Sunday, Prime Minister Narendra Modi marked the approaching tenth anniversary of the 2016 surgical strikes across the Line of Control. Those strikes followed the attack at Uri.

Modi said the operation showed the world a new example of India's counter-terrorism policy, that India follows zero tolerance, and that terrorism would meet a strong and decisive response. He also referred to Balakot and to Operation Sindoor.

In the same address he urged people to take part in the ongoing Census, and spoke about anti-drug campaigns among young people and about cleanliness ahead of 2 October. This report records what he said in the broadcast. It does not assess the operations he named.
