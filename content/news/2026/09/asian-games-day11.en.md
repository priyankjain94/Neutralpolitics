India's women's 4x400m relay won gold on Day 11 of the Asian Games, and the medal tally moved past 50. Day 11 was Tuesday, 29 September. The team was Kiran Pahal, Poovamma Machettira, Prachi and Vithya Ramraj. Their time was 3:29.69. Bahrain took silver. China took bronze. The reports call this India's first athletics gold of these Games.

The same accounts call the relay India's sixth gold overall. On Tuesday night India was 10th on the medal table, with six golds. That rank and that gold count are what the Tuesday-night table carried. They are not a count taken after Wednesday morning's archery final.

Gulveer Singh added a bronze in the 5000m. The reports describe it as his third medal of these Games. The earlier two were silvers, in the 10,000m and the 1,500m. This wrap states the bronze and that place in his own tally. It does not add a time for the 5000m.

Neeru Dhanda won the women's trap individual gold on the same competition day. That is the one line this daily report carries. Her score, and any claim of an Asian record, are not here. Outlets did not agree on that tally wording, and the trap gold already has its own report.

The all-medal total is the point on which the outlets do not agree. Counts in the material used here run from 51 to 53. One of those counts still had a boxing bronze pending. Because the figures differ, this report does not choose one. It says the tally is past 50, and more than 50. It does not say 51, and it does not say 53. Rank and golds are stated. The full split into silver and bronze is not.

A line that Day 11 itself produced eight medals was set aside in the account this report follows, even where two papers agreed, and it is not used here. Nor is a claim that the relay is India's first and only track-and-field gold, or that the relay has dominated six of the last seven editions. Those sentences were one outlet's wording and conflicted with another description of the title. Prachi is named by her first name only. Surname spellings in the reports do not match.

Wednesday morning, which is Day 12, brought a separate result. India's women's compound archery team beat China for gold. The archers named with that result are Chikitha Taniparthi, Prithika Pradeep and Jyothi Surekha Vennam. This daily wrap does not give the score, and it does not say whether a record was equalled. That match has its own report. A seventh-gold count after the archery was also left out of this wrap: when it was checked, the tally pages had not yet moved off six golds.

## What's next

Day 11, as reported, is the relay gold, Gulveer's 5000m bronze, Neeru Dhanda's trap gold, and a Tuesday-night table of 10th place and six golds, with the medal count past 50. The compound final is the Wednesday-morning update, and it is not folded into the Tuesday total.
