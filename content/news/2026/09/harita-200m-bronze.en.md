Harita Bhadra won bronze in the women's 200 metres at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday. She clocked 23.20 seconds and finished 0.01 seconds ahead of Japan's Abigail Fuka Ido.

Singapore's Shanti Pereira retained the title in 22.78 seconds. China's Chen Yujie took silver in 22.93 seconds. India's Unnathi Aiyappa Bolland finished sixth in 23.46 seconds.

Sportstar reported that Bhadra is only the sixth Indian woman to win an Asian Games medal in the 200 metres, after Roshan Mistry, Christine Brown, P.T. Usha, Rachita Mistry and Dutee Chand.
