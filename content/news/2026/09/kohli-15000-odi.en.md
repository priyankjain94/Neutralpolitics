Virat Kohli on Sunday became only the second men's batter after Sachin Tendulkar to complete 15,000 runs in one-day internationals. He reached the mark during India's chase of 296 against West Indies in the first ODI at the Greenfield Stadium in Thiruvananthapuram.

Moneycontrol and ESPNcricinfo reported that Kohli got there in his 303rd innings, 74 innings faster than Tendulkar, who took 377. Hindustan Times' live coverage marked the milestone as India pressed the chase. The crowd applauded, and Kohli raised his bat with captain Shubman Gill.

In the same innings Gill scored his tenth ODI hundred. ESPNcricinfo's commentary called him the fastest Indian to that mark. Tendulkar finished his career with 18,426 ODI runs. Kohli already leads the format's list of centuries, with 54. This report is about the milestone during the chase. It does not give the match result, which was not the subject of the material used here.
