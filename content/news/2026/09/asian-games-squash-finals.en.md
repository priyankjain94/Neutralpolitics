Abhay Singh and Anahat Singh won silver in the squash singles at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday. Both lost their finals to Malaysian opponents at Nagoya Kinjo Futo Arena.

World No. 26 Abhay Singh lost 9-11, 5-11, 5-11 to defending champion Ng Eain Yow. Anahat Singh, 18, lost 4-11, 4-11, 7-11 to world No. 5 Sivasangari Subramaniam. She is the first Indian woman to reach an Asian Games squash singles final.

A day earlier, Abhay had beaten Pakistan's Muhammad Ashab Irfan 3-0, with games of 11-3, 12-10 and 11-6, to become the second Indian man after Saurav Ghosal to reach the men's singles final. Anahat had come back from behind to beat Japan's Satomi Watanabe 3-2. India's squash team events were scheduled to begin on Monday.
