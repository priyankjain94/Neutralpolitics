Sawan Barwal won silver in the men's marathon at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Saturday. His time was 2:11:37, a national record. Japan's Ichitaka Yamashita took gold in 2:10:49.

It is India's first Asian Games marathon medal in 44 years, since 1982, and the country's first such medal won outside India.

Barwal switched to road racing only about a year ago. Earlier in 2026 he had already broken Shivnath Singh's national record from 1978.
