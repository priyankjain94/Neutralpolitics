The US Federal Bureau of Investigation has added Satinderjeet Singh, known as Goldy Brar, to its Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list. The FBI is offering a reward of up to $1 million for information leading to his arrest. The Indian Express, CBC News and The Times of India reported the listing. The desk account of the FBI wanted page says he was added on 28 September.

The FBI wanted page lists what he is wanted for. The charges are Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations (RICO) conspiracy; conspiracy to interfere and attempted interference with commerce by extortion; and conspiracy to distribute and possess with intent to distribute controlled substances. These are charges. They are not a finding of guilt.

The same page alleges that he leads the Bishnoi group's operations in North America. That is the FBI's allegation. A federal arrest warrant was issued on 1 July 2026. The FBI page in this file was first published on 6 July 2026 at 08:18 UTC.

The Indian Express, CBC and The Times of India also report that he was charged in July in connection with the killing of Hardeep Singh Nijjar in 2023. CBC's time on the file is 28 September, 15:05 EDT. That report, like the others, is about a charge. It is not a conviction.

He has not been convicted in these cases. The reward, the warrant and the place on the Ten Most Wanted list describe the FBI's search. They do not decide the allegations.

## What's next

The FBI page asks for information that could lead to an arrest and sets the reward at up to $1 million. No arrest, and no court outcome, is in these reports.
