At least 56 people have died and 46 have been injured in Uttar Pradesh after two or three days of exceptionally heavy rain, according to state Relief Commissioner data reported on Sunday.

Sitapur recorded the most deaths, seven. Lakhimpur Kheri, Bahraich and Balrampur had five each. Ayodhya and Gorakhpur had four each. The data also said 1,021 houses were damaged and 107 animals died.

Flash-flood alerts and warnings of further rain were issued. Assessment of crop loss was under way. Officials said the toll may rise. The figures are those reported from the Relief Commissioner. They are not described as a final count.
