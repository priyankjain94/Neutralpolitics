Police in Namsai, Arunachal Pradesh, said on Sunday that 51 people — 47 children and four adults — have been traced or rescued in what they describe as an alleged child-trafficking and illegal-placement racket.

They said they arrested Ema Loyi of Kaba village, the fifth alleged facilitator. The case was registered on 17 August after a complaint from the District Child Welfare Committee. Police said the number rose from 19 to 38 and then to 51 during operations in more than one district and in Assam.

Police said families were lured with false promises of education, and that many of the children were placed as domestic help or as hotel workers. They said alleged payments of ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 per child are under investigation. Those payments, the promises and the roles of those arrested remain allegations unless a court finds otherwise. This report does not name the children.
