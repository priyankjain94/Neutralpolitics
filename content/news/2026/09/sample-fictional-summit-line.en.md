Nothing in this story took place. There is no Isle of Lamps and no Port of Quiet, and they did not meet.

The sample says their delegates stood up at a round table and one of them said, "The lamps stay lit while we talk." The other said that was a courtesy, not a promise. The Sample Herald and the Example Tribune both carried those two sentences, which were written here and nowhere else.

The point of the page is the layout: a world kicker, two short columns for what each side says, and links a reader can see are examples.
