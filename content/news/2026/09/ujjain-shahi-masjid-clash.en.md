Tension rose outside Ujjain's Shahi Masjid on Monday morning, 28 September 2026, as municipal teams moved to clear portions of the mosque that stand in the line of a widened road. The Indian Express, ABP Live and The Statesman reported stone-pelting and the use of tear gas near Gopal Mandir and Chatri Chowk. The work is a partial removal of the sections in the road alignment. It is not a demolition of the whole mosque.

The civic plan widens about 900 metres of the Kanthal–Gopal Mandir stretch to 15 metres, ahead of Simhastha 2028. ABP reported two portions of about 11 feet each in that alignment. The mosque is described as Waqf property near Mahakaleshwar and the Kshipra. Residents had sat in overnight. On Sunday night the crowd held JCB machines back. Reports said an understanding that the portions would be removed by the mosque side itself broke down when work resumed on Monday.

Protesters allegedly threw stones. Police fired tear gas. The Statesman reported more than a dozen shells. NewsDrum reported more than 18. Heavy security, including the Rapid Action Force, stayed deployed. Earlier on Sunday, more than 600 police, with RAF and STF units, had joined a flag march, Free Press Journal reported, while Superintendent Pradeep Sharma said demolition was on hold and talks were continuing. Later accounts of the force on Monday ranged from the hundreds to about 2,000. Those figures are what different reports gave; this article does not pick one.

Crime Branch inspector Pushpa Panchal was injured on the hand while moving women protesters, The Statesman and NewsDrum reported. Police say the women protesters were not injured. Congress MP Imran Pratapgarhi criticised the action. That is his statement. AIMIM leader Asaduddin Owaisi had called the earlier sit-in a Gandhian protest and linked it to concerns about the Waqf law. That, too, is his view.

A single judge of the Madhya Pradesh High Court at Indore, Justice Sandeep N. Bhatt, had dismissed petitions against the civic notice around 9 September. The mosque committee's appeal was listed for Monday. By Monday evening, News18 and Free Press Journal reported that the division bench at Indore disposed of the appeal after noting a settlement between the mosque side and the Ujjain Municipal Corporation, and made the corporation's assurances binding. The reports do not set out the full text of those assurances.
