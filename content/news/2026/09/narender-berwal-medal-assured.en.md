Narender Berwal assured India a third boxing medal at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday. In the men's +90kg quarterfinal at Nishio Gymnasium he beat Thailand's Adthapong Saengtep after the referee stopped the contest in the first round.

Times of India reported that the bout was stopped at 1 minute 37 seconds, after two knockdowns. Hindustan Times and Olympics.com described a stoppage inside about two minutes. The reports agree on an early referee stoppage. They do not all use the same clock time.

Berwal goes to the semi-finals with at least a bronze guaranteed, because losing semi-finalists in Asian Games boxing receive bronze. Reports said a possible later opponent is Kazakhstan's world champion Aibek Oralbay. That is a possible path, not a confirmed draw in the material used here. Earlier the same day Ankush Panghal in the 80kg and Parveen Hooda in the 65kg had also assured medals.
