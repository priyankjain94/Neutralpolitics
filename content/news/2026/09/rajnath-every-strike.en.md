Defence Minister Rajnath Singh told the NDTV Defence Conclave on Sunday that "as long as our neighbour continues the state policy of terrorism, India is completely ready for every kind of strike against terrorism." The account of the speech says he was referring to Pakistan.

He said "India believes in peace" and "never wants war" or foreign land, but will not compromise on security. Dialogue, he said, "ends right there" if a country shelters terrorism against India.

The remarks followed recent exchanges between India and Pakistan at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation and at the UN General Assembly. This report records the minister's words. It does not describe a new military order.
