This account is developing. On 30 September 2026, flydubai flight FZ1073, from Dubai to Tel Aviv, diverted to Tabuk in Saudi Arabia. flydubai, Saudi Gazette, AP, The National and the BBC report the diversion. The landing time in the notes is 09:45 local time. The flight landed in Saudi Arabia. It did not land in Israel.

What flydubai itself says is narrower than the claim that spread with the video posts. The airline says an altercation occurred in the flight deck. It says the aircraft was secured by on-duty flydubai crew who were travelling on the flight. It says all passengers and crew are safe and accounted for. The BBC and The National carry that statement. flydubai also says the underlying reasons and motives are unknown and are subject to a formal investigation, and it asks people not to speculate. That is the airline's position on why it happened. A motive is not confirmed.

After landing, the Tabuk airport operator, quoted by Saudi Gazette, says the captain and co-pilot were injured and taken to hospital. A later Saudi Gazette report says the passengers left on a replacement aircraft. AP and The National also report that the passengers flew on to Tel Aviv on another plane. The notes identify that aircraft as another flydubai flight. "Injured and taken to hospital" is the airport operator's line. A more precise medical condition is not confirmed here, because the accounts of how badly the captain was hurt do not agree and are not from the airline.

The stabbing and the crash attempt are Israeli accounts, and they stay labelled as claims. AP reports Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu saying that one pilot stabbed the other and "apparently tried to crash the plane". The National, the BBC and ABC carry that reported remark. Israeli officials told Reuters they see a possible terror attack. The BBC also attributes that kind of assessment to Defence Minister Katz. "Possible" and "apparently" are the words in those reports. They are not a finding that a terror attack occurred, and they are not flydubai's wording. This report does not use stronger labels that appear only as slogans in some coverage.

What is not confirmed is a short list, and it is the list that matters. flydubai has not given the pilots' names. The National reports the airline saying it cannot disclose nationalities. Other reports that name a country are, in the notes checked, downstream of Israeli sources. They are not confirmed by flydubai or by Saudi, Emirati or other authorities in the material used here, so no nationality is stated. Netanyahu, in the AP account, referred to an arrest. No Saudi confirmation of that arrest was found in the reports checked. Which pilot is alleged to have stabbed the other is also not settled in a way this report will repeat: some Israeli-official accounts specify a co-pilot and a captain, and other copy says only "one pilot". The conflict is the reason neither version is adopted.

A line that one hero saved everyone overstates what the sources say. AP says passengers and crew intervened. flydubai credits the on-duty crew who were on board as passengers. No individual is named for that. The descent, too, is not a single figure. AP, citing Flightradar24, describes a drop from about 33,000 feet to 17,000 feet in about a minute. Other outlets give other altitudes and other times. Because those numbers do not match, this report does not pick one.

## What's next

The status remains developing. Confirmed, on the airline and airport statements, is a flight-deck altercation, a diversion to Tabuk, injuries to the two pilots, a safe count of people on board, and a onward flight to Tel Aviv. The stabbing, the crash attempt and a possible terror attack are Israeli officials' claims. Motive, names, nationalities and the reported arrest are not confirmed.
