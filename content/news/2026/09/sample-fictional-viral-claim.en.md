This fact-check is a sample. It does not check a claim that anyone made.

A fictional post said blue cows walked through Example District before noon on 28 September 2026. The Sample Herald looked for a district by that name and did not find one. The Example Tribune did the same. There is no footage, because there was no event.

We would not publish a verdict on a real claim with only that. Here the verdict is Unverified so the label, the claim and the evidence list can be reviewed on the page. It is not a finding about the world.
