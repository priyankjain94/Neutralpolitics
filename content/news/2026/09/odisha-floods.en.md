The Odisha government evacuated more than 70,000 people from low-lying areas on Saturday and sheltered them in 395 relief camps as rivers rose after days of heavy rain, officials said.

Flash floods and waterlogging affected 13 districts. Balasore was among the worst hit. The Subarnarekha reached 11.07 metres at Jaleswar, above its danger level of 10.36 metres. Sixteen gates of the Hirakud Dam were opened.

Thirty-three ODRAF teams, three NDRF teams and about 300 Fire Services teams were deployed. Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi was conducting an aerial survey of Ganjam, Koraput and Nabarangpur on Sunday.
