Unverified social-media claims about a female student at Lovely Professional University in Phagwara were denied by police. Kapurthala police said they had received no such complaint and had found no evidence. They said a suicide near the campus four or five days earlier is not linked to the claims.

The university's registrar called the claims "false, baseless and fabricated."

About 2,000 students protested from around 2 am on Sunday. Police said protesters damaged property, set fire to property and blocked the Jalandhar–Phagwara highway. The highway was later cleared. Talks with students were continuing when this account was published.

Neutral Politics is not repeating the content of the unverified posts. On the material here, the claim itself is unverified: police report no complaint and no evidence, and the university denies it.
