France is the country with the most time zones once its overseas territories are counted, according to the account published with this item. The span is 12 zones, from UTC−10 in French Polynesia to UTC+12 in Wallis and Futuna.

On the same account, the United States and Russia have 11 time zones each. Midday in Paris is therefore a different hour for French citizens in those territories.
