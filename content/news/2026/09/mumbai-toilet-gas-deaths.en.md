Prabhakar Dhanivar, 24, and Bhavin Desai, 18, died after they were allegedly exposed to a strong chemical smell inside a public toilet near Parsi Panchayat Gate by Mrunaltai Bridge, Ram Mandir (East), in Mumbai.

They were taken to Trauma Care Hospital around midnight and declared dead at 3:49 am on Sunday. Vanrai police registered an accidental death report. The post-mortem was still awaited when this account was published.

Police said that, prima facie, gas from a nearby small-scale industrial unit may have been involved. They also said the cause is not confirmed. This report treats the gas link as a police assessment, not a finding.
