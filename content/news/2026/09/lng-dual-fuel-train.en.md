Amit Shah on Sunday flagged off India's first LNG-diesel dual-fuel DEMU train from Sabarmati and launched railway projects worth more than ₹1,542 crore.

Two 1,400 HP Driving Power Cars at the Sabarmati Integrated Coaching Depot were converted so that liquefied natural gas can replace about 40 percent of the diesel they use. The eight-coach train, which is not air-conditioned, ran from Sabarmati to Mahesana. Its regular route had not been finalised.

The projects launched the same day included a ₹700 crore shed for WAG-12 locomotives, Kavach on 404 kilometres, automatic signalling on 122.57 kilometres, and doubling of the Sabarmati–Sarkhej line.
