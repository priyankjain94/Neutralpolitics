External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar told the 81st UN General Assembly on Saturday that India's right to defend itself against terrorism "will be exercised", according to the account published with this item.

Without naming Pakistan, he said "a serial practitioner of terrorism" had misrepresented facts before the Assembly the previous day. The account treats that as a reference to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's address. He said governments backing cross-border terrorism must be held accountable.

He also said countries far from conflicts were being penalised, urged restraint in the Gulf and protection for seafarers, and said India supports a two-state solution in West Asia. No separate source URL for the speech was on file.
