A video from a road inspection in west Delhi on Sunday, 27 September 2026, shows Deputy Chief Minister and PWD Minister Parvesh Sahib Singh Verma pushing down a phone and slapping the man who was filming. He is Saheb Singh, also spelled Sahib Singh, 22, who handles social media for AAP MLA Jarnail Singh of Tilak Nagar. The inspection was on a newly laid stretch of the Vikaspuri Ring Road, also called the Outer Ring Road.

On the video, Jarnail Singh peels pieces of the road surface off by hand. Verma then pushes the phone down and slaps Saheb Singh. People nearby step in. Hindustan Times, The Times of India and Outlook described that sequence.

Verma has not denied the slap. He says he reacted in the heat of the moment after one of the MLA's workers allegedly abused his family, and that the circulating clip lacks context. Delhi BJP called the incident wrong and unfortunate, expressed regret, and alleged provocation. Those are allegations about what was said before the slap.

AAP says the new road is corrupt and wants Verma sacked and arrested. Arvind Kejriwal visited the site on 28 September. Verma alleges the MLA sought a commission. Complaints from a contractor and from PWD personnel allege that Jarnail Singh tried to stop the work on 24 September and demanded ₹50 lakh. The MLA and AAP deny the abuse allegation and the commission allegation. Verma ordered an independent audit, including core-cutting. As of 7 pm on 28 September, no result had been reported. Both claims are unproven while the audit and the police inquiry are pending.

The Times of India, with ANI inputs, reported five complaints and no FIR. One is from Saheb Singh, two are from PWD personnel against Jarnail Singh and others, and two are from BJP workers. They were filed at Vikaspuri and Tilak Nagar and are under investigation. Police also say Saheb Singh was arrested in June in a case of assaulting an MCD worker and is on bail. Hindustan Times notes that an FIR copy shared by an officer did not name him.

On 27 September, police detained Saurabh Bharadwaj, Jarnail Singh and MLA Kuldeep Kumar at an AAP protest, Hindustan Times reported. Rahul Gandhi linked the episode to his "vote chori" campaign. That is his comment. Neither side's allegation is treated here as established.
