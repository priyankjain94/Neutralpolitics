The death toll from Saturday's suspected militant gun attack in Manipur's Senapati district rose to three on Sunday. Ashiho Sani, 49, who had been injured in the attack, died at a private hospital at about 3 am.

Two Naga civilians were killed at the spot on Saturday. The other injured man, Adani Manio, 33, remained under treatment.

The attack came shortly before the United Naga Council announced that it was suspending its economic blockade of highways. The killings drew condemnation from political leaders. Police described the firing as a suspected militant attack. This report does not name an organisation as responsible, because the account on file does not.
