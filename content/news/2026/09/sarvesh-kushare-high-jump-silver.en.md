Sarvesh Kushare won silver in the men's high jump at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday, clearing 2.25 metres. Chinese Taipei's Fu Chao-hsuan also cleared 2.25 metres and took the gold on countback. South Korea's Woo Sang-hyeok won bronze at 2.22 metres. Sportstar and the Times of India reported the countback. Inshorts carried the result, credited to Asian Games results.

Kushare cleared every height from 2.10 metres through 2.25 metres, then missed 2.28 metres three times.

The medal ends a 56-year wait for India in the men's high jump at the Asian Games. Bhim Singh's bronze in 1970, at 2.03 metres, was the previous one. Sportstar said Kushare is the third Indian man on the podium, after Ajit Singh in 1954 and Bhim Singh in 1966 and 1970.
