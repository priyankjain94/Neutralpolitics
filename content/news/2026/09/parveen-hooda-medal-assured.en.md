Parveen Hooda won her women's 65kg quarterfinal by a unanimous 5-0 decision against Uzbekistan's Navbakhor Khamidova at Nishio Gymnasium on Sunday. Khamidova was the silver medallist at the 2025 World Championships. It was Hooda's first bout at the Aichi-Nagoya Games after a walkover in the previous round.

She is in the semi-finals, so at least a bronze is assured under the rule that both losing semi-finalists receive bronze. Ankush Panghal had assured India's first boxing medal of the day earlier on Sunday, in the men's 80kg.

Hooda's bronze from Hangzhou 2022 was later taken away after a World Anti-Doping Agency whereabouts suspension. This report records that history as reported. It does not restate the doping case.
