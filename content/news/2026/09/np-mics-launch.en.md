NP Mics is a people-powered media network by Neutral Politics. The account says contributors across India report, ask, explain and verify the issues that matter.

The caption sets out five formats. NP Ground is field reports. NP Janta is citizen voices. NP Explained is current-affairs explainers. NP Interview is questions to politicians, experts and key voices. NP Check is verifying viral claims.

Selected stories may be published, credited and paid. Every story is verified under the Neutral Politics 2-source rule. The caption puts it as: many Mics, one standard.

To become a contributor, the post says, DM "MIC" to @theneutralpolitics on Instagram.

No outside source URL is on file for this announcement. That is why it stays a draft and is not on the public pages, the sitemap, RSS or search.
