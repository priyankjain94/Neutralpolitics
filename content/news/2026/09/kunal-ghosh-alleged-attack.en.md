Trinamool Congress MLA Kunal Ghosh alleged that a group on motorbikes attacked his car in Kolkata's Phoolbagan area on Sunday morning. He said the riders surrounded the car as he was going to a programme, smashed a window and tried to drag him out.

Ghosh said his security personnel drew weapons and that he took shelter at Narkeldanga police station, where he began filing a complaint. He said this followed an alleged attack on his car in Uttarpara a day earlier.

He blamed the Bharatiya Janata Party. The BJP denied involvement and pointed instead to infighting inside the Trinamool Congress. Police said an investigation is under way, including a review of CCTV. Both the attack and the political blame are allegations. This report does not treat either side's account as established.
