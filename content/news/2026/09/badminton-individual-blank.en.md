India's individual badminton campaign at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games ended on Sunday without a medal, the first time that has happened in 12 years.

In the women's singles quarterfinals, PV Sindhu lost to China's Chen Yu Fei 21-11, 18-21, 10-21. Unnati Hooda lost to Japan's Akane Yamaguchi 16-21, 21-14, 17-21. The women's doubles pair Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand, and the mixed doubles pair Kapila and Crasto, also went out in the quarterfinals.

India's only badminton medal at these Games is the bronze won by the men's team. At Hangzhou in 2022, India won three badminton medals.
