On a wet Saturday evening in Nagoya, Tajinderpal Singh Toor won shot put silver with 20.64 metres on his fifth attempt. It was not a third straight Asian Games gold. Iran's Mohammadreza Tayebise won the gold with a Games-record 20.81 metres.

In the women's 400 metres, Prachi Choudhary took bronze in 53.21 seconds. Bahrain's Salwa Eid Naser won gold in a Games-record 49.20 seconds. Iran's Zahra Zarei took silver in 52.38 seconds.
