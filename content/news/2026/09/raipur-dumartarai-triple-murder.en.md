Police in Raipur said a 36-year-old man, Bhuneshwar Sahu, originally from Balod, allegedly killed his 32-year-old wife and their two minor sons with a knife late on Saturday night. The house was a rented one in Dumartarai, under Mana police station.

Police said the boys died at the spot and the woman was declared dead at hospital. They said Sahu then injured himself. He is in custody and under treatment at Dr B.R. Ambedkar Memorial Hospital, also known as Mekahara.

Police said preliminary inquiries point to marital suspicion. The investigation was continuing. The deaths are alleged. A court has not, on the material here, recorded a finding.
