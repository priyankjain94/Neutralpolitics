The Bombay High Court on 30 September 2026 stayed, for the time being, a bail order for Sachin Waze. The order it stayed is the special court's, dated 29 September. The National Investigation Agency had challenged that bail. LiveLaw, Moneycontrol carrying PTI, India TV and Bar and Bench report the stay. Moneycontrol, citing PTI, places the special-court order on Tuesday, which is 29 September. India TV states 29 September. This article is about the stay. It is not a separate story of the grant, and it is not a final cancellation of bail.

The bench was Justices Girish Kulkarni and Neela Gokhale. LiveLaw, Moneycontrol via PTI and India TV name both judges. Bar and Bench describes the hearing as before a Justice Kulkarni-led bench. Notice was issued to Waze. The NIA appeal is listed for 7 October 2026 in Moneycontrol's PTI copy, in India TV and in Bar and Bench. LiveLaw places the listing in the first week of October, which is the same week as 7 October and does not give that date. The hearing of the appeal is ahead. It has not happened.

The stay is interim, and the words the reports use say so. LiveLaw says the bail is "for the time being stayed". Moneycontrol via PTI and India TV call it an ad-interim stay. An ad-interim stay holds the special-court order while the appeal waits. It does not, in these reports, cancel bail for good, and it does not record that the High Court accepted the NIA's case on the merits. Those further steps are not in the order as these four outlets describe it.

Waze is not out. Moneycontrol via PTI and India TV say he remains in Thane Central Prison, and that the formalities of release had not been completed when the stay came. Bar and Bench, Moneycontrol via PTI and India TV report that the jail authorities are to be informed of the High Court order immediately, so that he is not released. Bar and Bench also carries a line that he will be released from Thane Central Prison. That line is a submission about what would have followed the special-court order. It is not the effect of the stay. The effect reported by Moneycontrol and India TV is that he stays in that prison.

Who he is, in these reports, stays inside the language of an accusation. LiveLaw, Moneycontrol via PTI and Bar and Bench describe him as a dismissed police officer and as an accused in the 2021 Antilia bomb-scare case and in the murder of Mansukh Hiran. "Accused" is the status this report uses. The stay order does not convict him. It does not acquit him. It does not drop any charge. Nothing in the four reports says the High Court decided those charges.

The NIA's argument, and the special court's reasons, are not this order. Moneycontrol reports the NIA saying the special court's order was totally wrong, and saying Waze intended to create terror. Those are the agency's claims in the appeal. They are not findings. They are not used as facts. The special court's own reasoning for bail, including a line that there was no prima facie case under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, a period of custody, and a remark about a muhurat, is not needed to state the stay and is not repeated. A bail in a separate corruption case appears in India TV alone. The date of an NIA arrest in March 2021 appears in that paper alone. Which earlier bench was unavailable, and a Bar and Bench line that Waze was not represented, are not in the agreed account. None of those is stated.

## What's next

The NIA appeal is listed for 7 October 2026, or, in LiveLaw's wording, the first week of October. Until that appeal is decided, the special court's bail order of 29 September stands stayed on an ad-interim order. Sachin Waze remains an accused, in Thane Central Prison, and this stay is not a conviction and not a final refusal of bail.
