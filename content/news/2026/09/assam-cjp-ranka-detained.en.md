Assam Police on Sunday detained Cockroach Janta Party co-convener Ashutosh Ranka and several associates, before the party's first regional volunteers' meeting in Guwahati.

Party founder Abhijeet Dipke said the team was taken to a location that was not disclosed, and alleged that lawyers were denied entry. Ranka alleged that Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma ordered the detentions because the party planned to highlight the condition of schools and issues in Kaziranga.

The party also alleged that roads to the venue were barricaded. It demanded the immediate release of those detained. These are the party's allegations. The account on file does not include a police statement explaining the detentions.
