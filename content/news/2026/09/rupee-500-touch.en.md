The current new-series ₹500 note carries marks that can be read by touch.

Five slanted lines on the left edge and five on the right edge are raised. There is also a raised circle with ₹500 printed in it, so a visually impaired person can identify the note without seeing it. The portrait of Gandhi and the Ashoka Pillar emblem are raised as well.

The account used here says these features apply to the current new-series ₹500 note only. It cites the Reserve Bank of India and Paisa Bolta Hai. No separate URL was in the file.
