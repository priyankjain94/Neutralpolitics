The Indian Air Force's multinational exercise Tarang Shakti began at Jodhpur Air Force Station on Saturday and will run until 12 October. The dates given with the exercise are 26 September to 12 October. This is the second edition, after 2024.

About 40 countries are involved. Seven foreign air forces are flying their aircraft alongside the IAF, and 32 nations are attending as observers. Around 80 aircraft, Indian and foreign, and about 1,500 military personnel are taking part.

Six US F-35 stealth fighters are flying in an exercise in India for the first time. US Ambassador Sergio Gor said six F-35 Lightning IIs and more than 200 US airmen are in Jodhpur for their first Tarang Shakti. French Rafales and UAE F-16s are also in the flying contingent, according to the account published with the exercise.
