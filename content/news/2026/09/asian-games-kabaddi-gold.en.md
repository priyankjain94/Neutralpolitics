India's men's and women's kabaddi teams both beat Iran on Saturday and won gold at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, retaining their titles, according to the account published with this story.

The men won 40-34. The women won 37-34. The same account says 17-year-old Nikita Kumari impressed on her senior debut.

It also said India won seven medals that day, naming Sawan Barwal's marathon silver in a national-record time and Tajinderpal Singh Toor's shot put silver, and that the overall tally reached 30, putting India back in the top 10. Those other results are also reported in separate items where source material exists. No separate source URL for the kabaddi finals was on file here.
