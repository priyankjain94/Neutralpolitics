The Sensex fell 1,124.02 points, or 1.52 per cent, on Monday, 28 September, and closed at 72,771.72. That was its lowest close since 30 March. The Nifty 50 fell 360.25 points, or 1.56 per cent, to 22,780.25.

All sectoral indices ended lower. PSU banks were down by more than 3 per cent.

The market reports cited with these figures pointed to Brent crude above $108, uncertainty over talks between the United States and Iran on the Strait of Hormuz, selling by foreign institutional investors, and the rupee's move to 96.03. Those are the reasons given in the note. They are not a forecast.
