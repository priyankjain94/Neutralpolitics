A 63-year-old temple priest was allegedly assaulted inside a Shiva temple in Jaipur on Friday, 25 September 2026, after an argument over the volume of devotional music. Pandit Ramkrishna is the priest of the temple at Ganesh Nagar Main, Niwaru Road, in the area of Kardhani police station. The time given by The Times of India, Bhaskar, Free Press Journal, Aaj Tak and India Today is about 7:45 pm, during evening worship. The temple CCTV stamp reads 2026-09-25 19:48.

Every account says a neighbour living opposite the temple objected to the speaker and the volume of the music. News18 Hindi reports that the priest says he was teaching children a recitation and was not using a microphone. That is his account, and it is contested.

The CCTV, as described by the reports, shows a young man knocking the priest to the ground and punching and kicking him. The priest's clothes were torn before others intervened. India Today notes that in the same footage the priest is seen pushing the young man first. This article does not go beyond that description of the recording.

The priest complained on Saturday, 26 September. Station house officer Sawai Singh said Harish Chaturvedi and Devansh were arrested for disturbing the peace, according to Bhaskar, The Times of India and Free Press Journal. India Today and Aaj Tak describe the same two men as detained, and as father and son. The priest's complaint also names Ritu, of the same family. News18 Hindi reported that the case is under sections 115(2), 126(2) and 3(5) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, and that assistant sub-inspector Suresh Kumar is on the inquiry.

The family of the accused has filed a cross-complaint. Bhaskar, Free Press Journal and NewsX say police are examining both sides. On Sunday, 27 September, the priest's family and local residents protested outside Kardhani police station and asked for stricter action. Until a court decides, the assault remains an allegation, and so does the account of who pushed first.
