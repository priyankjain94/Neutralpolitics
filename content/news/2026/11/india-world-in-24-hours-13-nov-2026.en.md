This roundup is verified. The carousel was posted at 9:07 pm IST on 13 November 2026. The package was current as of 7:47 pm IST. Eight stories follow, each from the posted caption and that item's notes. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. Photographs on the story slides are file or representative images. They are not from these events.

## Kaziranga

On NH-715 near Kaziranga, in Assam's Golaghat district, a minibus and a container truck collided head-on. Officials say at least 23 people died. Many were injured and were taken to hospitals, including in Jorhat. Officials say the toll may rise. Golaghat superintendent Swapnaneel Deka's account is carried by Reuters. District commissioner Biswajit Phukan's account is carried by The Indian Express. An executive magistrate's account is carried by PTI and The Hindu. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma posted at 5:05 pm IST. This page does not state a cause as fact. It names no victim. It gives no injured count and no ex gratia figure. The slide is a 2017 file of the Kaziranga highway, CC BY-SA 4.0, Horshadeep Saikia, Wikimedia Commons, not this crash.

## Bengaluru

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated a 7.5-kilometre elevated stretch of Bengaluru Metro's Pink Line and launched projects of more than Rs 15,600 crore. He also flagged off the Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Madgaon Vande Bharat. The primary posts are from the Press Information Bureau at 2:23 pm IST and from the Prime Minister at 2:42 pm IST. India Today, The Indian Express and Moneycontrol report the openings. This is not the whole Pink Line. The slide is the JP Nagar 4th Phase station under construction in April 2026, CC BY-SA 4.0, Sameer2905, Wikimedia Commons, not the opening.

## Sports awards

President Droupadi Murmu presented the National Sports Awards. Seventeen athletes received the Arjuna Award, including Tejaswin Shankar, Divya Deshmukh and Vidit Gujarathi. There is no Khel Ratna this year. Reports, including PTI as carried by The Hindu and The Indian Express, say this is the first year without one since 2014. That line stays with the reports. The primary text is Press Information Bureau release 2335776, from the President's Secretariat, at 2:07 pm IST on 13 November. The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports awardee list is also a source. No minor is named. The slide is a 2017 file of President Ram Nath Kovind presenting an Arjuna Award, CC BY-SA 3.0, Prashanti Singh, Wikimedia Commons, not this ceremony.

## JEE Advanced

JEE Advanced 2027 will be held on Sunday, 23 May 2027. IIT Delhi will organise it. The official site, jeeadv.ac.in, was fetched and answered. IIT Delhi posted the date at 5:46 pm IST. The slide is the IIT Delhi main building in 2022, CC BY-SA 4.0, TheVyomanaut, Wikimedia Commons.

## C.S. Karnan

Former Madras and Calcutta High Court judge C.S. Karnan died in Chennai at the age of 71. In 2017 the Supreme Court sentenced him to six months in prison for contempt. The Hindu, The Indian Express, Hindustan Times and LiveLaw report the death, and they describe him as the first sitting High Court judge to receive a prison sentence. The wording used here is sentenced to prison. It is not "jailed". The slide is a file of Calcutta High Court, CC BY-SA 4.0, Indrajit Das, Wikimedia Commons.

## Fujairah

A crash of 23 vehicles in Fujairah, in the United Arab Emirates, killed 14 people and injured 30. Those figures are the UAE interior ministry's, posted in Arabic on the evening of 12 November IST, and carried by the official WAM agency. The crash itself was late on 11 November, local time. The figures were checked again at 9 am IST on 13 November. The Indian consulate in Dubai said Indians were among the dead, and did not give a number. The BBC and The Times of India, on 13 November, also report the crash. No one is named. The slide is a file of a road in the Fujairah mountains, CC BY 4.0, Aleksandr Serebrennikov, Wikimedia Commons, not this crash.

## Tuberculosis

The World Health Organization's Global TB Report 2026 says India, in 2025, passed a milestone of a 35 percent reduction in tuberculosis deaths compared with 2015. About a quarter of the world's TB cases are in India. The primary text is the report, dated 12 November, and its PDF, plus a WHO post at 1 am IST on 13 November. Figures of 38 percent and 94 percent, associated with the health ministry, are not used. The slide is a file of WHO headquarters in Geneva, CC BY 2.0, United States Mission Geneva, Wikimedia Commons.

## The fourth ODI

New Zealand beat India by 244 runs in the fourth one-day international. India were all out for 89. Matt Henry took 6 for 27. India still lead the series 2-1. The last match is on Sunday. The Black Caps posted at 2:22 pm IST. The ICC posted at 1:59 pm IST. Cricbuzz, The Indian Express and Business Standard also report the result. This page does not call the margin the biggest of its kind. The slide is a 2024 file of Bay Oval, CC BY 4.0, New Zealand Defence Force, Wikimedia Commons, not this match.
