This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is VERIFIED. The carousel was posted at 1:23 pm IST on 6 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used. It is shown whole.

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla has accepted the resignation of Aga Syed Ruhullah Mehdi from the Srinagar parliamentary seat. Gazette of India notification S.O. 6067(E) states that. The Lok Sabha Secretariat issued the notification on 5 November 2026. It says the resignation has been accepted with effect from 3 November 2026. The Srinagar Lok Sabha seat is vacant. Those are the words of the notification, as this package records them.

The Tribune and Greater Kashmir report the same acceptance and the same vacancy. Gazette Tracker carries the text of S.O. 6067(E). The Gazette PDF is on egazette.gov.in. This page uses that notification. The resignation was accepted. This page does not describe the Speaker's act as a removal.

Mehdi had publicly announced on 3 November that he was quitting the National Conference and resigning his seat. The package places that announcement at Budgam. This note is the formal Speaker acceptance and the Gazette follow-through. It is not a new resignation. The [3 November roundup](/news/2026/11/india-world-in-24-hours-3-nov-2026) recorded the announcement, and said the Speaker's acceptance had not then been reported. This page is that later acceptance. It does not replace that roundup, and it does not reopen the arguments he gave that day.

No bye-election date is set in this Gazette notification. This page does not invent one. It does not state an Election Commission schedule. The reported line stops at a vacant seat.

![Neutral Politics graphic. Typographic card. No photograph. Shown whole.](/media/2026/11/speaker-accepts-ruhullah-mehdi-resignation-srinagar-seat-vacant.webp)

Read together, the reported lines are these. Speaker Om Birla has accepted Aga Syed Ruhullah Mehdi's resignation from the Srinagar Lok Sabha seat. Notification S.O. 6067(E), issued by the Lok Sabha Secretariat on 5 November 2026, makes the acceptance effective from 3 November 2026, and the seat is vacant. The Tribune and Greater Kashmir report that acceptance and that vacancy. The acceptance follows Mehdi's public announcement on 3 November, at Budgam, that he was quitting the National Conference and resigning the seat. It is not a fresh resignation, and it is not a removal. The notification sets no bye-election date.

The sources named on the carousel are the Gazette of India, The Tribune and Greater Kashmir.
