Eight stories follow, each from the posted caption and that item's notes. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. Photographs on the story slides are file or representative images. They are labelled as such.

## Rabi subsidy

The Union Cabinet approved Nutrient Based Subsidy rates for the Rabi season from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027. The tentative sum is about Rs 62,370.02 crore. That is Rs 24,417.73 crore more than Rabi 2025-26. The posted caption rounds those figures to about Rs 62,370 crore and Rs 24,418 crore more. The primary text is Press Information Bureau release 2334972, at 3:11 pm IST on 11 November. BusinessLine has per-kilogram rates. They are not used here. The slide is a file of a farmer spreading fertiliser, CC BY-SA 2.0, kiran kumar, Wikimedia Commons.

## Moody's forecast

Moody's Ratings raised its forecast for India's growth in 2026-27 to 7 percent, from 6.5 percent. The document is the Global Macro Outlook 2027-28. This is a forecast. It is not a rating action. The Times of India quotes Moody's. PTI, carried by The Hindu, also reports the move. The Moody's report itself was not opened, so the figure stays with those accounts. The slide is a file of the Mumbai skyline at night, CC BY-SA 3.0, Cididity Hat, Wikimedia Commons, not a picture of a rating event.

## A new Andhra party

V. Vijayasai Reddy, a former YSRCP member of the Rajya Sabha, launched the Parivartana Rajaneethi Party in Vijayawada on 11 November. He says the party will contest all 175 Andhra Pradesh Assembly seats. He says he will be a mentor, and that he does not want to be chief minister. Those lines are his. The Hindu, at 1:46 pm IST, The Times of India, the Daily Pioneer and CNBC-TV18 report the launch. A figure for a youth quota, and a line that half the candidates will be women, are not used. The slide is a file of a Rajya Sabha oath, GODL-India, Vice President's Secretariat, Wikimedia Commons, not the Vijayawada launch.

## Kedarnath

The doors of Kedarnath Dham closed for winter at 8:30 am on 11 November. Officials say more than 19 lakh pilgrims came this season. Rudraprayag district magistrate Vishal Mishra, in an account carried by ANI and Asianet, said more than 19.20 lakh. Winter worship moves to the Omkareshwar Temple at Ukhimath. Aaj Tak, Times Now Navbharat and Moneycontrol are among the reports. This page does not call the season a record. The slide is a file of the temple, public domain, Shaq774, Wikimedia Commons.

## The IPL trade

Before IPL 2027, Mumbai Indians captain Hardik Pandya moves to Punjab Kings at Rs 18 crore. His earlier fee was Rs 16.35 crore. Arshdeep Singh moves the other way, to Mumbai Indians, at his existing fee of Rs 18 crore. The primary lines are an IPL media advisory on iplt20.com and the Mumbai Indians release, both of 11 November. ESPNcricinfo, The Times of India and Hindustan Times were re-checked the same evening. The IPL's own graphic is not used. The slide is a 2020 file of Pandya, CC BY 3.0, Bollywood Hungama, Wikimedia Commons, not this trade.

## Abu Dhabi

US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi ended on 11 November, after two days. The sides agreed a large exchange of prisoners. US envoy Jared Kushner said the numbers will be finalised on Thursday. There is no ceasefire breakthrough. The Associated Press, Al Jazeera carrying AP and Reuters, Euronews, Interfax-Ukraine and the Kyiv Independent report the close. The slide is a file of the Abu Dhabi skyline, CC BY-SA 4.0, comedian44, Wikimedia Commons.

## Ebola in Congo

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has passed 11,000 confirmed cases. Government data, carried by Reuters and then by Al Jazeera, put deaths at at least 5,387. The World Health Organization's Disease Outbreak News, DON625, is a separate count, as of 3 November: 10,668 cases and 5,198 deaths. This page does not merge the two. The slide is a file of an Ebola treatment unit in Liberia in 2014, CC BY 2.0, CDC Global, Wikimedia Commons, not this outbreak.

## Lahore's air

The Pakistan Air Quality Initiative, an independent research group and not a government agency, says its evidence does not support blaming Lahore's smog after Diwali on fireworks in India. In the group's account, PM2.5 in Lahore was about 10 percent higher, against about 40 percent in Faisalabad and Multan. Dawn reports the group's count of 16,714 likely crop-residue fires in Pakistan's Punjab from 1 October to 9 November. The word likely is the report's. The primary posts are the group's own, on X, on the evening of 10 November. India Today also reports the finding. This page does not say India is cleared. The slide is a NASA MODIS archive from 10 November 2024, public domain, not this week's sky.
