This roundup is verified. The carousel was posted at 9:06 pm IST on 12 November 2026. The package was current as of 7:51 pm IST. Eight stories follow, each from the posted caption and that item's notes. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. Photographs on the story slides are file or representative images.

## October prices

Provisional retail inflation, the consumer price index, was 6.09 percent in October 2026. In September it was 5.54 percent. That is above the Reserve Bank's upper limit of 6 percent. Food inflation, the consumer food price index, was 8.94 percent. Onion prices were up 116 percent. Garlic, ginger and lemon were among the sharpest rises. The primary text is Press Information Bureau release 2335416, from the Ministry of Statistics, at 4 pm IST on 12 November. Business Standard and a Reuters account in The Economic Times also report it. The figures are provisional. The slide is a file of onions at Koyambedu market, CC BY 2.0, McKay Savage, Wikimedia Commons.

## Kishtwar

Two special police officers who had been missing in Kishtwar, Jammu and Kashmir, since Wednesday, 11 November, were found dead on Thursday morning. The bodies had bullet wounds. Officials said their weapons were also missing. A formal police statement has not come. The Times of India, carrying what officials told PTI, The Hindu, The Indian Express and the Tribune report this. This page does not attribute the deaths to any group. The slide is a file view from Pochal, CC BY-SA 4.0, PIXI-14, Wikimedia Commons, not this search.

## Parsi-Parlo

A fire on Thursday morning in Parsi-Parlo town, Kurung Kumey district, Arunachal Pradesh, killed a woman and her three children. The husband is still missing. That line is the deputy commissioner's, via PTI. The district disaster management officer, via PTI, says at least 24 houses were affected. No one is named. Chief Minister Pema Khandu expressed grief on X at 10:41 am IST. Hindustan Times also reports the fire. The slide is a file of Sarli, CC BY-SA 4.0, Frontierman123, Wikimedia Commons, a representative photograph, not Parsi-Parlo.

## Punjab list

The Aam Aadmi Party issued a second list of 31 candidates for the 2027 Punjab Assembly election. Names are now settled for 71 of 117 seats. In Lambi, minister Gurmeet Singh Khuddian is replaced by his relative, Randhir Singh Dhira Khuddian. The posted line says relative, not nephew. The primary list is the party's own, on X, at 5:07 pm IST. The Indian Express and India Today report it. The slide is a 2026 file of Bhagwant Mann, GODL-India, Press Information Bureau, not this list.

## Visas

The home ministry cut the main visa categories from 13 to 9, and the sub-categories from 49 to 23. The tourist visa will be called a visitor visa. The forms will be shorter. The primary text is Press Information Bureau release 2335182, at 7:39 pm IST on 11 November. The slide is a file of an India e-visa, CC BY-SA 4.0, CrFilip98, Wikimedia Commons.

## The Kremlin

US President Donald Trump's envoys, Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, met Russian President Vladimir Putin in the Kremlin on 12 November. The meeting followed the Abu Dhabi agreement on a large exchange of prisoners between Russia and Ukraine. The primary note is on kremlin.ru. The Associated Press, Reuters as carried by Yahoo, and RIA also report the meeting. No deal is claimed. No ceasefire is claimed. The slide credit is a Kremlin photograph, CC BY 4.0, filed as a representative image.

## Indonesia

Vice-president Gibran Rakabuming Raka resigned during an impeachment process. MPR Speaker Ahmad Muzani said so. Gibran is the son of former president Joko Widodo. The Jakarta Post, citing Muzani, and ABC News report the resignation. The slide is his 2024 official portrait, public domain, Darwis Triadi and the Ministry of State Secretariat, not the resignation.

## The climate index

In the Climate Change Performance Index for 2027, India moved from 23rd to 28th. The overall rating stayed medium. The report was released at COP31 in Antalya, Turkiye. The primary page is the India country page on ccpi.org. Down To Earth and The Times of India also report the move. The slide is a file of a COP31 energy ministerial, CC BY 4.0, Necati Savas for the European Union, 2026.
