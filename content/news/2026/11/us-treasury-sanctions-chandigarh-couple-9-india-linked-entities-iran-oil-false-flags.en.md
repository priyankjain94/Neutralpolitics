This note is verified as a record of the carousel. The stamps on that carousel are BREAKING and VERIFIED. It was posted at 8:59 am IST on 5 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used. The claims below are US Treasury allegations. They are not a court finding.

On 4 November 2026 the US Treasury sanctioned Chandigarh-based Suniel Kumar Sharma, his wife Jaspreet Preeti Shahid, and nine India-linked entities. The release is sb0681. The Treasury alleges that they ran a maritime fraud network that provided false flags and certifications to vessels carrying Iranian oil. It says the couple impersonated flag registries of Guyana, Eswatini, Laos, Dominica, Aruba, Nicaragua and Sint Maarten. Vessels named are PING SHUN, LATTAFA, BINARY STAR and VERA. Those sentences are the Treasury's allegations.

The entities named include Oceaniek Technologies India, Aryavart Ship Management, and the Singapore-based Eswatini Maritime Affairs and International Ship Registry. The Treasury account also names Precept Trends, Polynesia Maritime, Alexarya Finserv, Alexarya Media and Entertainment, Viharaboating, and Aryavart Maritime and Educational Trust.

Ashwani Kumar Dhiman and Ishwinder Singh were also designated. The Treasury account describes the two as Indian citizens heading their firms. Turkiye's Fugo Ground Services is accused of providing aviation services to Iran. That accusation is the Treasury's. It is not stated here as a finding.

The action is under Operation Economic Outcast and Executive Order 13902, which the Treasury account ties to Iran's petroleum, petrochemical and aviation sectors. Assets of those designated that are under US jurisdiction are frozen.

No charge or finding by an Indian authority is reported. No response from India's Ministry of External Affairs is reported. This page does not supply one. It does not call the people or the firms smugglers or criminals.

The outlets named on the carousel are the US Treasury, The Hindu, India Today and Moneycontrol.

Read together, the reported lines are these. On 4 November the US Treasury sanctioned Suniel Kumar Sharma, Jaspreet Preeti Shahid and nine India-linked entities, and it alleges false flags and certifications for vessels carrying Iranian oil. The registries it names are Guyana, Eswatini, Laos, Dominica, Aruba, Nicaragua and Sint Maarten. The vessels it names are PING SHUN, LATTAFA, BINARY STAR and VERA. Ashwani Kumar Dhiman and Ishwinder Singh were also designated. Fugo Ground Services is accused of aviation services to Iran. The action is under Executive Order 13902 and Operation Economic Outcast, and US-jurisdiction assets are frozen. These are Treasury allegations, not a court finding. No Indian charge and no MEA response is reported.
