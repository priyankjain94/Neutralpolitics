This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The stamps are BREAKING and VERIFIED. The carousel was posted at 9:18 am IST on 4 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used.

Police say a suspected blast damaged the Anupgarh-Suratgarh railway line near 50 GB village on Tuesday night, 3 November. The village is in Sri Ganganagar district, in the Ramsinghpur police station area, close to the India-Pakistan border. That place is the police account, as News18 and ANI report it.

The suspected blast came about 45 minutes after a passenger train on the Anupgarh-Suratgarh line had passed. The train had left at about 9 pm. News18 and Dainik Bhaskar report that timing.

Two concrete pillars under the track and two sleepers were damaged. News18 reports a crater about two feet deep. No one was hurt.

The chief public relations officer of North Western Railway said two passenger trains, 59711/12 and 59715/16, were cancelled as a precaution. That is the officer's line, in the ANI copy carried by the Indian Economic Observer.

ATS, the Crime Investigation Department, the Forensic Science Laboratory, a dog squad and a bomb disposal team are investigating. Sri Ganganagar superintendent of police Harishankar said it is too early to state the exact nature of the incident. He said the cause will be known after the forensic examination. He also said there is no established link with 7.5 kg of RDX recently recovered in the border area. Those sentences are his. This page calls it a suspected blast. The cause is under investigation. It does not call this terror, sabotage or an IED, and it does not say the train was targeted.

The outlets named on the carousel are News18, ANI, Dainik Bhaskar and Daily Jagran. The ANI copy used here is the one on the Indian Economic Observer.

Read together, the reported lines are these. On Tuesday night, near 50 GB village on the Anupgarh-Suratgarh line in Sri Ganganagar, police report a suspected blast about 45 minutes after a passenger train that had left at about 9 pm. Two concrete pillars and two sleepers were damaged. News18 reports a crater about two feet deep. No one was hurt. North Western Railway cancelled passenger trains 59711/12 and 59715/16 as a precaution. ATS, CID, the forensic laboratory, a dog squad and a bomb disposal team are probing. SP Harishankar says it is too early to state the exact nature, that forensics will show the cause, and that there is no established link with 7.5 kg of RDX recently recovered in the border area. The cause is under investigation.
