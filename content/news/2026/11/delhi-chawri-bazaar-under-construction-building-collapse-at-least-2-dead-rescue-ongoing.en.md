This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is VERIFIED. The carousel was posted at 12:59 pm IST on 5 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used.

On Thursday morning, 5 November, a three-storey building that was under construction collapsed in Old Delhi. The place is Gali Sitara, in Sheesh Mahal, near Chawri Bazaar Metro station. The same lane is also reported as Gali Sattara. The Hindu and PTI are the reports named for that place, and for the call at 9:50 am. The accounts put the collapse at about 9:50 am.

Delhi Police say at least two people died and one person was injured. Several others were feared trapped under the debris. That is the police line, as the reports carry it. The Indian Express live page reports the Delhi Police figures as two dead and one injured. Hindustan Times reports two dead and one injured. The Hindu, in an update modified at 12:44 IST, reports at least two bodies. PTI's headline says two killed.

This page uses one line for the toll: at least 2 dead, and 1 injured. The check of those reports was around 12:55 IST on 5 November. At that hour the toll was still developing. Rescue was ongoing at the time of reporting. Two is not written here as a closed count. The carousel went up at 12:59 pm IST, a few minutes after that check.

At least six Delhi Fire Services units were sent to the site. NDRF teams were on search and rescue. PTI and Hindustan Times report that senior officers were on the site. Those are the response lines in the reports. This page does not add a unit count of its own, and it does not describe the search beyond what those reports say.

The building was under construction. It is described as three storeys. An eyewitness said lintels laid in recent months had fallen. Hindustan Times carries that account, citing ANI. The words belong to that eyewitness, as the newspaper reports them. They are not an official cause. The cause of the collapse is not officially established. This page does not say the collapse was the result of negligence. It does not name a builder, a contractor, or a violation.

The identities of the people who died are not confirmed. This page names no victim.

The reports place the collapse in a season of heavy Diwali footfall in Old Delhi's wholesale markets. That setting is theirs. They also name two other recent collapses in Delhi, as context: Satya Niketan on 6 September, with 7 dead, and a collapse in New Seemapuri. Those are separate incidents, given here only as the reports give them. This page does not reopen them.

This collapse is also separate from the Chandni Chowk Town Hall facade collapse. The package for this note says the two are not the same event. The source note calls that facade collapse the previous day's. This page does not describe that facade collapse. It only keeps the two incidents apart.
