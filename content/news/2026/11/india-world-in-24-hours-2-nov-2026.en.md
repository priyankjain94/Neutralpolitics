## Cyclone Murasu

IMD's advisory, based on observations at 05:30 IST, describes Murasu as a severe cyclonic storm about 260 km southeast of Ongole. The crossing is a forecast. IMD expects it to cross the south Andhra coast between Nellore and Narsapur, close to Ongole, between 11:30 pm IST on 2 November and 4 am IST on 3 November, with winds of 100 to 110 kmph, gusting to 120 kmph. Landfall has not happened. This page does not call the storm very severe. It does not give a toll, and it does not predict damage.

Andhra Pradesh has begun moving people from vulnerable coastal areas. Prakhar Jain, managing director of the state disaster authority, told Reuters that about 2,000 people had been evacuated and about 30,000 were planned. That pair of figures is what he told Reuters. The health department has set up 116 relief centres and 1,688 medical teams, with 13,165 emergency beds, The Times of India and Deccan Chronicle report. They also report that 493 of 6,870 women in late pregnancy had been shifted to health centres. APSDMA, via The New Indian Express, says 6 NDRF teams and 7 SDRF teams are deployed in Krishna, Bapatla, Guntur, Nellore, Prakasam, Markapuram, Palnadu and Tirupati. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu cancelled a Monday visit to Delhi to review preparedness. The helplines named are 112, 1070 and 1800-425-0101, and Mana Mitra on WhatsApp at 9552300009. The fuller note is [Murasu now severe; landfall forecast](/news/2026/11/imd-bay-of-bengal-depression-likely-severe-cyclone-murasu-south-andhra-nov-3). ## Jhunjhunu

Eight labourers were killed on Monday 2 November at Chhapoli village, in the Udaipurwati police station area of Jhunjhunu district, Rajasthan. Police said a gravel-mixing machine at a road-construction site touched an overhead 11,000-volt line. Udaipurwati station house officer Rampal is the officer named for that account, via PTI, and ETV Bharat also reports it. ETV Bharat says five died at the site and three in hospital. Several others were injured. One report puts the injured at three and another at five, so this page says several injured and does not pick one number. The station house officer, via PTI, said all eight who died were local residents. They are not named here.

The road between Chhapoli and Kadamkund was being built with public participation. Chief Minister Bhajanlal Sharma ordered a high-level inquiry and asked the district collector for a report. Energy Minister Heeralal Nagar said the road was being built privately, on a small scale, with public participation, and that if any departmental or technical lapse is found, action will be taken. That is his statement. The inquiry has not reported a finding. This page does not blame the power company or the villagers. ## GRAP Stage II

The Commission for Air Quality Management on Monday invoked Stage II of the Graded Response Action Plan across Delhi-NCR, with immediate effect, after Delhi's air entered the very poor category. The order cites the AQI rising from 117 on 31 October to 261 on 1 November and 324 on 2 November, at 4 pm. Business Standard reports 325 at 5 pm. The commission cited unfavourable weather. This page does not attribute the rise to firecrackers or to stubble burning. IMD and IITM, in the commission's order, expect the air to stay very poor in the coming days. This page does not quote a sharper forecast than that.

Stage I and Stage II measures now apply. They include mechanical road cleaning, water sprinkling and anti-smog guns, stricter pollution-under-control checks, and a bar on coal and firewood in tandoors at hotels, restaurants and eateries. Diesel generators are regulated, with exceptions for specified emergency services. Metro, RRTS and CNG and electric bus services are to run more often. The Indian Express reports that parking fees are to be raised. This is not a construction ban, and it is not a school closure. Those belong to later stages. ## Corvettes

On Monday the Defence Ministry signed contracts worth Rs 53,866.50 crore with Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers and Goa Shipyard Limited for eight Next Generation Corvettes for the Indian Navy. GRSE will build five and Goa Shipyard three. The figure is the combined value, not a price for each yard. The signing was at Kartavya Bhawan-2 in New Delhi, in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh, the Press Information Bureau says. The ministry says this is the first project in which the Navy designs the warships in-house and defence public-sector yards chosen through competition build them. The ships are to carry advanced missiles, weapons and sensors for air, surface and sub-surface missions. The roles named are protection of sea trade routes, anti-surface and anti-submarine warfare, coastal defence, littoral operations and surveillance. No missile is named here, the ships are not called stealth, and no delivery date is given. The Press Information Bureau expects about 72 lakh person-days of work in the shipyards and about 460 lakh person-days in ancillary industry and small firms over 10 years. ## Hylo Open

In Saarbrücken, Germany, on Sunday 1 November, India won both singles titles at the Hylo Open. PV Sindhu beat Line Kjaersfeldt 21-12, 21-14 in the women's final. Lakshya Sen beat Indonesia's Jonatan Christie 21-17, 21-10 in 39 minutes in the men's final. It is Sen's first title of 2026 and his second Hylo Open title, after 2019. His record against Christie is now 4-4. He is world No. 14 and was seeded seventh. This page does not use a career-title count. The slide is a 2018 file of Lakshya Sen, BugWarp / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this final.

## Hormuz

Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei said on Monday, at a weekly briefing, that Iran is considering reopening the Strait of Hormuz within two days if the United States accepts Iran's conditions. He said messages were being exchanged with Washington through mediators and that everything depends on the United States. He called the two-day possibility another indication of Iran's good-faith approach. That is his statement. An earlier plan, in September, through Qatar, had proposed about a week. Iran's stated demands are an end to the war on all fronts, the unfreezing of assets abroad, the lifting of oil-sector sanctions, and an end to the US naval blockade. Those demands are not described here as accepted. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, in an Al Jazeera report on 1 November, said the United States must accept a new Iran-Oman transit route. That claim is not attributed to the United States. No agreement has been announced. The strait is not described as open. The package notes that Hormuz is a key route for India's crude and LPG imports, and that Indian seafarers have been caught in incidents there, including MT Louise-1 on 30 October. ## Formula 1

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli, of Italy, became Formula 1's youngest world champion, at 20 years and 68 days, after finishing third at the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday. Max Verstappen won the race. Lewis Hamilton was second. The FIA says Antonelli needed a top-nine finish to secure the 2026 drivers' title, with races still to go. ## Indian students in the US

Active Indian student records in the United States were 316,893 in October 2026, down from 366,258 in October 2025. That is a fall of 49,365, or 13.5 percent. The figures are US government SEVIS data, reported by IANS and The Economic Times. Master's records fell from 292,929 to 234,034, about 20 percent. Doctoral records rose from 28,609 to 41,729, about 46 percent. The Economic Times also reports all international active records falling from 1,283,240 to 1,192,572, about 7.1 percent. India remains the largest source of international students. SEVIS is a monthly snapshot of active records, including students on Optional Practical Training. It is not an annual enrolment count. This page does not attribute the fall to one US policy. The slide is a 2019 file of students at the University of North Carolina, Dennis Ludlow / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not these records.
