This roundup is verified. The carousel was posted at 9:14 pm IST on 17 November 2026. The package was current as of 8:56 pm IST. Eight stories follow, each from the posted caption and that item's notes. The cover is an NP graphic, a recap cover. Photographs on the story slides are file or representative images. They are not from these events.

## Tehri

Near Gular, in Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand, a bus fell into a gorge. Officials and the police say 9 people died and 25 were injured. The bus was going from Haridwar to Joshimath. The crash was around 4:50 am. The police are investigating. PTI, The Week and The Times of India are the cited reports. This page does not state a cause as fact. It names no one. The slide is a file of the drive from Haridwar to Joshimath, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons, not this crash.

## Cyclone Akvan

IMD National Bulletin No. 16, issued at 6:15 pm IST on 17 November, says Cyclone Akvan may become a severe cyclonic storm around this evening. It may cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coast, between Nellore and Visakhapatnam, around noon on 20 November, as a very severe cyclonic storm, with winds of 120 to 130 kmph and gusts of 145 kmph. That forecast is the IMD bulletin's. The slide is a file of Tropical Cyclone Fani, public domain, Wikimedia Commons, not Akvan.

## Fuel levies

The Centre raised the export levy on diesel from Rs 11.50 to Rs 14 a litre, and the levy on aviation turbine fuel from Rs 11 to Rs 12.50 a litre, from 16 November. The petrol export levy is zero. Excise on fuel sold in the country is unchanged. The cited text is Finance Ministry notifications, carried by PTI and Scroll. The slide is a file of a petrol pump in Nagpur, CC BY 1.0, Wikimedia Commons, not this levy.

## Unemployment

The MoSPI periodic labour force survey says the unemployment rate in October was 4.9 percent, down from 5.0 percent in September. Urban unemployment was 6.7 percent and rural unemployment was 4.2 percent. The figures are for people aged 15 and above, on the current weekly status. The bulletin is dated 16 November. The slide is a file of a construction worker in Delhi, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons.

## Tata Sons

A letter from the Reserve Bank of India on 16 November tells Tata Sons to follow the rules for an upper-layer non-banking financial company, including listing. Hindustan Times and Mint report that the letter sets no new deadline. The Economic Times is also cited. The slide is a file of Bombay House, the Tata Group headquarters in Mumbai, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons.

## Manila

At the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit in Manila, in the Philippines, Prime Minister Narendra Modi put several proposals. A joint statement on disaster management was issued. These are proposals. They are not decisions now in force. The cited sources are the Prime Minister's website and the Ministry of External Affairs, via PTI and ANI. This page does not add proposal texts the caption does not give. The slide is a file of the 15th ASEAN-India Summit, public domain, Wikimedia Commons, not this meeting.

## Tara Gas

The Ministry of External Affairs said on 17 November that, after an attack on the Panama-flagged LPG tanker MT Tara Gas in the Strait of Hormuz, 21 of 24 Indians were brought ashore at Khasab, in Oman. Two injured Indians are in hospital. One Indian is missing. Shipping Minister Sonowal said this was a second attack, and that the crew had to leave the ship. The search continues. The sources are the MEA statement and Sonowal's post on X. This page does not say that all 24 Indians are safe. The slide is a 2019 file of the LPG tanker Run Far off Peng Chau, Hong Kong, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons, not Tara Gas.

## The visa-stay rule

A federal judge in Boston vacated a Department of Homeland Security rule on duration of status. The rule would have set fixed stays, of up to four years, for F-1 students, J-1 visitors, and holders of the I media visa. Inside Higher Ed, the Boston Globe, The Hindu and The Economic Times report the order. Those reports expect the government to appeal. This page does not say an appeal has been filed. The slide is a September 2024 file of the John Joseph Moakley United States Courthouse, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons.
