This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is VERIFIED. It is not marked breaking. The carousel was posted at 10:55 am IST on 5 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used. A photograph carried with the Hindustan Times report is not used here.

On Wednesday night, 4 November, two rescue boats capsized in the overflowing Bethupalli tank in Telangana's Khammam district. Hindustan Times reports that the boats capsized around 7:30 pm, and that the tank was overflowing after Cyclone Murasu rains. That time and that rain line are the newspaper's.

The people on the boats had set out to save five fishermen stranded by the rising floodwater. The group was mostly State Disaster Response Force and fire services personnel, with one local resident. Officials say all 11 people were rescued by Thursday morning. This page uses that completed line. It uses 11 people.

District fire officer Ajay Kumar said the personnel were rescued in phases between about 2:15 am and about 7 am on 5 November. He said the rescue in those hours was by the NDRF and fire services. He said Collector T S Divakar was on site. Those sentences are his, as the reports carry them.

An NDRF team from Vijayawada rescued the five stranded fishermen separately, by about 11:20 pm. Hindustan Times reports that rescue. The five fishermen are not the 11. The rescued personnel were taken to Sathupalli hospital.

Reports say Chief Minister Revanth Reddy, minister Ponguleti Srinivas Reddy and Union minister of state Bandi Sanjay Kumar monitored the operation. That monitoring line stays with the reports. This page does not turn it into a statement of its own.

Early reports put the count at 9 or 10 personnel. The Times of India and Deccan Herald are the other two outlets named on the carousel, and their counts were those earlier ones. Hindustan Times's final tally is 11 people, including one local resident. This page uses 11.

Cyclone Murasu, the storm named in the Hindustan Times rain line, is the storm in this site's earlier note, [Murasu crosses near Ongole](/news/2026/11/imd-bay-of-bengal-depression-likely-severe-cyclone-murasu-south-andhra-nov-3). Read together, the reported lines are these. Around 7:30 pm on 4 November, two rescue boats capsized in the overflowing Bethupalli tank in Khammam, after rains linked to Cyclone Murasu, Hindustan Times reports. The group was mostly State Disaster Response Force and fire services personnel, plus one local resident, and had set out to save five fishermen. An NDRF team from Vijayawada rescued those five fishermen separately by about 11:20 pm. District fire officer Ajay Kumar said the personnel were rescued in phases between about 2:15 am and about 7 am on 5 November, by the NDRF and fire services, and that Collector T S Divakar was on site. The rescued personnel were taken to Sathupalli hospital. Officials say all 11 were rescued by Thursday morning. Reports say Chief Minister Revanth Reddy, minister Ponguleti Srinivas Reddy and Union minister of state Bandi Sanjay Kumar monitored the operation. Early counts of 9 or 10 came before Hindustan Times's final tally of 11 people.

The sources named on the carousel are Hindustan Times, The Times of India and Deccan Herald.
