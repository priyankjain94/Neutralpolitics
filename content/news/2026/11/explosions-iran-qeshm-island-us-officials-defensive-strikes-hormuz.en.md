This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 9:16 am IST on 3 November 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic. No photograph is used. The lines below keep each account with the people who gave it.

Iranian media reported several explosions late on Monday, local time, near the Strait of Hormuz. IRNA reported five explosions in rapid succession on Qeshm Island. Witnesses reported two aircraft overhead. Fars reported explosions heard from the direction of the sea at Bandar Lengeh, in Hormozgan province.

The Wall Street Journal and the Associated Press, citing unnamed US officials, reported that the US military carried out what the officials called defensive strikes in the Strait of Hormuz, aimed at Iranian air-defence or anti-shipping capability. One official told the Associated Press that the targets were Iranians using surface-to-air missiles against US assets. Those sentences are the officials' account, carried by those two news organisations. They are not an official statement from Washington. This page does not say, as its own conclusion, that the United States attacked Iran, and it does not say that a war resumed.

Earlier on Monday, Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed it had brought down, or captured, US drones near Qeshm. That is a claim. It is unverified. This note does not repeat it as fact.

There was no immediate official comment from Tehran or from Washington. No casualty details had been reported. No damage details had been reported.

The caption's line on India is this. The Strait of Hormuz is a major route for India's crude oil and LPG imports. This page stops there. It does not guess at a price, and it does not guess about Indian ships.
