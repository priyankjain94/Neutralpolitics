## Cyclone Murasu

Severe Cyclonic Storm Murasu crossed the south Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Narsapur, close to Ongole, between 3:30 am and 5:30 am IST on Tuesday 3 November. IMD put the winds at the crossing at 100 to 110 kmph, gusting to 120 kmph. The posted caption says thousands of trees, hundreds of electricity poles and boats were damaged. The New Indian Express, with PTI, and NDTV report thousands of trees uprooted, hundreds of power poles damaged, fishing boats wrecked and crops hit in Prakasam, Bapatla, Palnadu and Guntur. Kothapatnam in Prakasam recorded 16.4 cm of rain from 8:30 am on Monday to 5 am on Tuesday, the highest in the state in that account.

The posted carousel was revised at 9:10 pm IST. It cites APSDMA managing director Prakhar Jain, via PTI, for at least two deaths in cyclone-related incidents, from a 7:24 pm IST report. The posted caption gives 12,230 people in relief camps. IMD's forecast is that the storm is weakening. The earlier bulletin said it would weaken to a cyclonic storm by noon and a deep depression by midnight. This page does not say that change has already happened, and it does not call the storm very severe. The fuller note is [Murasu crosses near Ongole](/news/2026/11/imd-bay-of-bengal-depression-likely-severe-cyclone-murasu-south-andhra-nov-3). ## MSV Al Jusufi

Union Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal said on X that the Indian-flagged motorised sailing vessel MSV Al Jusufi was struck by an unidentified aerial object near the coast of Oman on Monday 2 November. Fourteen Indian seafarers were on board. Thirteen were evacuated and rescued by the Omani Coast Guard. One seafarer, from Gujarat, died of his injuries. He is not named on this page. Sonowal said the Indian Embassy in Oman is in touch with local authorities, and the ministry is working to bring the survivors and the remains home. Who carried out the strike has not been stated. This page does not name a country or a group, does not call the object a missile or a drone, and does not say the vessel sank. It does not link the strike to the reported events at Qeshm. The slide is a 2014 file of a trading dhow on Dubai Creek, Rod Waddington / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0, not this vessel.

## Kedartal

On Tuesday, the fifth day of the search, rescue teams recovered two bodies from avalanche debris near Kedartal in Uttarkashi, Uttarakhand. PTI and The Indian Express say the bodies are a man and a woman, and that they have not been identified. Six members of the trekking party are still missing. A joint team of the State Disaster Response Force and the Nehru Institute of Mountaineering is carrying out the search. It stopped for the afternoon and is to resume on Wednesday 4 November. The Indian Express reports an alternative helipad at about 4,005 metres. The party had 12 members. Four of them, three porters and a cook, were rescued on 31 October. Eight had been missing. The Indian Express describes those eight as five trekkers from Bankura in West Bengal, two women from Uttar Pradesh, and a local guide. No one is named here. This page does not say everyone is dead, does not say the search is over, and does not use figures of seven missing or twelve dead. The slide is a 2007 file of Kedartal, Peter Andersen / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0, not this search.

## ISSF Worlds, Doha

On Tuesday in Doha, Ravinder Singh, Saurabh Chaudhary and Amanpreet Singh won gold in the men's 50m pistol team event at the ISSF World Championships. The total is 1,659. PTI, via Rediff and DD News, and Sportstar give the scores as Ravinder Singh 559, Saurabh Chaudhary 551 and Amanpreet Singh 549. China took silver with 1,648. South Korea took bronze with 1,643. In the individual event, Ravinder Singh won silver with 559. Gold went to Anton Aristarkhov, competing as an Individual Neutral Athlete, with 561. Bronze went to Romania's Luca Joldea with 558. Sportstar and PTI say the men's 50m pistol is not an Olympic event. Sportstar says these medals opened India's tally at these championships. This page does not call the event Olympic, does not say it brings an Olympic quota, does not say India leads the medal table, and does not call Ravinder Singh world champion. The championships are still going on. The slide is a 2025 file of Saurabh Chaudhary, Government of India / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not this final.

## Aga Syed Ruhullah Mehdi

On Tuesday, at a gathering in Budgam, Srinagar Lok Sabha MP Aga Syed Ruhullah Mehdi announced that he was leaving the National Conference and resigning his parliamentary seat. India Today and The Indian Express report the announcement. He told supporters the party had moved away from promises on restoring Article 370, constitutional rights, reservation reforms, and the release of political prisoners. That is what he cited. India Today and The Hindu place the announcement amid pressure from dissident National Conference MLAs on Chief Minister Omar Abdullah's government. This page does not quote reactions as findings. A Lok Sabha resignation takes effect once the Speaker accepts it. Acceptance has not been reported. No by-election is reported here. This page does not say he has joined another party. The slide is a 2024 file of Mehdi, Syed Shahzaman1 / Wikimedia Commons, CC0, not the Budgam gathering.

## Abdulla Shahid

Maldives police arrested former foreign minister Abdulla Shahid late on Monday under a court order, over the alleged abuse of a minor. India Today, citing the Maldives Police Service, reports the arrest at about 9:59 pm local time on 2 November, at his home in Hulhumale. He denies the allegation. Shortly before the arrest he wrote on X that it was baseless and politically motivated. That is his statement. The case is under investigation. This page does not say he has been charged or convicted, and it gives no detail about the minor. Shahid is 64. He was foreign minister from 2018 to 2023, president of the 76th UN General Assembly, and Speaker of the People's Majlis from 2009 to 2014. The Standard reports that he resigned as MDP president on 14 August 2026 to contest the party's 2028 presidential primary. This page calls him a former MDP president. It does not connect the arrest to India-Maldives politics. The slide is a 2018 portrait, The President's Office, Maldives / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0, not this arrest.

## Mukesh Ambani

Forbes reports that Reliance Industries chairman Mukesh Ambani has overtaken ByteDance co-founder Zhang Yiming as Asia's richest person, after Reliance shares rose nearly 12 percent on Monday 2 November, following the quarterly results. Moneycontrol and Business Today put his net worth at about $88.8 billion. The ranking is Forbes's, and it moves with share prices. This page does not call him the world's richest, and it does not give a rank from another index. The slide is a 2014 file, Prime Minister's Office / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not this ranking.
