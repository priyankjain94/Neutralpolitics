This note records the carousel. The stamp on that carousel is VERIFIED. It was posted at 11:16 am IST on 5 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used. A photograph carried with The Indian Express report is not used here. What follows is what the reports said. A seizure is not a charge.

West Bengal Police on Wednesday, 4 November, seized ₹1,02,79,000 in cash, about ₹1.03 crore, from a Trinamool Congress party office at Sarisha in Diamond Harbour, South 24 Parganas. PTI and The Indian Express report that amount and that place. Additional SP Abhishek Majumdar confirmed the seizure. That confirmation is what the reports say.

Reports say the office is linked to rebel TMC MLA Shamim (Samim) Ahmed, the MLA from Magrahat Paschim. The Indian Express describes him as in the rebel TMC camp. This page says the office is linked to him. It does not attribute the cash to him.

Police said the raid followed the questioning of Sumit Roy, an aide of TMC MP Abhishek Banerjee. Roy was arrested on 31 October in the Kalitala soil-theft case. That arrest and that questioning are the police account in the reports. The probe concerns alleged illegal soil mining from 2017 to 2025. Alleged stays on that mining. This page does not state a scam figure.

Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari called the seizure the "tip of the iceberg". That is his remark, as the reports carry it. Alongside it, the Mamata Banerjee-led TMC alleges the cash was planted, and alleges that evidence was planted. The two lines stay together. Neither is treated here as a finding.

The MLA did not respond to reporters. No charge against Shamim Ahmed over this cash is reported. No charge against Abhishek Banerjee over this cash is reported. This page does not imply guilt.

On 4 November, West Bengal Police seized ₹1,02,79,000, about ₹1.03 crore, from a Trinamool Congress party office at Sarisha in Diamond Harbour, South 24 Parganas. Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari called it the "tip of the iceberg", and the Mamata Banerjee-led TMC alleges the cash was planted and alleges evidence was planted. The MLA did not respond to reporters. No charge against Shamim Ahmed or Abhishek Banerjee over this cash is reported. The cash is not attributed to the MLA.
