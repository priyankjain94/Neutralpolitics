This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 9:05 am IST on 2 November 2026. The stamp is VERIFIED. The cover is an NP graphic, a typographic card. No photograph of a person is used. What follows is what media reports said that morning. It is not an official account from the Enforcement Directorate.

Reports say an Enforcement Directorate team reached the Hoshiarpur home of Congress leader and former Punjab minister Sunder Sham Arora at about 5 am on Monday 2 November. The address in the reports is Jodha Mal Road. Some of those reports spell the name Sundar. This page uses Sunder Sham Arora, the spelling in the caption.

ABP Live reports that the visit was part of searches at 16 locations in Punjab. The team was reportedly from the Enforcement Directorate office in Jalandhar. Both of those lines are reports. This page does not turn them into an official list of sites or an official posting of the team.

Babushahi reported that Income Tax officials were also involved. That sentence stays with Babushahi. This note does not state a joint Enforcement Directorate and Income Tax action as a fact of its own. It says reports said so, and it names the outlet.

At the time of these reports, the Enforcement Directorate had not put out an official statement. No reason for the searches had been stated officially. It was not known whether anything was found. This page does not say that anything was seized. It does not name a case. It does not describe an allegation. A search is not a charge. The Enforcement Directorate has not stated any wrongdoing.

Read together, the morning's reported lines are these. The day is Monday 2 November 2026. The time is about 5 am. The place is Jodha Mal Road, Hoshiarpur. The person named in the reports is Congress leader and former Punjab minister Sunder Sham Arora. ABP Live links the visit to searches at 16 locations in Punjab. The team was reportedly from ED Jalandhar. Babushahi reported that Income Tax officials were also involved. There was no official ED statement, no stated reason, and no confirmed find. A search is not a charge, and the ED has not stated any wrongdoing.
