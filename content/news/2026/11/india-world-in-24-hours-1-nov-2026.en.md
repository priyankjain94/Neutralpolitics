## Cyclone Murasu

IMD's mid-day bulletin on 1 November speaks of Cyclonic Storm Murasu over the southwest Bay of Bengal. The crossing is a forecast, not a completed event. IMD says it is likely to cross the south Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Narsapur between 11:30 pm IST on 2 November and 4 am on 3 November, as a severe cyclonic storm. Winds of 100 to 110 kmph, gusting to 120 kmph, are the forecast at that crossing. The bulletin also says the system is to intensify into a severe cyclonic storm by the morning of 2 November. This page does not call it very severe. It does not give a toll or a damage figure. It does not name a point inside the Nellore-Narsapur stretch.

At 11:30 am IST on 1 November the position was about 12.1°N, 85.3°E: about 560 km east-southeast of Chennai, about 630 km east-southeast of Nellore, and about 660 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam. India Today reports that Odisha has also issued an alert. An earlier note on this site used the morning bulletin, which had not fixed this night window. [Bay depression may be Cyclone Murasu](/news/2026/11/imd-bay-of-bengal-depression-likely-severe-cyclone-murasu-south-andhra-nov-3). This item is the later bulletin. The window and the speeds remain forecasts.

## Ramlila Maidan

The opposition INDIA bloc held a Vote Bachao, Desh Bachao rally at Delhi's Ramlila Maidan on Sunday 1 November. The package says it was held after Delhi Police permission, and that there was no nod for a march. The demand is the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged irregularities in the voter rolls during the Special Intensive Revision. That claim is the opposition's allegation. This page does not treat it as a finding.

Speakers included Rahul Gandhi, Mallikarjun Kharge and Priyanka Gandhi, and leaders of the Samajwadi Party, the Trinamool Congress, the Jharkhand Mukti Morcha and the Rashtriya Janata Dal: Akhilesh Yadav, Mamata Banerjee, Hemant Soren, Revanth Reddy and Tejashwi Yadav. The Times of India reports that Kharge said the agitation would continue until the Chief Election Commissioner resigns. The Associated Press reported that tens of thousands attended. That attendance line is the Associated Press's. It is not a count made for this page.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah, in Chandigarh, criticised the date. He said holding the rally on the anniversary of the 1984 anti-Sikh riots rubs salt in the wounds of the victims. That line is PTI's report of what he said. BJP leaders held counter-marches. The morning note, written before this rally, is [INDIA bloc rally at Ramlila Maidan](/news/2026/11/india-bloc-ramlila-maidan-rally-nov-1-cec-sir). This item is the report that the rally was held.

## GST

Gross GST collections in October 2026 were ₹2,31,290 crore, up 22.9 percent from ₹1.88 lakh crore in October 2025. The figures are provisional government data released on Sunday. Revenue from domestic transactions grew 15.8 percent to ₹1.59 lakh crore. GST on imports rose 42.2 percent to ₹72,246 crore. Within the gross figure, CGST was ₹42,935 crore, SGST was ₹51,340 crore, and IGST was about ₹1.37 lakh crore. Refunds were ₹27,934 crore, up 6.5 percent. Net collections after refunds were about ₹2.03 lakh crore, up about 25.6 percent. For April to October, gross collections were ₹14.78 lakh crore, up 13.2 percent, and net collections were ₹12.69 lakh crore.

The Times of India gives some state lines: Maharashtra ₹35,150 crore, up 13 percent; Karnataka up 18 percent; Gujarat up 16 percent; Uttar Pradesh and Bihar up 24 percent. A Financial Express headline uses the word record. Other reports do not. This page says the collections were up 22.9 percent. It does not call them a record, and it does not credit or blame a party.

## Kabaddi

India won both titles at the first Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship in Ahmedabad on Saturday. The women's team beat Uganda 35-15. Sakshi scored 9 points, and the captain is Nidhi Sharma. The men's team beat Sri Lanka 54-20. Aditya Shinde had 10 raid points, and Bharat Hooda had 8. The men won all eight of their matches. In the semi-final they beat Bangladesh 56-27. Both teams went through the tournament unbeaten. It ran from 23 to 31 October at the Veer Savarkar Sports Complex. This is the first edition of that championship. It is not a Commonwealth Games event, and it is not a world title. This page gives no prize money and no medal count.

## Commercial LPG

From 1 November, oil marketing companies raised the price of the 19-kg commercial LPG cylinder by ₹10 to ₹11.50. In Delhi it is ₹2,820, up ₹10. In Mumbai it is ₹2,774.50, up ₹10. In Kolkata it is ₹2,964, up ₹10. In Chennai it is ₹2,994.50, up ₹11.50. ABP Live also gives Bengaluru at ₹2,908, up ₹10. That Bengaluru rate is ABP Live's alone. The companies named for the monthly revision are Indian Oil, HPCL and BPCL. Commercial cylinders are used mainly by hotels, restaurants, dhabas and caterers. The 14.2-kg domestic cylinder is unchanged. This page does not give a rupee price for it, and it does not give a cause for the commercial rise.

## Dolpa

Six Indian tourists, four men and two women, are stranded by heavy snowfall in Nepal's Dolpa district. Local officials said they were returning from Dho towards Shey Phoksundo Lake. Contact was lost in the Numa La and Baga La pass area, where there is no mobile network. The place named for the evacuation attempt is Danigad, ward 8 of Shey Phoksundo Rural Municipality. India Today quotes municipal chair Dhawa Samduk Gurung on the lack of a mobile network between wards 4 and 8. Dolpa's Chief District Officer, Junu Hamal, told The Kathmandu Post that a Nepal Army helicopter was being dispatched to evacuate them, with the cost to be met from the Disaster Management Fund. A Nepal Army team has been deployed. Officials said locals had warned the group about the snowbound passes. Upper Dolpa has had several days of heavy snowfall, and the administration is urging tourists to stay in safe locations.

This page does not say they have been rescued. It does not say they are safe. Nepali reports describe the rescue as under way. India Today, at 10:27 am IST, said they were not yet traced. Their names and home state are not used here. A suggested link between the snow and Cyclone Arnab is left out.

## Quebec

The government of Quebec, the Parti Québécois government, plans to bar elementary school students from wearing visible religious symbols. Education Minister Joël Arseneau has announced the proposal. The symbols named are turbans, hijabs, kippahs and crosses. No bill has been tabled, and no start date has been announced. The legislature resumes on 17 November. The proposal would mainly affect Sikh, Muslim and Jewish children, and groups from those communities have criticised it, citing religious freedom and equal access to education. This is a plan. It is not law. It is not a ban by Canada.

## Fishermen

In the early hours of Sunday 1 November, about 1 am, the Sri Lankan Navy detained seven fishermen from Rameswaram and Thangachimadam, in Tamil Nadu's Ramanathapuram district, and seized their trawler. Tamil Nadu Fisheries Department officials, in The Hindu, said they were picked up between Katchatheevu and Mannar North and taken to a Sri Lankan naval port for legal proceedings. The Sri Lanka Navy says the trawler was fishing illegally in Sri Lankan waters north of Mannar. That is an allegation. India has not confirmed a violation. A Navy web address uses the figure nine. The article title and the other outlets say seven. This page uses seven. The Hindu also says 398 tokens were issued on 31 October and that boats were due back on Sunday evening. Fishermen's associations have asked the Centre to intervene and are to meet on the next steps. The fishermen are not named here.
