## Bunker Roy

Sanjit Roy, known as Bunker Roy, died on 10 November in Tilonia, Rajasthan, aged 81. He founded Barefoot College, the Social Work and Research Centre, in 1972. The Indian Express, PTI as carried by The Hindu, The Times of India and The Wire report the death. A statement came from the family and from fellow activists. No cause of death is given. The slide is a file photograph, CC BY 2.0, David Shankbone, Wikimedia Commons.

## The jobs survey

The Periodic Labour Force Survey bulletin for July to September 2026 puts the unemployment rate at 5.1 percent. In April to June it was 5.4 percent. The overall rate eased. The rural rate is 4.3 percent. The urban rate is 6.8 percent, a little higher than before. PTI, carried by Business Standard, and DD India report the bulletin. The Ministry of Statistics press note of 10 November is listed. Its PDF was not opened. This page uses the figures as stated, and the word eased for the overall move. The slide is a file photograph, CC BY-SA 4.0, Jim Carter, Wikimedia Commons.

## The poll code

The Election Commission wrote to Goa, Manipur, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand. During the Model Code of Conduct, restrictions that apply to government employees will also apply to retired officials who are re-employed by those state governments. The instruction was made public on 10 November. The Tribune, The Times of India, Hindustan Times and PTI as carried by The Hindu report it. The code is not in force. No poll dates are announced. Hindustan Times dates the letter 9 November. The other timing used here is that it was made public on 10 November. This page does not choose the letter's own date. The slide is a file photograph, CC BY 2.0, Ramesh Lalwani, Wikimedia Commons.

## Haj 2027

India and Saudi Arabia signed the bilateral Haj agreement for 2027 in Jeddah on 9 November, during Minority Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju's visit. India's quota is confirmed at 1,75,025. That is the Press Information Bureau release of the Ministry of Minority Affairs, release ID 2334463, at 8:19 pm IST on 9 November. This page does not say the quota increased, and it does not say it is the highest. The slide is a file photograph, CC BY-SA 4.0, Adli Wahid, Wikimedia Commons.

## Doha pistol

At the ISSF World Shooting Championship in Doha, the Indian women's 25m pistol team won gold. Esha Singh scored 590, Manu Bhaker 587, and Divya Thadigol Subbaraju 584. The team total is 1761, with 68 inner tens. No Indian took a medal in the individual final. Manu Bhaker was joint fourth. Esha Singh was eighth. Those places are the official ISSF results pages. The slide is a file, GODL-India, Rajya Sabha TV, Wikimedia Commons, not this final.

## Abu Dhabi

The first day of US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine ended in Abu Dhabi on 10 November. It is the first such meeting since February. The talks resume on 11 November. No deal is announced. The Associated Press, The Guardian, a Deutsche Welle live page, and Ukrainska Pravda report the day. The slide is a file photograph, CC BY-SA 4.0, comedian44, Wikimedia Commons.

## Qantas

Qantas said that about 850 roles at the Qantas Group are expected to be affected over the coming years under a technology and artificial-intelligence partnership with Accenture. Some of the work will go to Accenture. It will be delivered mostly by Accenture teams in India, and some in Australia. The first phase is by the middle of 2027. The Qantas Group media release of 10 November, 11:48 am Australian Eastern Daylight Time, is carried by a Mirage News syndication. The BBC, Reuters as carried by The Straits Times, and The Guardian also report it. This is not a line that 850 jobs are being sent to India. The slide is a file photograph, CC BY-SA 4.0, Sean Glossop, Wikimedia Commons.

## Naga ancestral remains

The Pitt Rivers Museum at the University of Oxford agreed to return 39 Naga ancestral remains. The transfer was signed with Naga leaders at a ceremony on 9 November. The museum says the remains were collected in the 1910s to the 1940s, during the colonial period. The destination stated is Nagaland. The museum's page is titled "A path home". The BBC and the Mokokchung Times also report the agreement. The remains are not shown. The slide is the exterior of the Oxford University Museum of Natural History, the building through which the Pitt Rivers Museum is entered. CC BY-SA 4.0, Ethan Doyle White, Wikimedia Commons.
