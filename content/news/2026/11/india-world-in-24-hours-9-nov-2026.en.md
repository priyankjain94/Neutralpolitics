## Madhya Pradesh fires

On Diwali night, two separate house fires killed 12 people in Madhya Pradesh. Six died in Barhi, in Katni district. Six died in Chhapara, in Seoni district. Four people were rescued in Seoni. The Times of India, carrying PTI, Navbharat Times, and Dainik Bhaskar English report the toll and the two places. The cause is under investigation. This page does not state a cause, an ignition time, or blame. It does not say the four people rescued in Seoni were injured. No one is named.

## Cancer-drug margins

Revised maximum retail prices on listed anti-cancer medicines take effect on 9 November 2026. This is not a new notification dated today. The Press Information Bureau's 17 October release says the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority notified a trade-margin cap that day, under paragraph 19 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013, for 137 identified non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, including patented drugs. The official factsheet identifies the order as S.O. 5690(E) of 17 October 2026. The cap is 30 percent of the MRP excluding GST. GST is added on top. It is not a flat 30 percent discount, and it is not a 30 percent cut on every product. The 137 figure is a count of listed entries, including combinations. It is not 137 single molecules and not 137 brands.

PIB's release of 7 November puts the estimate of annual consumer savings at Rs 3,502.31 crore. That is an official estimate of the aggregate, not a guarantee of any one bill. The same release says 76 companies had by then reported revised MRPs for 1,225 formulations covering 121 drugs, with reductions reported in 1,031 formulations. Those counts are not the same unit as the 137 listed entries. This page does not mix them. It does not recommend a brand or a substitute.

## Mumbai crackers

The Urban Disaster Management Authority of Brihanmumbai banned the use of rockets and similar aerial crackers from 9 November until 11:59 pm on 25 November 2026. Hindustan Times, India Today, The Indian Express and The Times of India report the order. It applies inside BMC limits, in public and private places, including terraces, balconies, courtyards, parking areas and slums. Other crackers remain under existing law and court orders. This is not a ban on every cracker, and it is not a ban across the whole Mumbai region.

BMC and Mumbai Fire Brigade data, as those four outlets report them, put fire calls on 8 November at 142, of which 137 were linked to firecrackers. This page gives no penalty and no casualty figure.

## Arpita Singh

Painter Arpita Singh died in Delhi on 9 November. She received the Padma Bhushan in 2011. The Press Information Bureau's Padma list for that year names her under art, painting. Her husband, Paramjit Singh, confirmed the death to PTI. The accounts do not agree on her age, or on the exact place of death inside Delhi. This page omits both.

## WMO at COP31

On 9 November, the first day of COP31 in Antalya, the World Meteorological Organization said 2026 is set to be one of the four warmest years on record. Its January-to-September average was about 1.47°C above the 1850–1900 pre-industrial average. That line is not an annual figure. Data run only through September. This page does not say the Paris 1.5°C limit was breached. That limit is a long-term average.

Saulo said, in the WMO news release, that the update "gives new meaning to the phrase off the charts", and that El Niño does not add new heat to the planet but releases heat from the ocean. Those words are the release's. The organisation says the final report comes in March 2027. The same update says El Niño has been intensifying since August and is expected to reach record strength by December, that global mean sea level is at a record in the satellite series since 1993, and that preliminary data through July point to a tenth consecutive year of record ocean heat content. Those sentences stay with WMO.

## Passport request

Lawyers for former Bangladesh prime minister Sheikh Hasina, of Matrix Chambers, wrote on 9 November to the Bangladesh High Commission in New Delhi. They asked for a new passport and a national identity card. Hindustan Times, ThePrint, the Associated Press, and Reuters as carried by The Japan Times report the letter. It is a request. No decision is reported. The package names Dhaka state minister Humaiun Kobir as saying the government insists on extradition. That line is his, as the reports carry it. This page does not say a document was issued.

## INS Surat

INS Surat arrived in Muscat on 8 November for the 15th edition of the India-Oman naval exercise Naseem Al Bahr, Sea Breeze. The Ministry of Defence said so in PIB release PRID 2334344 on 9 November. The release describes harbour and sea phases, with cross-deck visits, operational interaction and sports, and a coordinated patrol with the Royal Navy of Oman to follow. It states no end date. This page does not add one.
