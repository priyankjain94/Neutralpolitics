This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The carousel was posted at 10:57 am IST on 1 November 2026. Around 11:00 am IST the march wording was rechecked and updated. The stamp is VERIFIED. The Opposition INDIA bloc is holding its Chalo Dilli rally at Delhi's Ramlila Maidan on Sunday 1 November. The demands are these. Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar should resign. The Special Intensive Revision of the voter rolls, SIR in the caption, should be rolled back. The bloc alleges that eligible voters were removed from the list. It calls that vote chori. Those lines are the Opposition's allegations. This note labels them as alleged. It does not treat them as established.

Delhi Police say the rally at Ramlila Maidan has permission, with conditions, and that it must end by 5 pm. Traffic restrictions in central Delhi run from 10 am to 5 pm. The roads named are BSZ Marg, JLN Marg, Minto Road, Barakhamba Road and Aruna Asaf Ali Road. The Hindu is the report named for that police advisory.

There was an earlier question about approval for the march. News18 reported that the rally had a nod and that there was no permission for a march from Connaught Place. Hindustan Times reported that the stir at Ramlila ground was permitted and that there was then no word on a Connaught Place march. This note does not turn those earlier lines into a ban. The march was not banned.

The update, from the morning of 1 November, is this. Deputy commissioner of police, Central, Rohit Rajbir told ANI that a police plan for the march route is in place. The noon march runs from the Minto Road and Connaught Place junction to Ramlila Maidan. That morning line supersedes the earlier report of no approval for the Connaught Place march. The recheck around 11:00 am IST is the point at which this wording was fixed for the carousel.

On the other side, the package records three lines, and they stay attributed. On 31 October, at Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said the Opposition was doing politics of obstruction. The Times of India is the report named for that remark. The BJP questioned the choice of 1 November and held a separate march in Hari Nagar. The BBC reports that both the BJP and the Election Commission denied the allegations. That denial is the BBC's. This note does not add a denial from any other outlet. A recheck around 11:00 am IST found no newer Election Commission or BJP statement.

What the revision is, in this package, is also the BBC's account. The caption describes SIR as the Election Commission's fresh check of the voter list. The BBC reports that the Commission says the purpose is to update the list and to remove the names of people who have died or who have shifted. This page attributes that purpose to the BBC. It does not present it as a document read apart from that report.

The Chamber of Trade and Industry asked the INDIA bloc to postpone the rally until after Diwali. That request is in the package. This note does not say whether the rally was postponed. The rally date in the package is 1 November.

The rally follows the symbolic public impeachment of the Chief Election Commissioner on 31 October, which had no legal effect. That item is in [India & World, 31 Oct: 8 stories](/news/2026/10/india-world-in-24-hours-31-oct-2026). An earlier, separate day was 6 October, when a march toward the Election Commission was stopped. [Police stop the INDIA bloc march to the Election Commission; Rahul and Priyanka Gandhi briefly detained](/news/2026/10/india-bloc-march-ec-stopped-rahul-priyanka-detained). That page is not this rally. This note does not report a detention, a clash, or an outcome for 1 November. The recheck around 11:00 am IST found none. It also does not give a crowd number.
