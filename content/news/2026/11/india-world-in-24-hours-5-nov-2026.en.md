## Chawri Bazar

Afternoon reports put the toll from the under-construction building collapse in Chawri Bazar, Old Delhi, at 2 dead and 4 injured. The place in those reports is the Gali Sattara and Sheesh Mahal area. The morning note on this site, [under-construction building collapses near Chawri Bazaar](/news/2026/11/delhi-chawri-bazaar-under-construction-building-collapse-at-least-2-dead-rescue-ongoing), used the line then available: at least 2 dead and 1 injured, with rescue still going, and with the lane also reported as Gali Sitara. This page uses the later toll. It does not add a further number, and it names no one who died or was hurt. Reports also spell the market Chawri Bazaar.

The Municipal Corporation of Delhi suspended Assistant Engineer (Building) Mohd Naeem and Junior Engineer Aman Dalal. The Times of India and News18 report the suspension as being for negligence. That is the ground those reports give for the suspension. It is not a court finding on this page. Chief Minister Rekha Gupta ordered an inquiry. The Tribune and News18 report that. An FIR has been registered against the contractor or the builder. An FIR is not a conviction. This page does not name a builder, and it does not call anyone guilty.

News18 reports that the structure had no sanctioned building plan. Hindustan Times reports that Delhi Police had informed the MCD of unauthorised construction months before the collapse. That line stays with Hindustan Times. The slide is a 2006 file of Chawri Bazar, Wasted Time R / Wikimedia Commons, public domain, not this collapse.

## The detention clock

On 5 November a bench of Justices Ujjal Bhuyan and Atul S. Chandurkar held that the Article 22(2) deadline of 24 hours, to produce a person before a magistrate, runs from detention, when liberty is curtailed, and not from the paperwork of a formal arrest. The Hindu, LiveLaw and PTI report that holding. The accounts say an artificial distinction between detention and arrest is constitutionally unsustainable under Article 22(2) and Section 56 of the BNSS. The only exception the reports name is the time needed to travel from the place of arrest to the magistrate. That exception is The Hindu's. Inquiry time cannot be excluded. Investigation time before the formal arrest cannot be excluded. A magistrate must record the exact date and time of detention. This page does not add any other exception.

LiveLaw's account of the case says four NDPS accused were detained at about 2 pm on 29 September 2025 and shown arrested at about 11 am on 30 September, a gap the reports call about 18 hours. The Court ordered their immediate release for the Article 22(2) breach. Views that left out pre-arrest detention were held incorrect: Orissa in Aryaman Pattanaik, Kerala in Arun K Thomas, and Madras in Kannan. Kerala's view in Biswajith Mandal was approved. The Hindu's account says fundamental rights take precedence over special-law bail restrictions, in the UAPA, PMLA and NDPS context, when a production timeline is breached. Formal arrest is not abolished. Undertrials are not released as a class. Those bail bars are not struck down. The fuller note is [the 24-hour clock starts at detention](/news/2026/11/supreme-court-24-hour-production-deadline-starts-from-detention-not-formal-arrest-article-22). The slide is a 2019 file of the Supreme Court, Subhashish Panigrahi / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, not this hearing. No court video is used.

## Sensex and Nifty

Indian equity benchmarks rose on Thursday for a sixth straight session. The BSE Sensex closed at 79,310.01, up 2,008.61 points, or 2.60 percent. India Today and PTI carry that close. PTI's headline rounds the move to 2,009 points. The NSE Nifty closed at 24,536.60, up about 576 points, or 2.40 percent. India Today and Business Standard carry that close. This page reports those closes. It is not investment advice. It does not call either close a record.

Reports attribute the rally to lower crude oil prices after the US midterm elections, foreign investor buying, and a positive global mood. Business Standard is among the outlets that carry those drivers. India Today reports that Bajaj Finance was among the top gainers, up about 8 percent. ## Tanvi Patri

Fifteen-year-old Tanvi Patri, from Odisha, became the first Indian woman to reach a badminton final at the Youth Olympic Games. On Wednesday in Dakar she beat Japan's second seed, Yuzuno Watanabe, 21-14, 11-21, 21-19. The semi-final lasted 54 minutes. The win assures at least a silver in girls' singles. She meets China's Yi Xin Zhang in the final on 6 November, at about 2 pm IST. She has not won gold. The final has not been played.

India Today, Open, Times Now and the Olympics.com information service carry the result. Before her, H. S. Prannoy in 2010 and Lakshya Sen in 2018 won Youth Olympic badminton silver for India in the boys' singles. That is the reports' context. This page does not place the Games in Buenos Aires. It uses Dakar. The slide is a 2016 file of a shuttlecock, annca / Wikimedia Commons, CC0, not this match.

## Special Summary Revision

On Thursday the Election Commission announced a Special Summary Revision of electoral rolls in six States and Union Territories: Arunachal Pradesh, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Mizoram, Odisha and Sikkim. Draft rolls are to be published on 30 November 2026. Claims and objections run until 30 December 2026. Final rolls are on 21 January 2027. The Election Commission press note, The Times of India and PTI are the sources for that calendar.

This is a Special Summary Revision, SSR. It is not the Special Intensive Revision, SIR. The Times of India and PTI draw that distinction. The exercise, as the reports describe it, is aimed at enrolling young and first-time voters. The Times of India reports that advance applications are allowed for people who turn 18 on 1 April, 1 July or 1 October 2027. Chief secretaries have been asked to provide staff and resources. This page does not say any name will be deleted. It does not give a voter count for these six places. The slide is a 2009 file from the Election Commission office, Election Commission of India / Wikimedia Commons, GODL-India, not this revision.

## Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, announced on Thursday, in an Instagram video, that he is retiring from international football. BBC Sport and the Associated Press report the announcement. His Portugal career began in August 2003, the BBC reports. The reports give him 234 caps and 146 goals, and call both men's international records. He said he would hand back the number 7 shirt. ## Swift

NASA's Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory re-entered over North Sumatra, Indonesia, on 4 November at 6:09 pm IST, which is 7:39 am EST. NASA and the US Space Force confirmed the re-entry, the reports say. Spaceflight Now places it near 3.4 degrees north and 98.6 degrees east. The observatory was launched on 20 November 2004 for a planned mission of two years. It ran for nearly 22 years and detected more than 2,000 gamma-ray bursts. NASA, the Associated Press and Live Science carry that record.

Higher solar activity increased atmospheric drag, NASA and the Associated Press report. NASA had contracted Katalyst, for about 30 million dollars, to try a robotic boost of the orbit. The capture attempt was called off. Spaceflight Now and the Associated Press report that. The observatory decayed and re-entered. This page does not say it failed or broke. No damage is reported. No injuries are reported. This page does not say debris hit anyone or any property. The slide is a 2025 NASA illustration of the spacecraft, public domain, not a picture of the re-entry.

## Abu Dhabi

Ukraine, the United States and Russia are to hold trilateral working-level talks in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November, with the United States mediating. Reuters and Ukrinform report the plan. US envoy Steve Witkoff is the account named for the mediation. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on 4 November that he held a call of about 90 minutes with Witkoff and Jared Kushner, to prepare for the meeting. Ukrinform and Ukrainska Pravda report that call. He said the discussion covered post-war security guarantees, air defence including Patriot interceptors, reconstruction, and cooperation he called Drone Deals. Those topics are his account, as Ukrinform carries it. Ukrinform also reports Brussels meetings, on reconstruction on 4 November and on security guarantees on 5 November, with European and NATO representatives.

No peace deal is reported. No ceasefire is reported. The talks are at team level. This page does not say President Zelenskyy or President Putin will attend. The slide is an 18 August 2025 White House file of Zelenskyy with Witkoff and Secretary of State Marco Rubio, public domain, not this call.
