This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is VERIFIED. The carousel was posted at 10:50 am IST on 6 November 2026. The cover is a Neutral Politics graphic. It is typographic. No photograph is used. It is shown whole.

Fifteen-year-old Indian shuttler Tanvi Patri won silver in girls' singles badminton at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. She lost the gold-medal final to China's Zhang Yi Xin. The Olympic Information Service, The Indian Express, Hindustan Times, NDTV Sports and Xinhua report the silver. The reports place the final at Dakar Arena. Gold in that final is Zhang Yi Xin's. Silver is Patri's. This page does not say she won the final, and it does not say she won gold.

The Indian Express and NDTV describe her as the first Indian woman to win a Youth Olympics badminton singles medal. The package for this note also ties that description to the Olympic Information Service, and to comments from the Badminton Association of India carried by The Indian Express. Those words stay with those reports. This page does not add a record of its own beyond that description.

This is a Youth Olympic Games result. It is not a medal from the senior Olympic Games. The caption on the carousel says the same limit. Youth Olympics, not the senior Games.

The [5 November roundup](/news/2026/11/india-world-in-24-hours-5-nov-2026) recorded that she had reached the girls' singles final, including that she had beaten Japan's Yuzuno Watanabe in the semi-final. This page is the result of that final. It does not repeat a score from the semi-final.

A score, a game count and a duration are not used here. The accounts do not agree on those details, so this page does not choose one. It does not narrate the games. It does not name a next event. It does not list earlier boys' medallists.
