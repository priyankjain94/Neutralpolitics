This note is verified. The series is NP EXPLAINS. The stamp is VERIFIED. The carousel was posted at 8:52 am IST on 6 November 2026. The cover is an official still, Photo Id 219577, credited Courtesy: DRDO/PIB. It is shown whole. The other slides in the carousel are typographic.

The Defence Research and Development Organisation conducted a successful flight-test of the indigenously developed quasi-ballistic missile Pralay from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, off the Odisha coast, at about 11:10 am IST on 5 November 2026. That time and place are the account in Press Information Bureau release PRID 2333276. The Hindu independently reported the same core facts. This page uses 5 November. It does not use 3 November.

The PIB release says the flight was part of user evaluation trials. All stipulated objectives were fully met. That conclusion, the release says, was confirmed by data from range instruments deployed by the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur. Those sentences are the release's.

Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad, developed the missile along with other DRDO laboratories. Bharat Dynamics Limited produced the system. The release describes Bharat Dynamics Limited as a Development-cum-Production Partner. Senior representatives of DRDO, of industry, and of the Defence Forces witnessed the trial. That witness line is in the release.

The release says that with this successful test, induction of the system has become imminent. Imminent is the release's word. This page does not say Pralay has been inducted. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and DRDO Chairman Rajesh Kumar Singh congratulated the teams. That congratulation is in the same account.

The Times of India and The Tribune, in the brief read with this package, describe Pralay as a solid-propellant, surface-to-surface missile that can be fired from a mobile launcher. They put a reported strike range at 150 to 500 km, depending on payload. That figure is reported capability. This page does not state a range flown in this test. It does not state a number of targets. It does not state an evacuation number.

This page does not frame the test against any country. It does not add a threat line, and it does not add a war line.

![Official still of the Pralay flight-test. Courtesy: DRDO/PIB. Shown whole.](/media/2026/11/drdo-pralay-flight-test-user-evaluation-trials-odisha.webp)

Read together, the reported lines are these. At about 11:10 am IST on 5 November 2026, DRDO flight-tested the indigenous quasi-ballistic missile Pralay from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island off Odisha. PIB release PRID 2333276 says it was a user evaluation trial and that all stipulated objectives were met, on data from the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur. Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad, developed it with other DRDO laboratories. Bharat Dynamics Limited, the Development-cum-Production Partner, produced it. Senior DRDO, industry and Defence Forces representatives witnessed the trial. The release says induction has become imminent. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and DRDO Chairman Rajesh Kumar Singh congratulated the teams. The Hindu reported the same core facts. A reported capability of 150 to 500 km, depending on payload, is in The Times of India and The Tribune, with a mobile launcher. That is not a range for this flight. The system is not described here as inducted.

The sources for the core facts are PIB release PRID 2333276 and The Hindu. The capability lines are The Times of India and The Tribune.
