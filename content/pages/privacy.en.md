This notice covers neutralpolitics pages, contributor uploads, newsletter signups, and messages sent through the forms. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. It is a working text for this first version and should be read by a lawyer before the site is opened to the public.

## What we collect

If you send a video, we ask for your name, Indian mobile number, email, city, state, a description of what you saw, and the file. If you join the list, we store an email or a WhatsApp number, the language you pick, and the topics you tick. If you report an error or send a claim, we store the message and any email you add. We store a hash of the IP address, not the raw address.

## Why

Contributor details are used to verify a video and to reply. They are not published unless you asked to be credited by name. Signup details are stored so a newsletter or a WhatsApp alert can be sent later, after that sending is approved. No message is sent in this version. Reports are used to correct or check a story.

## How long

A rejected video and the personal details with it are deleted after 30 days. Details attached to a video we publish are kept for one year and then cut down. A signup stays until you unsubscribe. You can ask for deletion. Taking a video down is straightforward when it has not been published. A story already published may stay, with the file removed where that is feasible.

## Who can see it

The database is locked so the public cannot read these rows. Only the desk, through the password-protected admin page, can review them. We do not sell this information.
