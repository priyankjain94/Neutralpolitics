---
title: "Ownership and funding"
description: "Who publishes Neutral Politics, and what the site says about ads, data and mailboxes."
---

Neutral Politics publishes this website and the Instagram account [@theneutralpolitics](https://www.instagram.com/theneutralpolitics/).

The site does not name an individual owner or editor. The About page says those names can be added later. No street address is published.

This version of the site does not run ads. It does not sell reader data. No funder is named on the site.

The contact page prints desk@theneutralpolitics.in, corrections@theneutralpolitics.in and contribute@theneutralpolitics.in. Those mailboxes are listed for when domain mail is connected. Until then, the working route is a direct message on Instagram. The corrections address is still a placeholder.
