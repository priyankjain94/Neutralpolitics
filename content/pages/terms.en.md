title: "Terms of use"
description: "The rules for reading this site."

The pages are published by Neutral Politics for general information. They are not legal, financial or medical advice. Sample articles are labelled and are not reports of real events.

You may link to any public page. You may not copy a story onto another site and present it as your own. Photos keep the credit printed under them.

The contributor terms, for people who send video, are on a separate page. Those terms are the ones that apply to a submission.

The desk can refuse a submission, correct a story, or remove a page. Indian law governs these terms.
